# Recruiting creators

## Where to find them

- TikTok/Instagram search: niche hashtags (#homeschool, #homeschoolmom,
  #homeschooltok, #homeschoolcurriculum)
- Creator marketplaces (Collabstr, Aspire, Grin)
- Competitor comment sections: who's already talking about the problem?
- Homeschool Facebook groups and Reddit (r/homeschool)
- Referrals from existing creators (best source at scale)

## 4-check vetting filter (in order)

Fail one = skip the rest. Don't waste time on maybes.

### 1. Portfolio fluency
Does their content feel natural? If it looks like a commercial, red flag.
For Homa: look for confessional/storytelling style, not polished product
demos. Mom creators who talk about real struggles > creators who do
scripted app walkthroughs.

### 2. Engagement consistency
Consistent engagement = skill. Random viral spikes = luck. Check the last
20 posts, not just the best one. Look for steady 3-5% engagement, not one
10% outlier.

### 3. Communication quality
Slow/unclear comms now = 10x worse during a campaign. If they take 3 days
to reply to the initial outreach, they'll ghost during production.

### 4. Product understanding
Can they explain the app in one sentence after a brief description? If
they can't reframe it naturally, they'll produce generic content.

For Homa: "It helps moms find where their kid actually is before picking
curriculum". If they say something like this unprompted, they get it.

## Follower count is irrelevant

The top single video (15.6M views, 1.4M likes, 9.17% engagement) came
from a creator with a modest following. Recruit for authenticity and
storytelling ability, not audience size.

## Outreach template

```
Hey [name]! I love your content about [specific video/topic]. We're
building Homa. It helps homeschool moms figure out exactly where their
kid is before choosing the next lesson. We're looking for moms who can
share their real experience with it. Would you be open to a quick 20-min
call to see if it's a fit? No pressure either way.
```

Keep it short, specific (reference their actual content), and low-pressure.
