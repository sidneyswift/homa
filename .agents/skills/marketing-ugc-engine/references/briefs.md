# Creative briefs

## The one-page brief (5 required components)

If one is missing, the creator will guess wrong. Every time.

1. **Campaign objective** (one sentence): "After watching this video, the
   viewer should believe ___."
2. **Key product messaging** (2-4 points max): Benefits, not features. For
   Homa: "Find the real level before choosing the next lesson," "Stop
   guessing, start knowing," "One worksheet can reveal the missing skill."
3. **Target audience insights**: Age, interests, behaviors, apps they already
   use. For Homa: homeschool moms 28-45, anxious about gaps, active on
   TikTok/Instagram, comparing curricula.
4. **Content requirements**: Platform, length, aspect ratio, branding moments.
5. **Creative inspiration**: 3-5 links to high-performing reference videos.

## The golden rule: guide, don't script

When we started, briefs were 3-4 pages. The content was polished. It also
performed terribly.

Now briefs fit on one page:
- What the app does (2 sentences)
- Who it's for (1 sentence)
- 2-4 features that matter (bullets)
- 3-5 reference videos
- A general script for guidance

That's it.

## What NOT to do in a brief

- Don't leave compensation ambiguous (creates friction)
- Don't skip usage rights (needed for ads later)
- Don't use corporate language (creators copy your tone)
- Don't list every feature (pick 2-4 that matter)

## Homa brief template

```markdown
# [Campaign Name]: Creator Brief

## Objective
After watching this video, the viewer should believe ___.

## What is Homa? (2 sentences)
Homa helps homeschool moms find their child's real learning level: the
actual skill gaps, not just the grade on the box. One quick assessment
shows exactly where to start and what comes next.

## Who watches this
Homeschool moms (28-45) who feel overwhelmed choosing curriculum, worry
about invisible gaps, and want confidence that they're teaching at the
right level. Active on [platform].

## Key messages (pick 2-3 per video)
- [ ] The grade on the box isn't always the right level
- [ ] One worksheet can reveal the skill you've been skipping
- [ ] Stop guessing which curriculum. Find the level first
- [ ] [Custom message for this campaign]

## Content specs
- Platform: [TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts]
- Length: [15-60 seconds]
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Must include: on-screen hook text in first 0.5s
- Must NOT include: children's faces, names, or voices

## Reference videos
1. [link] · why this works: [one line]
2. [link] · why this works: [one line]
3. [link] · why this works: [one line]

## Compensation
[rate] per video, paid [weekly/biweekly] via [method].
Bonus: [structure if applicable].
Usage rights: perpetual, all platforms.
```
