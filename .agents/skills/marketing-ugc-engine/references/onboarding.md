# Onboarding creators

## The 20-minute call (non-negotiable)

Every creator. No exceptions. Even at 590 creators, every single one gets
a call before posting anything.

### 6-step call structure

1. **Introduction** (3-5 min): Ask about them. What do they create? Why
   homeschooling? Spot red flags (vague answers, no real experience,
   purely transactional energy).

2. **App overview** (3-5 min): Explain Homa in 2 sentences. Then ask them
   to explain it back. If they can't reframe it naturally, they'll
   produce generic content. For Homa: "It helps moms find their child's
   real learning level before choosing curriculum. One quick check shows
   the gaps and what comes next."

3. **Campaign terms** (5 min): Videos/week, content types, timeline. Be
   specific. "We're looking for 2 videos per week for 4 weeks, mostly
   confessional/discovery format."

4. **Accounts and warm-up** (3 min): Verify their accounts. Explain
   warm-up: post 3-5 niche-relevant videos before mentioning Homa. This
   trains the algorithm to classify the account correctly. For Homa:
   homeschool tips, curriculum opinions, mom life, then naturally
   introduce the app.

5. **Compensation** (5-7 min): Present the rate. It's not negotiated. Be
   transparent about bonuses and payment schedule.

6. **Next steps** (2 min): Collect info (email, payment details, handles).
   Send onboarding pack. Set first draft due date.

## The first 15 minutes after the call

1. Register in master roster: name, handle, platform, country, rate, date,
   Tier B (everyone starts here).
2. Create private communication channel (iMessage/WhatsApp/DM group):
   [Creator Name] x Homa.
3. Add to general creator community chat for accountability and culture.

## The 5-item onboarding pack

Send immediately after the call:

1. **The brief** (one page, from `references/briefs.md`)
2. **Reference videos** (3-5 examples of what great looks like)
3. **Brand guidelines** (Homa voice: reassure before inform, no AI-first
   framing, no child faces/names)
4. **Posting schedule** (which days, what times)
5. **Payment details** (rate, schedule, bonus structure, payment method)

## Account warm-up protocol

Post 3-5 niche-relevant videos before mentioning Homa:
- Homeschool tips and opinions
- "What I wish I knew when I started homeschooling"
- Curriculum comparisons or honest reviews
- Day-in-the-life (no children visible)

Purpose: trains the algorithm to classify the account in the homeschool
niche. First Homa mention lands in the right audience, not a cold start.
