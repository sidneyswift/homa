# Scaling and iteration

## Creator tiering (review monthly)

| Tier | Profile | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **S (Elite)** | Consistently viral, fully autonomous, gets the voice without notes | Highest rates, biggest bonuses, first access to new campaigns, minimal QA |
| **A (Top)** | Consistent winners, strong hook game, reliable output | Higher rates, bonuses, trusted with harder formats |
| **B (Reliable)** | Steady output, acceptable quality, room to grow | Standard rates, give them S/A format examples to stretch |
| **C (Underperform)** | Below threshold after feedback cycle | One more feedback round + specific coaching. No improvement = phase out gracefully |

Everyone starts at Tier B. Tier up/down based on rolling 4-week performance.

### Promotion triggers
- B → A: 3+ videos scoring 18+ (out of 25) in a month, zero revision requests
- A → S: Consistent 20+ scores, at least one breakout (>100K views or top engagement), needs zero feedback
- Any → C: 2+ revision requests in a week, or average score below 15

### Phase-out protocol (Tier C)
Don't ghost or shame. "Hey, we're pausing the campaign with you for now.
Really appreciate the work you put in. If we spin up something that's a
better fit, we'll reach out." Pay everything owed immediately.

## Compensation structure

### Base rates (industry ranges, adapt to budget)
- Micro creators (new/small): $15-30 per video
- Established (proven viral history): $300-1,500/month retainer

### The bonus system
Turns compensation from a transaction into a game:
- Views milestone bonus (e.g., $50 at 100K views, $100 at 500K)
- "Video of the week" bonus ($25-50)
- Referral bonus for bringing in quality creators
- Tier-up bonus (moving from B → A gets a rate increase)

### The pay-on-time rule
Pay on the date you promised. Not a day later. Elite creators have been
lost because a payment was 5 days late. Set calendar reminders. Automate
if possible.

## The weekly iteration loop (every Friday)

1. **Pull top 5 and bottom 5 videos** of the week (by views, engagement,
   or conversion — pick the metric that matters for the campaign phase).
2. **Identify patterns in top performers:** What hook type? What format?
   What time posted? What creator tier?
3. **Identify what failed in bottom performers:** Weak hook? Wrong format?
   Bad timing? Creator mismatch?
4. **Update next week's format slots:** Double down on what worked, cut
   what didn't.
5. **Share findings in creator community channel:** Top performers get
   recognized, everyone learns.

### Friday report format

```markdown
# Week of [date] — Campaign [name]

## Top 5
| Video | Creator | Views | Eng% | Why it worked |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Bottom 5
| Video | Creator | Views | Eng% | Why it failed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Patterns
- [what worked]
- [what didn't]

## Next week changes
- Format slots: [adjustments]
- Creator focus: [who to push, who to coach]
- Brief updates: [any changes to the brief]
```

## Benchmark targets (2026)

| Metric | TikTok | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (1s) | >60% | >50% |
| View-through (3s) | >40% | >35% |
| Engagement | >5% | >3% |
| Organic CPV | $0.005-0.02 | $0.01-0.03 |

## Scaling checklist (before adding more creators)

- [ ] Current creators are at 80%+ approval rate
- [ ] QA process takes <1 hour per day
- [ ] Brief is proven (format has at least 3 hits)
- [ ] Payment process is automated or systematized
- [ ] Communication channels are manageable
- [ ] Weekly iteration loop is running consistently
