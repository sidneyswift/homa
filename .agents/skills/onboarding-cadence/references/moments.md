# The moments catalog

Observed delight moments, quirks, and mechanics from the 2026-08-18
Mobbin marathon (60+ apps: 13 at flow depth, ~50 at moment level).
Each entry: what the app does, why it works, and a Homa translation
worth considering. These are ingredients, not requirements; sprinkle,
never pour. Every translation is subject to the voice laws (reassure
before inform, offers never commands, no verdicts on a child) and the
Quiet authority design contract.

## Commitment rituals (the body remembers what the thumb did)

- **Life Reset, the vow** (mobbin.com/screens/1fad6a34-7396-4741-aa3a-79b8002d651a):
  "Read the Vow" then hold a fingerprint mark to swear it. A physical
  gesture converts a tap into a promise.
- **Superpower, sign your name** (mobbin.com/screens/31784106-9d86-4272-a294-fb10e6758136):
  a signature pad: "Commit to your health by signing your name."
- **Mindvalley, the contract** (mobbin.com/screens/5df3334f-6460-4179-8b0a-8f8a62088778):
  "I, commit to:" with two concrete clauses and a stat ("people who
  make a commitment are 10x more likely...").
- **Duolingo**: the daily-goal button says "I'M COMMITTED", not
  "Continue".
- Homa translation: at plan finalize (not onboarding's door), an
  invited pledge in her words, never a demand: a press-and-hold
  "We're doing this" beat when she starts her first week. Skippable,
  and it must never read as Homa extracting a promise for herself.

## Loading theater (the wait is a stage)

- **Lemonade, the joke step** : "ANALYZING YOUR DATA..." ticks real
  steps, then "Feeding Fluffy". One wink inside honest work
  (mobbin.com/flows/ed00a1b5-ae7d-4678-bab9-3cce82b3eeda).
- **Moonly, testimonial inside the wait**
  (mobbin.com/screens/c2a4d157-d2af-4a72-90a2-8ab7e77c4a1c): progress
  bars per sub-task plus a five-star quote while you wait.
- **Polarsteps, your data orbits**
  (mobbin.com/screens/b94a18a6-3947-4af8-81d2-86c8e650a0a6): the
  user's own profile photo with themed icons orbiting it, plus an
  honest duration expectation ("can take a couple of minutes").
- **Life Reset, laurels** (mobbin.com/screens/96ac6812-b174-4dc5-8a8f-e23364e36989):
  progress bar + step checkmarks + 1M+ social proof while building.
- **Anti-pattern, Me+** (mobbin.com/screens/9a4176f1-411a-40e3-8c55-da3717676279):
  "Analyzing your risk of depression, 20%". Fake precision on a heavy
  subject reads as creepy, not caring.
- Homa translation: the worksheet building ticker names true steps
  per child ("Tracing the letter S, Nora-sized") and may carry exactly
  one wink step ("Sharpening the good crayons"). Steps must be true;
  models-judge-code-verifies honesty applies to theater.

## Celebrations (two seconds, then move)

- **Duolingo ABC, the cast celebrates with you**
  (mobbin.com/screens/38019d11-7dfa-424c-aea8-651ad6acfeaa): a
  character jumps with joy amid emoji. The celebration has a face.
- **Speak, materials rain** (mobbin.com/screens/49d467f5-28f5-48fb-ac77-ec42caedd0e2):
  "Lesson Complete!" with letters of the subject matter raining
  around the mascot. Confetti made of the thing you did.
- **Mindvalley, the collectible**
  (mobbin.com/screens/25a769b9-2417-4369-a5c1-f6965ca9c027): "Day One"
  as a dated iridescent medal. The celebration is a kept object.
- **Monzo, celebration with a next step**
  (mobbin.com/screens/f27bc928-e996-4df8-8465-9979e851e4ee): "You've
  done it" confetti plus "Add money" as the very next button.
- Weak form: bare confetti with generic copy ("You are all set!").
  Confetti without specifics is wrapping paper on an empty box.
- Homa translation: print success rains tiny letter S's, not generic
  confetti. Onboarding completion mints the family's first page-of-
  firsts entry (ties into the celebration system: a receipt, never a
  verdict). Every celebration names the thing that happened.

## Permission choreography (sell the message, not the channel)

- **Strava** (mobbin.com/screens/c43f3937-d7e3-4af8-9a56-1bb85e63a06d)
  and **ChatGPT** (mobbin.com/screens/6ca31203-996b-46c7-abbc-ebc8a7486035):
  the pre-prompt shows the actual notification you would receive,
  rendered as a real push bubble ("Way to go 👏 Kudos for another
  activity in the books!").
- **Fixtured** (mobbin.com/screens/fac12af3-b7ad-4ea7-a2ef-ab09e53a3d37):
  three concrete example notifications with real dates and content.
- **Liven, the button is the benefit**
  (mobbin.com/screens/89046b2a-c06f-43cb-b0ae-738cffa0f3bb): the CTA
  reads "Remind me to practice", not "Enable notifications".
- **BeReal, scarcity honesty** (mobbin.com/screens/ea01a85a-7c11-4128-97c5-dbc5d20e03f5):
  "All notifications on the app are silent except the one that will
  indicate you when to post once a day."
- Homa translation: the notifications ask (in context, never at
  launch) previews a real morning brief with the family's own kid
  names, and the button says what she gets ("Send me Nora's morning
  note"). If Homa promises few notifications, the promise is kept.

## Asks that give while asking

- **Duolingo ABC, the why-line**: every question carries its reason
  ("We'll personalize the experience for this age").
- **Fiverr, reassurance inside the ask**
  (mobbin.com/screens/f052d523-3364-4a0a-a51f-19d832b87cfd): "What
  type of freelancer are you? Whatever your style, we'll make it
  work", with hand-drawn illustrated options.
- **Lemonade, pre-framing the sensitive ask**: before health
  questions about the pet, "Don't worry, this won't affect your price
  or eligibility. It's just to help you understand what will and won't
  be covered."
- **Recommended chips**: Duolingo ("RECOMMENDED" on start-from-
  scratch), Cleo AI challenge lengths ("14 days · Getting hot in
  here · Recommended"), Headspace. The app wears its opinion openly
  inside its own question.
- **Oura, life-context options**: "I have small kids", "I live a
  hectic life". The quiz asks about the person, not the settings.
- Homa translation: why-lines under every kid question, pre-framing
  before anything that could feel evaluative ("this is not a test of
  Nora, it's a map for me"), and a Recommended chip wherever Homa has
  an opinion. She decides and Homa suggests.

## Payoff artifacts that echo the user

- **Lemonade, the charmed echo**: the final quote page describes the
  pet in the user's own facts with editorial warmth: "A ridiculously
  cute, 3-year-old Shiba Inu from New York."
- **Headspace, shows its work**: "For Calm Explorers like you", "Why
  this recommendation" with the user's own quiz factors.
- **Breathwrk, goals as chips**: "Your custom plan is ready" over the
  user's own goal chips.
- **Mindvalley, numbers of the plan**: "4 Programs, 4 Lessons,
  4 Meditations", the artifact quantified.
- Homa translation: the worksheet reveal subtitle is the charmed
  echo ("For a ridiculously curious 4-year-old who loves Paw
  Patrol"), and the week plan opens with "built from what you told
  me" plus the actual answers.

## Journey previews and first-action coaching

- **Life Reset, Habit Academy path**
  (mobbin.com/screens/0d8849b9-6253-472b-9067-2b5f40c648b8): the
  road ahead as a milestone path.
- **Starbucks, the status ladder**
  (mobbin.com/screens/3116dd65-38f2-4ccb-9607-778aa6537adf): Green,
  Gold, Reserve shown at welcome.
- **Deepstash, first-action toast**
  (mobbin.com/screens/174ebd2a-93a0-47fc-9320-e8f758964cc6): "Your
  First Save!" celebrated and explained the moment it happens.
- **Oura, the mindset interstitial**
  (mobbin.com/screens/81a7fdc0-873c-4ed9-82ce-428a5c3ef8b9): mid-
  setup, a screen that only reassures ("Try not to feel pressured to
  achieve a pre-set ideal... every mother walks her own path").
  Reassure-before-inform as a whole screen.
- Homa translation: after the worksheet, one glimpse of the road
  (worksheets today, the week plan next, the binder growing). First
  time she does anything in the app proper, a one-line first-action
  toast. The Oura-style reassurance screen fits perfectly before the
  first progress conversation.

## The reciprocity and possession moves (from the core corpus)

- **Finch**: possession before any ask ("You hatched a birb!"), and
  the character asks for your name only after you named it.
- **Notion**: interest picks become real workspace pages.
- **Duolingo ABC**: the child gets a companion character during setup.
- Homa translation: the assembling child card is the possession; if a
  companion moment is ever wanted, it belongs to the child's card,
  chosen by the kid at the end of onboarding, not demanded.
