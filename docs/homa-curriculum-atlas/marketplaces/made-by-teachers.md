# Made By Teachers

## Snapshot

Made By Teachers is a small per-item marketplace for teacher-made
printables: a TPT clone in structure (individual seller shops, $3-10
resources, free items as top-of-funnel, bundles, seasonal categories)
without TPT's scale or brand. Buyers browse by grade (PreK-12), subject,
and holiday; sellers must be in the US, Canada, or Australia, upload
original resources (PDF, Office formats, images, zip), and get paid
monthly by PayPal once earnings pass $50. The platform remits marketplace
sales tax itself and sells paid front-page placement by grade level
("Promote my products"). Basic and premium seller accounts differ mainly
in upload file-size limits (90 MB vs about 200 MB). It is
classroom-first; homeschool families encounter it mostly through search
results for worksheet topics rather than as a destination.

## Reputation and rankings

- No homeschool review presence: no Cathy Duffy entry, no award record,
  minimal community discussion. Trust-checking sites (Scamadviser, Scam
  Detector) rate the site legitimate with an established domain and
  meaningful traffic, with scattered reports of download hiccups.
- Seller-side commission is not published on the site's public FAQ. One
  seller-blog source states 20% platform commission for basic sellers;
  treat that number as single-source and unverified.
- The interesting fact is that it survives at all: it competes with TPT
  on identical mechanics with none of TPT's network effects, sustained
  by SEO long-tail traffic and sellers cross-listing the same catalogs
  they sell on TPT and Etsy. Trajectory: stable niche remainder, not a
  rising player.
- For Homa: no direct competitive weight on its own; it matters as
  evidence that the per-item printable model has a commodity tail of
  interchangeable venues where sellers multi-home. The mom's problem
  (search cost, no quality signal beyond stars) is identical on all of
  them. See `../comparisons/marketplaces.md`.
