# Classful

## Snapshot

Classful is an education social-commerce site that bolts a resource
marketplace onto fundraising tools: digital resources and physical
products, self-paced video courses, teacher wishlists, donation jars,
and school fundraisers in one place. Its homepage names homeschoolers
explicitly in its audience list alongside teachers, schools, PTOs,
nonprofits, and parents. The marketplace hook is price: no listing or
subscription fee, a 5% seller fee plus 2.9% and $0.30 Stripe processing
on digital resources (20% plus processing on courses), which makes it
the cheapest commission structure in the printable-marketplace field,
far under TPT's 20-45% and modestly under Etsy's roughly 11-14%
effective take. Sellers must be US-based; payouts flow through Stripe,
and the platform collects and remits US sales tax. It claims tens of
thousands of educators; independent verification of scale does not
exist.

## Reputation and rankings

- No homeschool review presence, no award record, and thin independent
  coverage of any kind; almost everything knowable comes from the
  company's own pages, so scale and traction claims stay unverified.
- The fundraising side (donation jars, classroom wishlists) is the
  actual identity; the marketplace reads as an attached monetization
  surface rather than a destination buyers seek out.
- Like Made By Teachers, its real function in the ecosystem is as a
  multi-homing venue: sellers list existing TPT and Etsy catalogs here
  because listing is free and the commission is low, accepting near-zero
  organic discovery in exchange.
- For Homa: evidence for the comparison doc's commodity-tail point, and
  a data point that racing commission to the bottom does not create
  buyer demand; discovery and trust do. See
  `../comparisons/marketplaces.md`.
