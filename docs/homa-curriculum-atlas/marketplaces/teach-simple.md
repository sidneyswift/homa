# Teach Simple

## Snapshot

Teach Simple is a small subscription marketplace for teaching resources:
one flat monthly fee buys unlimited downloads of the whole library
(worksheets, lesson plans, ebooks, games, PK-12), positioned explicitly
as the anti-TPT. Founded in 2021 and led by Cole Vineyard, a marketing
executive rather than an educator, it is a Netflix-style answer to the
per-item marketplaces: its school-accounts page claims 50,000+ resources,
several orders of magnitude below TPT's millions. Every submitted product
passes a manual quality review before listing (contributors with 10 clean
approvals get 24-48 hour express review), and contributors are paid 50%
of net revenue through a usage-based "subscriber share" points model
rather than per-sale royalties, with PayPal payouts from a $50 minimum.
It markets to homeschoolers alongside classroom teachers, and its content
is standards-focused (Common Core US, plus CA, UK, AU curricula).

## Reputation and rankings

- No homeschool-press presence: no Cathy Duffy review, no award record,
  and near-zero mention in homeschool communities. Its visibility comes
  from its own SEO blog, which reviews competitors like TPT and
  Education.com and pitches Teach Simple as the alternative.
- Trustpilot sentiment is split: subscribers praise unlimited access for
  the price, while a persistent complaint thread reports free trials
  converting to paid and subscriptions that keep charging after repeated
  cancellation attempts. Third-party review aggregation echoes the
  billing complaints. Weigh the pattern, not any single review.
- The curation pitch (every item reviewed before listing) is the genuine
  differentiator versus TPT and Etsy, but the library is small, the team
  is tiny (single-digit staff per public LinkedIn data), and traffic
  trackers show modest, declining visits. Trajectory: marginal player,
  survival not assured.
- For Homa: the interesting datum is the model, not the company. It
  tests "curated library plus flat subscription" against TPT's per-item
  bazaar, the same trade Twinkl runs at real scale, and the billing
  complaints show how fast subscription trust burns when cancellation
  is hard. See `../comparisons/marketplaces.md`.
