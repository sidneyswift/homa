# Derek Owens (Lucid Education)

## Snapshot

Derek Owens is the human-graded end of homeschool distance math and
physics: recorded video courses (Prealgebra, Algebra 1, Geometry,
Algebra 2, Precalculus, AP Calculus, Computer Science, Physical Science,
Physics, with honors variants) where students watch hand-worked video
lessons, fill in companion note workbooks, and mail in handwritten
homework and tests that Owens and his assistants grade with roughly
48-hour turnaround. The billing is distinctive: $65 per month per course
while enrolled (nine months maximum), half price if the parent grades
instead, start any month, pause without paying. Alongside the distance
program he teaches live classes in the Atlanta area. His niche is the
family that wants real rigor and a real teacher's red pen on shown work,
without live class schedules, and his physics-first sequencing advice
(physics as the first high school science) travels widely in homeschool
STEM circles.

## Reputation and rankings

Reviewer and forum sentiment is unusually uniform: aggregated reviews
describe him as a gold standard for homeschool high school math and
physics, with Cathy Duffy and The Homeschool Mom cited among admirers,
and Well-Trained Mind threads praising the lecture clarity and the
grading quality (partial credit on logic shown, not answer-only
checking). The skeletal-notes format, where students complete workbook
notes while watching, is regularly singled out as the engagement
mechanism. The two recurring cautions: cost versus self-graded
courseware, and rigor that punishes students placed too high; there is
no placement test, only syllabi, prerequisites, and sample lectures,
plus a first-month tryout of regular versus honors assignments.
Trajectory: stable, capacity-bound, personality-anchored.
