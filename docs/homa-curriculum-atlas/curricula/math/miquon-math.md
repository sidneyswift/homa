# Miquon Math

## Snapshot

Miquon Math is a discovery-based lab program for grades 1 to 3,
developed in the 1960s by Lore Rasmussen at the Miquon School in
Pennsylvania and still in print. Six color-named consumable workbooks
(Orange through Purple, two per grade) hold over 650 tear-out lab
sheets that children explore with Cuisenaire rods, meeting all four
operations, fractions, and clock arithmetic in first grade. There is no
script; the essential Lab Sheet Annotations guide teaches the parent the
concept behind each sheet, the standard advice is to start at Orange
regardless of age, and the program is a classic on-ramp into conceptual
sequels like Singapore-family programs or Beast Academy.

## Reputation and rankings

A revered veteran with a small, devoted following: reviewers praise the
mental flexibility and pattern thinking it builds, and its rod
methodology remains the reference point for Cuisenaire teaching. Honest
caveats: it needs a comfortable, prep-willing parent (the annotations
are notes, not scripts), the 1960s formatting looks dated, and almost
everyone pairs it with or transitions into a fuller program. Trajectory:
stable heritage niche in eclectic and secular circles.
