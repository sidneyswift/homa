# Life of Fred

## Snapshot

Life of Fred is Dr. Stanley Schmidt's story-form math series: hardcovers
narrating the absurd adventures of Fred Gauss, a five-year-old professor
at KITTENS University, with math arising from the plot. Ten elementary
books (Apples through Jelly Beans) run up through pre-algebra, algebra,
geometry, trigonometry, calculus, and beyond. Chapters end with a small
"Your Turn to Play" problem set with solutions; Zillions of Practice
Problems supplements exist for upper courses. It is the canonical "math
for story kids" product and a perennial debate topic.

## Reputation and rankings

Cathy Duffy reviews the series warmly for conceptual understanding and
real-world application. Community verdict is settled: brilliant as a
supplement, risky as a standalone, because most children need more
practice than the books provide. Fans credit it with making math-averse
kids love math; critics call the fluency gap disqualifying. Trajectory:
stable cult classic on steady word-of-mouth.
