# Horizons Preschool

## Snapshot

Horizons Preschool is the workbook-traditional entry in this category:
a boxed, Bible-based, 180-lesson preschool year from Alpha Omega
Publications' Horizons line (the publisher itself is profiled at
[../all-in-one/alpha-omega.md](../all-in-one/alpha-omega.md)). The
complete set contains two full-color student workbooks (90 lessons
each), a two-part teacher's guide with step-by-step plans, weekly
supply lists, and reading lists, a resource packet of 300+ loose
reproducible sheets, flashcards, and posters, and a 17-song music CD.
Every lesson starts with Scripture and spirals through phonics, math,
language arts, science, social studies, health, PE, music, and crafts,
aimed at ages 4-5 with a lighter Preschool for Threes set below it. It
is school-at-home scaled down to preschool: the natural pick for Abeka
and LIFEPAC-minded families who want a visible workbook year before
kindergarten, and the structural opposite of the category's play-based
mainstream.

## Reputation and rankings

Horizons as a line is best known for its award-cited K-8 math and its
bright spiral workbooks; the preschool level rides that reputation.
Old Schoolhouse crew reviews and Christian homeschool blogs describe
30-40 minute days, colorful and genuinely fun workbook pages, and
strong kindergarten preparation; the standing reservations mirror the
whole workbook-preschool genre, that desk pages and 180 structured
lessons are more than a 4-year-old needs, and secular families are
excluded by the Bible integration. It sells steadily through AOP,
Christianbook, and curriculum stores. Trajectory: stable legacy
product inside AOP's active Horizons line.
