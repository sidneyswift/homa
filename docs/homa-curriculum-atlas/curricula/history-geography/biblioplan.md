# BiblioPlan

## Snapshot

BiblioPlan, by Rob and Julia Nalle, is a classical Christian history program
built explicitly on the same four-year chronological divisions as The Story of
the World (Ancients, Medieval, Early Modern 1600-1850, Modern 1850-2000) and
designed so an entire K-12 family studies the same era together, each child at
their own level. The core is Lesson Plans Plus (formerly the Family Guide): 34
weeks of three-day plans that schedule BiblioPlan's own spines (Remember the
Days for K-8, the Companion or Consider the Years for grades 6-12),
living-book options in four grade bands, writing assignments by trivium stage,
and even cross-references for outside spines including SOTW and Mystery of
History. Cool History worksheet books in four levels (Littles, Middles, Upper
Middles, Advanced) carry the assignments, with a Cool History Classics variant
keyed directly to SOTW.

## Reputation and rankings

BiblioPlan is one of Cathy Duffy's 103 Top Picks; her review highlights the
breadth of the multi-level machinery and the freedom to mix resources. It has
a durable following among large Christian families and co-ops (dedicated co-op
teacher guides exist), and its FAQ even documents how to run it alongside
Classical Conversations' three-year cycle. The critical notes: the component
catalog is confusing to newcomers (guide, spine, Cool History level, maps,
discussion guide, timeline all sold separately), the ebook-versus-print price
ladder invites decision fatigue, and secular families are out entirely given
the Bible-integrated spine years. Stable mid-size player; live online and
self-paced course options ($200 for an 18-month self-paced seat) are recent
growth moves.
