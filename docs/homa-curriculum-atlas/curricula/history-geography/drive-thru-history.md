# Drive Thru History

## Snapshot

Drive Thru History is a video-first history curriculum: host and co-creator
Dave Stotts drives to the actual sites (Rome, Israel, Boston) and teaches
ancient, American, and biblical history on camera with fast humor and
archaeology-forward storytelling. The Adventures platform wraps the episodes
(103 lesson units across series including Ancient History, America, The
Gospels, Acts to Revelation, Bible Unearthed, and Ends of the Earth) with
worksheets, discussion questions, suggested primary-source readings, and Dig
Deeper articles, pitched at roughly ages 12 and up but watched by whole
families. For most households it is a supplement or a light spine for one
season, not a multi-year program; it is the category's clearest example of
history as premium video content.

## Reputation and rankings

The series has strong name recognition from Christian broadcasting and years
of Homeschool Review Crew coverage; reviewers consistently call Stotts the
reason it works ("genuinely entertaining while still being substantive" is the
common verdict, and kids quote his jokes). It suits visual and auditory
learners and textbook-resistant teens. The critical notes: it is a survey by
episode, not a sequenced curriculum with assessment depth; coverage is limited
to the produced series (no medieval or world-cycle path); and the
biblical-apologetics framing makes it a nonstarter for secular families.
Stable niche product with periodic new series releases and a free seven-day
trial plus sample lessons.
