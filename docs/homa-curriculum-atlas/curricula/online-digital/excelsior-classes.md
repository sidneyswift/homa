# Excelsior Classes

## Snapshot

Excelsior Classes is the teacher-owned entrant in the live academy
lane: founded in July 2016 and deliberately organized as a consortium
of Christian educators rather than a school, so policies stay
teacher-directed and enrollment stays a la carte. It serves grades
5-12 with live, college-prep courses (most meet once a week for 90
minutes via Zoom, math twice for 60) run through a Canvas LMS, with
teacher-graded work, clubs, chapel, and monthly social events around
the edges. It is accredited by ACS WASC as a private school but grants
no diplomas by design; parents keep placement authority and the
transcript. A dual-credit partnership with LeTourneau University and
NCAA-approved core courses cover the proof needs, and the company
leads its marketing with a VeryWell Family "#1 Online Teacher-Led
Christian Program" nod. Distinctive posture: school-like accountability
(traditional calendar, grades, deadlines) with homeschool-parent
control intact, at a price under the classical academies.

## Reputation and rankings

- Long-form parent reviews are warm and practical: the Canvas one-login
  experience, lively 75-90 minute live sessions, extensive graded
  feedback, and teachers who mail biology prizes; the recurring framing
  is "simplified my life as a homeschooling, working mom."
- Marketing claims worth their salt: ACS WASC accreditation and NCAA
  approval are verifiable postures; the VeryWell Family #1 ranking is
  a media listicle the company promotes heavily.
- Community visibility is thinner than TPS or Wilson Hill (fewer forum
  threads, more review-blog coverage), consistent with its younger age
  and consortium scale; no significant critical thread surfaced beyond
  the generic live-class objections (cost per course, fixed times).
- Trajectory: rising quietly; ESA and state-funding integration (a
  dedicated registration path) positions it well for the funded-family
  wave. A fuller profile should pin course counts, teacher counts, and
  churn stories.
