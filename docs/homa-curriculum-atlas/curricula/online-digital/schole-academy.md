# Scholé Academy

## Snapshot

Scholé Academy is [Classical Academic Press](../all-in-one/classical-academic-press.md)'s
live teaching arm: founded in 2014, it runs a la carte online classes
(279 in the 2026-27 catalog) that teach CAP's own Latin, logic,
writing, and grammar lines plus a widening spread of literature,
sciences, arts, and modern languages, K-12. The brand is the philosophy:
scholé, "restful learning," Christopher Perrin's banner idea, promising
contemplative Socratic classes over frenetic testing. Classes are
capped at 15 students (18 by exception), meet live on Zoom with Canvas
as the LMS, and require placement assessment after registration. The
faith posture is ecumenical (Nicene Creed) with a clever structural
twist: a shared Great Hall plus tradition-specific Houses of Studies
(Orthodox St. Raphael School and others). It is deliberately
unaccredited and issues no official transcripts, affirming the parent
as registrar, which places it at the opposite accreditation pole from
The Potter's School and Wilson Hill.

## Reputation and rankings

- Reviewer and forum consensus rates instruction quality high and
  consistently notes the irony that "restful learning" classes carry
  real workloads, especially the writing and Latin tracks; families
  choosing between classical academies file Scholé as gentler-paced
  than Wilson Hill and more formation-focused than Veritas.
- Its natural buyers are already CAP families: the academy teaches the
  publisher's curricula with the publisher's pedagogy, and course
  materials frequently come from the CAP store (see the CAP profile's
  reputation section for the publisher's award shelf).
- Support for learning differences (a dedicated center with special
  needs instructors) and the tradition-specific houses are
  differentiators nothing else in this lane matches.
- Trajectory: growing with CAP's rise; the catalog's breadth now runs
  well past CAP's own product lines. A fuller profile should cover
  teacher quality variance (noted in community threads) and how the
  grading philosophy (mastery language plus numeric grades on request)
  lands with transcript-anxious parents.
