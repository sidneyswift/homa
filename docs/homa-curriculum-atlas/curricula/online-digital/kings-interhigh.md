# King's InterHigh

## Snapshot

King's InterHigh is the UK's flagship live online school: a full school
replacement, not a curriculum product, for ages 7 to 19 (Years 3-13).
Founded as InterHigh in 2005 and acquired in May 2021 by Inspired
Education Group (which runs over 100 premium schools worldwide), it
teaches the English National Curriculum through live, interactive,
recorded lessons on three timetables (UK, Middle East, Southeast Asia),
runs IGCSEs and A levels, and in September 2022 became one of the first
schools in the world to deliver the IB Diploma Programme fully online.
It reports over 6,000 students with 44% outside the UK. In December 2025
Ofsted visited under the Department for Education's Online Education
Accreditation Scheme, the UK's new legitimacy stamp for full-time online
providers. It sits at the opposite pole from Wolsey Hall Oxford
(`wolsey-hall-oxford.md`): timetabled classes and teachers versus
self-paced tutor-marked study, at roughly double the price for a full
load. In UK home education terms its students are usually counted as
electively home educated, since online schools are not registered
schools; the family remains legally responsible.

## Reputation and rankings

- Reviewed by The Good Schools Guide and WhichSchoolAdvisor; WSA
  documents the structure, the three-timetable model, and fee tables,
  and notes the graduated camera-contribution levels designed for
  anxious students ([WSA review](https://whichschooladvisor.com/uk/school-review/kings-interhigh)).
- The Ofsted OEAS accreditation report (Dec 2025 visit) is the strongest
  external validation any UK online school can currently hold: live
  lessons, recorded "library lessons", staff visibility, and safeguarding
  procedures all documented ([OEAS report](https://kingsinterhigh.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OEAS-Report-Kings-InterHigh.pdf)).
- Community sentiment (Trustpilot at 823 reviews, UK home ed forums)
  praises teachers and the school-without-the-building fit for anxious,
  athlete, and expat kids; recurring complaints are price, VAT
  passthrough since Jan 2025, and the usual live-school rigidity that
  self-paced families reject.
- Trajectory: rising; Inspired's capital, the online IB first, and DfE
  accreditation position it as the premium consolidator while smaller
  UK online schools compete on price. A fuller profile should add churn
  texture and results data.
