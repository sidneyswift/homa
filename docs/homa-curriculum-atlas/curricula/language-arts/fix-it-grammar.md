# Fix It! Grammar

## Snapshot

Fix It! Grammar is IEW's editing-based grammar course: six levels, each a
full year (33 weeks) in which the student hunts for and corrects errors in
a daily four-passage story (Nose Tree, Town Mouse and Country Mouse, Robin
Hood, and on) that unfolds across the whole book. Ten to fifteen minutes a
day, four days a week, plus a day-one teaching note each week. The pitch is
grammar in context instead of drill: concepts are introduced one at a time,
spiral back all year, and transfer to the student's own writing because the
skill practiced is literally editing. IEW recommends everyone start at
Level 1 regardless of age. It frequently rides along with IEW writing (it
is bundled into SSS Basic Plus packages) but sells plenty standalone as the
cheap, painless grammar answer.

## Reputation and rankings

- Shares IEW's award halo (first place, Old Schoolhouse 2020 grammar
  category) and its distribution; commonly recommended in the same breath
  as SSS.
- Community sentiment is warm: parents report kids who "actually enjoy"
  grammar, and the price is a non-issue. Critiques: no sentence
  diagramming (classical families add FLL or analytical grammar later),
  day-one teaching notes are unscripted enough that grammar-shy parents
  feel thin support, and starting teens in Level 1 feels awkward to some
  even though the publisher insists on it. The fourth edition (current)
  reworked layout and now tells students how many errors to find.
- Trajectory: stable, bundled into IEW's ecosystem.
