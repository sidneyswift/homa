# WriteShop

## Snapshot

WriteShop is an incremental, process-focused writing curriculum spanning
K through high school in three bands: Primary (Books A to C, ages roughly
5 to 8, heavily activity- and play-based with the parent scribing),
Junior (Books D to F, roughly grades 3 to 6, adding self-editing,
graphic organizers, and Fold-N-Go grammar minibooks), and WriteShop I and
II (grades 6 to 12, a rigorous brainstorm-draft-revise-edit cycle with
detailed checklists and rubrics). It occupies the structured middle
ground between IEW's formula and Brave Writer's freedom: explicit
process steps and objective grading tools, but the student's own content
rather than source-text rewriting. Now distributed by Demme Learning
(the Math-U-See company), which stabilized its retail presence.
Mindshare is second-tier but persistent; it is a common landing spot for
families who bounced off IEW's cost or style.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews both bands warmly, calling WriteShop I/II "a great
  resource for parents who lack confidence" teaching writing; it appears
  reliably in writing-curriculum roundups.
- Sentiment: praised for step-by-step structure, strong editing
  checklists and rubrics, and brainstorming that defeats blank-page
  paralysis; criticized for high parent involvement (teacher's guides
  are essential and prep is real), dense manuals at the younger levels,
  and consumable per-child workbooks that add up. The upper levels are
  considered genuinely rigorous college prep.
- Trajectory: stable; the Demme Learning acquisition and video course
  options modernized delivery.
