# The Sassafras Science Adventures

## Snapshot

Sassafras Science is Elemental Science's story-based elementary line: purpose-written adventure novels in which twins Blaine and Tracey Sassafras zipline around the world learning science, paired with a SCIDAT logbook (the notebooking layer) and a teacher guide adding demonstrations, experiments, and 2-day or 5-day schedules. Six volumes (zoology, anatomy, botany, earth science, geology, astronomy) run about a semester each, aimed at K-5 multi-age use. It is the living-books answer for science, with audiobook editions beloved for car-schooling; like its sibling Classic Series it is neutral rather than strictly secular, though secular reviewers commonly embrace it.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews the six-volume series alongside Elemental's Classic line.
- Community sentiment is warm: engaging for story-loving kids, effective multi-age, cheap in ebook form. Standing critiques: occasional editorial or science errors in the novels, and the teacher guide being required to make it a curriculum rather than a read-aloud.
- Trajectory: stable, mature niche companion line.
