# Noeo Science

## Snapshot

Noeo (pronounced NO-ee-oh) is the science-in-a-box implementation of Charlotte Mason method: living books, narration, and a genuinely complete experiment kit, one discipline per year. Courses run Biology 1-2, Chemistry 1-3, and Physics 1-3, with Level 1 for grades 1-3, Level 2 for 4-6, and Level 3 for 7-8; each package bundles an instructor's guide, lab manual, secular trade books, and experiment materials (70-100 experiments a year), open-and-go in 15-40 minute sessions on a 2-day or 4-day week. No busywork by design: read, experiment, narrate into a notebook. Long associated with Logos Press, it sells direct at noeoscience.com, sits at the premium end of elementary science pricing, and its included kit is the market's most direct answer to the experiment-supply problem.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews the Biology, Chemistry, and Physics courses (grades 1-8); Charlotte Mason reviewers treat it as a rare CM-faithful structured science.
- Comparative reviewers praise the kits as superior to living-books rivals and frame it as the convenience buy; the standing critique is price versus library-plus-DIY.
- NGSS per publisher FAQ: meets standards for grades 1-5, partial in 6-8.
- Trajectory: stable niche premium option, with charter editions for charter-fund purchasing.
