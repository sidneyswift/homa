# Generation Genius

## Snapshot

Generation Genius is a K-8 science (plus math and ELA) video-lesson platform produced in partnership with the National Science Teaching Association: energetic kid-hosted lab videos covering every K-8 NGSS disciplinary core idea, with downloadable lesson plans, DIY activity guides, worksheets, and quizzes. It is classroom-first with a homeschool plan; at home it functions as a high-production supplement (press play, optionally run the activity) in grade bands K-2, 3-5, and 6-8. Depth and lab progression are not the goal; the videos are.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy characterizes it as lively, visually engaging, and secular; it is a standing recommendation in r/secularhomeschool supplement threads, and teacher reviews are strongly positive on engagement and alignment.
- Standing critiques: introduces concepts rather than building deep lab or notebook work, so full-program families pair it with a spine; recurring auto-renewal billing complaints mean homeschoolers should disable auto-renew.
- Trajectory: growing, with subject expansion and school-market traction.
