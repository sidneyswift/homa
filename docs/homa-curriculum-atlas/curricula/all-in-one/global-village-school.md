# Global Village School

## Snapshot

Global Village School (GVS) is a small, non-profit, WASC-accredited K-12
distance-learning private school founded in 1999, built around a
progressive values identity: peace, social justice, diversity, and
sustainability integrated with core academics (its K-8 guides are
titled Whole Child, Healthy Planet). It operates as a
homeschool-private-school hybrid: families enroll for teacher support,
records, and an accredited diploma while designing individualized
learning paths from GVS curriculum guides or their own materials, or
buy the guides without enrolling. It is the values-forward counterpart
to Oak Meadow's accredited-distance-school model, serving secular,
unschooling-adjacent, and activist-minded families, including many with
neurodivergent kids.

## Reputation and rankings

Coverage lives in directory profiles and progressive homeschool
roundups rather than mainstream review sites; no Cathy Duffy review was
found. Its reputation centers on flexibility (curriculum adapted to the
student, year-round rolling admission, self-directed paths), the WASC
accreditation earned "without compromising our educational philosophy"
(the school's own framing), and its explicit welcome to LGBTQIA+
families and students with learning and mental-health challenges.
The obvious trade-offs mirror Oak Meadow's enrolled track: private
school tuition, and a values identity strong enough that families not
seeking social-justice framing will look elsewhere. Trajectory: stable
small niche; it is a values community with a school attached more than
a mass-market curriculum.
