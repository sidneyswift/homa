# Rod and Staff

## Snapshot

Rod and Staff is a conservative Mennonite publisher in Crockett, Kentucky
(publishing since 1958) whose plain, black-and-white, Bible-saturated
textbooks are the anti-trend product of the homeschool market: hardcover,
non-consumable, extremely cheap, rigorous in a memorize-and-diagram way,
and sold by phone and paper catalog because the publisher keeps no real
web presence by conviction (Milestone Books is the authorized online
distributor). Coverage runs from preschool readiness materials through
grade 10 (math ends at grade 8, with recordkeeping and accounting for
grades 9-10). Its English and grammar program, with early
sentence diagramming, is legendary well beyond Mennonite circles, and
many eclectic and classical families buy just that strand. Mennonite
distinctives (nonresistance, separation, plain dress in illustrations,
hard work as virtue) run through everything.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy notes the strong Anabaptist doctrine throughout, the
  classroom-origin busywork to prune, and solid traditional academics;
  the English series is her recurring recommendation from the line.
- Community sentiment: "gold standard grammar," unbeatable durability
  and price, scripted teacher's manuals that make it easy to teach; the
  critical side calls it dry, visually austere, culturally specific, and
  learning-by-recitation. Multi-decade users praise the same books
  serving every child in the family.
- Trajectory: stable inside its constituency and as a grammar supplement
  brand outside it; it does not chase the mainstream market and never
  will.
- Homa note: families who mention Rod and Staff usually run either a
  plain-community full program or an eclectic stack using its English or
  math; ask which before assuming the household's style.
