# Amanda Bennett Unit Studies (UnitStudy.com)

## Snapshot

Amanda Bennett Unit Studies is the digital-PDF chapter of unit-study
history: short, cheap, downloadable topic studies (Baseball, Oceans,
Davy Crockett, Electricity, Christmas) that made the format an impulse
purchase instead of a lifestyle commitment. Bennett, an engineer turned
homeschool author writing since the 1990s, built four series sold at
UnitStudy.com: Download N Go (one-to-two-week studies for K-4 with
integrated lapbooks and day-by-day "quests"), Unit Study Adventures
(four-week studies for K-12 with lower and upper level daily lessons),
plus the Passport geography and Heroes for Today series. The studies
lean on booklists, embedded web links, guided research, and notebooking
rather than crafts, and their heyday ran through the 2010s blog and
review-crew era, when Download N Go launched in partnership with The
Old Schoolhouse's promotion machinery. The catalog (76 products) is
mature and quietly maintained rather than growing.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy carries a favorable review of all four series, filing
  them as limited unit studies: easy to start ("pull one up and get
  started"), research-and-writing flavored, combinable into a full
  year for families who want that.
- The 2010s review-blog record is warm and voluminous (The Old
  Schoolhouse review circuit, Ben and Me, Confessions of a
  Homeschooler): the repeated praise is genuine open-and-go with
  library-basket workflow; the repeated caveat, then and now, is
  internet dependence, static PDFs whose embedded links and book
  recommendations age (the publisher re-issues updated files), and
  book-sorting overhead on the long recommended-reading lists.
- Trajectory: legacy-stable. It remains the reference answer for "cheap
  one-week unit study on a topic," while the format's current energy
  (family-style, design-forward, credit-mapped) has moved to Gather
  Round and Campfire Curriculums. A fuller profile should verify
  current authorship activity and the size of its remaining audience.
