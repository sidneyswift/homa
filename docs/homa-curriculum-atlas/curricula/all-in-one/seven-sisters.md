# 7Sisters Homeschool

## Snapshot

7Sisters Homeschool is an ebook publisher and advice culture built by a
team of veteran homeschool moms, aimed squarely at homeschooling high
school without fear. The catalog is downloadable PDFs: literature study
guides that deliberately examine only two or three literary elements per
book (the house slogan is no busywork, don't kill the book), full-year
literature courses assembled from them, writing and composition
curricula, human development, career exploration, financial literacy,
and electives. Nearly everything is adaptable across published levels
(average, college prep, advanced, honors) with instructions for leveling
up, which doubles as transcript coaching: the brand teaches parents that
mom-designed courses and mom-issued credits are normal and defensible.
Around the products sits a large free content and podcast operation
(their long-running homeschool high school podcast and transcript
guides) that gives 7Sisters outsized trust relative to its tiny prices.

## Reputation and rankings

There is no major award record; the reputation is word-of-mouth and
review-blog driven, and it is warm. Reviewers consistently praise the
no-busywork design (guides run focused question sets with background,
vocabulary, writing assignments, and answer keys, in fillable PDFs) and
the built-in leveling tables for honors credit. The community treats
7Sisters as much as a mentoring voice as a curriculum vendor: their
transcript how-tos and credit guidance circulate well beyond their
customer base. Honest limits: these are guides and ebooks, not full
taught courses; parents wanting instruction, grading, or accountability
must add it; and the literature selections skew classic and gentle.
Trajectory: stable, sustained by the podcast and the permanent anxiety
of homeschooling high school.
