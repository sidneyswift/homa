# TAN Academy (TAN Books homeschool line)

## Snapshot

TAN Academy is the homeschool curriculum arm of TAN Books, the
traditionalist Catholic publisher founded in 1967 in Rockford, Illinois
by Thomas A. Nelson to keep pre-conciliar Catholic classics in print, and
owned since its 2008 bankruptcy sale by Saint Benedict Press (Good Will
Publishers) near Charlotte, North Carolina, which also acquired Neumann
Press's Catholic children's catalog in 2013. Rather than an enrollment
school or a full box, TAN Academy is a set of multimedia subject lines
marketed K-12 "by homeschoolers, for homeschoolers": the flagship "Story
Of" history series by Phillip Campbell (The Story of Civilization,
volumes 1-4, plus The Story of the Bible in two volumes and The Story of
the Church, seven volumes total) and the Foundations of Science
elementary series (eight topical books from Animals to Chemistry and
Physics). Each line uses a "schoolhouse model": one narrative spine
taught to all ages at once, with a textbook and tests for older students
and an activity book plus teacher's manual for younger ones, wrapped in
dramatized audio and streamed video lectures. TAN also markets academic
coaching sessions with enrollment-style language, but the product reality
as of this writing is curriculum lines plus the wider TAN religion and
devotional catalog that other Catholic programs schedule as spines (MODG
assigns TAN's The Old World and America, and Seton's own history credits
TAN as the other early reprinter of Catholic school texts).

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews The Story of Civilization as the Catholic
  counterpart to Story of the World: same narrative four-volume,
  four-year shape, "written primarily for a Catholic audience" with more
  thorough United States coverage; families cycle the volumes grades 1-8
  (see `../history-geography/story-of-the-world.md` for the program it
  is bought against).
- Catholic review blogs (Catholic Sistas is representative) praise the
  schoolhouse model's multi-age economics and the audio dramas; the
  series descends from TAN's 1930s-era Bible History reprint lineage,
  refreshed into modern packaging.
- Trajectory: rising within its lane; TAN's homeschool line has grown
  from reprints into original multimedia curricula, and its price point
  (about $119-170 per complete volume set as of Jul 2026) positions it
  as the affordable Catholic history and science answer rather than a
  full-curriculum competitor. A fuller profile should verify what "TAN
  Academy enrollment" actually includes.
