# The Peaceful Press

## Snapshot

The Peaceful Press, founded by Jennifer Pepito, publishes gentle,
literature-and-project-based family guides for preschool through
elementary, blending Charlotte Mason and Montessori influences: daily
read-alouds from beloved trade books (The Peaceful Preschool's
letter-of-the-week; The Playful Pioneers' American history through the
Little House series; The Kind Kingdom's European history through Narnia,
fairy tales, and Shakespeare retellings), with copywork sheets in
multiple levels, narration, poetry, hymns, nature study, recipes, and
practical life skills. Each digital guide costs $49 and runs a family
26-30 weeks; math and phonics are always separate. It is the budget
entry point of the gentle-CM aesthetic, strongest in the PK-3 band Homa
targets first.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews the line favorably as literature and project-based
  unit curriculum "based on the philosophies of Maria Montessori and
  Charlotte Mason," noting the CM influence deepens in the elementary
  guides (copywork, narration, picture study, nature walks).
- Community sentiment: warm, mother-to-mother loyalty ("the book list
  alone is worth the money" is a representative store review; Playful
  Pioneers carries 81 store reviews). The brand rides the same gentle
  Instagram current as Wild + Free, where Pepito is a visible voice.
- Limits families cite: not a complete curriculum (no math, phonics,
  spelling), elementary-only ceiling, and heavy read-aloud dependence on
  books the family must source.
- Trajectory: stable niche with steady new volumes (Nourishing Nature
  kindergarten, world history and geography guides).
