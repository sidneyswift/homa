# Wildwood Curriculum

## Snapshot

Wildwood is the free, secular Charlotte Mason curriculum: a volunteer,
community-built project publishing complete CM programs modeled on the
original PNEU programmes of the 1920s-30s, with modern, diverse,
inclusive book selections and no religious content. It organizes
children into Mason's Forms rather than grades (Form IB at ages 6-7
through Form III at 12-14 as of this writing, with higher forms in
development), keeps mornings academic and afternoons for nature study
and art, and offers both a narrowed made-for-you booklist and an
Options page explaining the reasoning behind each choice. Everything is
free on the website; families source the books. It is the zero-dollar
anchor of the secular CM world and a frequent first stop before
families pay for Build Your Library.

## Reputation and rankings

Well regarded in secular CM and SEA circles as the faithful-to-Mason
free option, praised for authenticity (its authors point users to the
original PNEU programmes for comparison) and inclusive booklists, with
the standing caveats that it is a work in progress (Form III still
being built), volunteer-paced, and requires the parent to assemble and
schedule everything themselves. Community guidance often frames the
choice as Wildwood (free, DIY, authentic) versus Build Your Library
(paid, scheduled, open-and-go). The team also runs a podcast,
Stonechats, which serves as its community channel.
