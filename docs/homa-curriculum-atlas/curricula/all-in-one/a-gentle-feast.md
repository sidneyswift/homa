# A Gentle Feast

## Snapshot

A Gentle Feast (AGF), by Julie Ross, is a paid, open-and-go Charlotte
Mason curriculum for grades 1-12 that puts the whole family on one
four-year history cycle (each cycle blends American, British, and world
history with an ancient civilization), taught across four Forms (I:
grades 1-3, II: 4-6, III: 7-9, IV: 10-12), three 12-week terms per year.
It sits between AmblesideOnline and Simply Charlotte Mason: more planned
and more modern-leveled than AO (books sit at age-appropriate reading
levels), more purist than SCM, sold as lifetime-access digital plans plus
morning time packets, with math excluded. Reviewers rank it first among
CM packages for parent training and support, and knock it as the most
expensive of the CM options with occasional thin history stretches.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews it warmly as a flexible family-style CM curriculum
  for Christian families; one roundup ranked it first among CM packages
  for its training videos and support.
- Community praise: the whole-family cycle, open-and-go usability,
  age-appropriate book levels (kids self-read earlier than with AO), and
  survivability through disrupted seasons.
- Criticism: priciest of the CM options compared (AO free, Living
  Heritage cheaper); morning time sold separately; one leaver found the
  book list less rich than AO's and history readings thin in stretches;
  next cycle's book list not visible before purchase.
- Trajectory: stable-to-rising within the CM niche, buoyed by Ross's
  podcast presence (The Charlotte Mason Show).
