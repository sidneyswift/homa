# Structured Home Learning

Placement note: the wave 2b plan slotted this stub under online-digital,
but the product is a physical book box with no online delivery, so it
lives in all-in-one where its category is true.

## Snapshot

Structured Home Learning is the most established UK curriculum-in-a-box
provider: year-level packs of real published textbooks (Cambridge,
Pearson, Collins, and specialist publishers) curated against the National
Curriculum for England, with an SHL day-by-day work schedule and parents'
notes turning them into an open-and-go home education year. Founder and
educational director Lindsay Brown, a former primary teacher who home
educated her own children, has supplied home education materials since
2005 (originally trading as Wits End), and the company reports customers
on every continent. Packs cover EYFS and Key Stages 1-4 in three tiers:
Essential (English, maths, science), Complete (adds history, geography,
art), and Enhanced (adds science experiments, cookery, music). The pitch
is deliberately anti-digital: physical books, no screens, no
subscriptions, ability-matched per subject (Year 4 English beside Year 3
maths), start any time. It is the UK's closest analog to the US boxed
tradition of Sonlight or BookShark, minus the literature philosophy: this
is the National Curriculum, at home, on paper.

## Reputation and rankings

- No formal review coverage (no Good Schools Guide or Cathy Duffy entry);
  its reputation is word-of-mouth in UK home education groups, where it
  is the standard answer for "a full offline curriculum that keeps us
  aligned in case the kids go back to school".
- Its own case studies foreground SEND and school-trauma families using
  the schedule-plus-ability-matching combination, the same demand driver
  documented across the UK providers in this wave
  ([case study](https://www.structuredhomelearning.com/case-study)).
- The return-to-school insurance framing (packs follow the National
  Curriculum, so re-entry is not disadvantaged) is its clearest
  positioning line and a genuinely distinct purchase logic from US boxed
  curricula.
- Trajectory: stable niche; a fuller profile should verify current pack
  contents, KS4 handling (GCSE-year support without being an exam
  provider), and community sentiment depth.
