# Campfire Curriculums

## Snapshot

Campfire Curriculums is the fast-rising outdoor-adventure wing of the
unit-study revival: whole-family studies (one purchase, four levels in
one, K-12, everything but math) on topics no one else touches, Skills
of a Camper, Survive off the Land, Volcanologist, Meteorologist, Bees
and Beekeeping, Home Defense, alongside novel studies like A Christmas
Carol. Founded by a homeschool dad who is a practicing ER provider and
US special operations veteran (the origin story starts with his son
asking how to fish), it wraps Charlotte Mason-style narrative teaching
in a five-step house method (Read, Explore, Write, Apply, Experience)
and deliberately courts fathers and working parents. Units ship as
no-printing-required PDFs in three tiers (Flexible, Classic, Signature
with a Core Connections companion), and the catalog maps roughly eight
Signature units to a Carnegie-credit year across science, history,
ELA, geography, Bible, and ethics. The brand's whole register,
self-sufficiency, careers, wilderness, is the aesthetic opposite of
Gather Round's soft beauty, aimed at the same multi-kid family.

## Reputation and rankings

- Cathy Duffy reviews it warmly as a limited-unit-study line with
  "unusual topics" and "an adventurous spirit," notes the three-level
  differentiation and Faith Talk sections, and flags that the Core
  Connections full-curriculum claim is too new to judge.
- Award shelf: repeated second and third places across categories in
  homeschool.com's Back to Homeschool awards (a reader-voting,
  marketing-heavy circuit per `../../market-overview.md`); the
  homepage's "award winning 5 years in a row" banner rides that
  circuit. No Cathy Duffy Top Pick or Practical Homeschooling win
  found.
- Community sentiment (review blogs, unit-study groups) is
  enthusiastic about engagement, dads participating, and the zero-prep
  screencast-or-print delivery; the open questions are the standard
  unit-study ones, whether Core Connections credits satisfy
  transcript-anxious families, and longevity of a young catalog.
- Trajectory: rising fast; it is the name currently pulling
  adventure-minded and father-involved families into the unit-study
  format. A fuller profile should verify founding year, founder
  identity, and sales scale.
