# Equipping Minds

## Snapshot

Equipping Minds is a cognitive development program, not an academic
curriculum: daily one-on-one exercises and games meant to strengthen the
underlying machinery (working memory, processing speed, visual and
auditory processing, attention, reasoning) so that academics become
learnable. Founder Carol Brown, a former private school leader in
Danville, Kentucky whose doctorate is from The Southern Baptist
Theological Seminary, built it on Reuven Feuerstein's theories of structural
cognitive modifiability and mediated learning, layered with
neurodevelopmental elements (primitive reflex exercises, vision
exercises, optional sound therapy). The recommended dose is 30 to 60
minutes a day, five days a week, for 12 to 36 weeks, delivered by the
parent from a teacher workbook (2026 edition, 245 pages) with training
videos, or through paid online therapy sessions and the parent-training
ROSES packages. Its audience is the Christian homeschool special-needs
circuit (Down syndrome, autism, ADHD, FASD, dyslexia, brain injury),
where it functions as the home-scale alternative to cognitive-training
clinics.

## Reputation and rankings

- Reputation lives in the Christian special-needs homeschool world:
  conference workshops, the Homeschooling Special Needs EXPO, SPED
  Homeschool circles, and word of mouth from adoptive and Down syndrome
  families; it has no mainstream curriculum-reviewer record.
- Evidence is largely first-party: Brown's doctoral research and case
  studies, with a 2018 publication in the Journal of Alternative Medicine
  Research. Independent replication is absent, and the wider
  cognitive-training category (working memory training and its transfer
  to academics) remains scientifically contested. The program's
  neurodevelopmental add-ons (primitive reflex work, sound therapy) sit
  in alternative-therapy territory. Families evaluating it should read
  the research page and price the full recommended stack, which reaches
  several hundred dollars before any therapist hours.
- Trajectory: stable niche presence with a refreshed 2026 workbook
  edition and steady conference visibility.
