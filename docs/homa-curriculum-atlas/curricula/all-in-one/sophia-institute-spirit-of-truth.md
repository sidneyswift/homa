# Spirit of Truth Home Edition (Sophia Institute)

## Snapshot

Spirit of Truth Home Edition is a K-8 Catholic religion curriculum from
Sophia Institute for Teachers, the education arm of Sophia Institute
Press, the nonprofit New Hampshire publisher John Barger founded in 1983
to bring Catholic classics back into print. It sits in this atlas not as
a full curriculum but as the leading example of the Catholic market's
"religion slot": a single-subject program eclectic Catholic families
drop into an otherwise mixed stack. The program began in parishes,
was adapted for Catholic schools, and then reworked into a home edition
with homeschooling-family input: 33 weeks of parent-led lesson plans per
grade built around sacred art reflections, saint stories, scripture,
games and role-plays, critical thinking questions, and "Catholic Family
Life" liturgical living suggestions per unit, covering the Catechism
age-appropriately across the grades. A consumable student workbook
(about $17-19) and a parent guide (about $40) per grade keep the cost
under fifty dollars a year, and a separate Spirit of Truth sacramental
preparation line serves First Reconciliation, First Communion, and
Confirmation years. For the wave 1 parallel, this is the Catholic
analog of a Bible-curriculum slot in Protestant stacks, competing
against Ignatius Press's Faith and Life (which CHC schedules), Seton's
in-house religion, and the Baltimore Catechism tradition.

## Reputation and rankings

- Homeschool reviews are positive and specific: Holy Heroes' long-form
  review praises the parish-to-school-to-home refinement path, the
  organized teacher manual an older child can self-run, the online
  parent support site, and the price; Raising Wild Saints names its
  sacramental prep programs the best available and notes families use
  the school edition at home too.
- Traditionalist-leaning Our Lady of Victory School lists Spirit of
  Truth among trustworthy resources for Catholic parents, evidence it
  travels across the Catholic spectrum (see
  `our-lady-of-victory-school.md`).
- Trajectory: rising; Sophia's teacher-institute distribution gives it
  parish and school network effects most homeschool religion products
  lack. A fuller profile should add Cathy Duffy coverage if it appears,
  usage scale, and how it competes with Faith and Life inside CHC-style
  stacks.
