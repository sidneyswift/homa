# Guest Hollow

## Snapshot

Guest Hollow is a small family company selling literature-based,
multimedia-heavy curriculum schedules from preschool through high
school, with a signature economics: the core schedules and its edited
online textbooks are free, and the family pays only for printable and
editable versions (high school biology's printable schedule is $35) plus
the real books, which the company cheerfully suggests borrowing from the
library. The flagship is its free high school biology, a CK-12 textbook
rewrite with embedded videos, a free printable workbook, and both
creationist and secular paths; Chemistry in the Kitchen (math-light,
practical) and literature-rich history, geography, and language arts
schedules fill out the catalog. The model is buffet, not box: the parent
acts as editor, picking from oversized lists of books, videos, recipes,
and activities. For budget families homeschooling high school, it is one
of the few genuinely free credible science options.

## Reputation and rankings

Its biology took first place in the Practical Homeschooling Reader's
Choice awards four years running (2020 to 2023) and second in 2024 and
2025, remarkable visibility for a free product from a tiny company.
Cathy Duffy reviews the biology favorably, crediting the CK-12
adaptation and the added videos, books, and lab options, and noting
secular homeschoolers can run the schedule with the original text.
Aggregated sentiment praises the engagement (kids who hated their old
curriculum is the house testimonial genre) and flags the honest
trade-offs: nothing is boxed, the parent sources every book, and the
choose-your-own-adventure density can overwhelm structure-needing
families. Trajectory: stable cult favorite with slow growth.
