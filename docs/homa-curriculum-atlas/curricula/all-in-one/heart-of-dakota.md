# Heart of Dakota

## Snapshot

Heart of Dakota (HOD) is a family-run South Dakota publisher (author
Carrie Austin) whose product is a ladder of twelve Charlotte
Mason-inspired guide books covering preschool through high school: Little
Hands to Heaven and the Little Hearts and Bigger Hearts guides for the
early years, the Hearts for Him Through Time series for the middle
years, and Hearts for Him Through High School. Each guide is a
day-by-day, box-checkable plan scheduling living books, Bible, narration,
dictation, and hands-on work, deliberately ramping students from
parent-led lessons toward independent study by high school. Guides are
placed by skill rather than grade, and families buy the guide alone or in
economy-to-deluxe packages with the scheduled books. It is the
strongest-reviewed of the smaller CM-flavored all-in-ones.

## Reputation and rankings

- Practical Homeschooling Reader Awards 2025: HOD won 8 awards, including
  first place in History, High School Curriculum, Literature, Geography,
  Middle School Curriculum, and Elementary Curriculum (publisher's
  announcement of the reader-voted results).
- Cathy Duffy reviews the PreK-8 series and the high school courses
  favorably: comprehensive, easy to use, low prep, biblically based from
  a Protestant view, with narration and dictation carrying the CM
  methods.
- Community sentiment: praised for taking the guesswork out of CM and
  for the independence ramp; criticized because each guide targets a
  narrow age band (hard to combine many siblings, the opposite of
  [My Father's World](my-fathers-world.md)), for providential-history
  framing in some texts, and for missing master supply lists.
- Trajectory: stable niche favorite with unusually strong award
  performance for its size.
