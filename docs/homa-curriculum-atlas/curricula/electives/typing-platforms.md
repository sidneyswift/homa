# TypingClub and Typing.com

## Snapshot

TypingClub (by EdClub) and Typing.com (by Teaching.com) are the free
typing default duo: two ad-supported, browser-based touch-typing
curricula that between them own the homeschool typing slot, usually
around grades 3-6, as the classic "check the box" elective a child
self-runs in 10-15 minutes a day. TypingClub offers 700+ sequenced
lessons with badges, videos, an animated instructor, and Jungle Junior
for pre-readers; star thresholds gate progression, making it
effectively mastery-paced. Typing.com runs beginner-to-advanced lesson
modules plus typing tests, printable completion certificates, and
bundled extras (digital citizenship units, intro coding). Both are free
because schools are the actual customer; homeschool parents ride along.
The practical difference reviewers converge on: Typing.com is simpler
to start and includes a progress dashboard, while TypingClub's parent
oversight requires registering its free School Edition (creating a
"class" for your own kids), slightly technical but then
premium-grade.

## Reputation and rankings

- Typing roundups for homeschoolers routinely place both at the top of
  the free tier, treating them as interchangeable defaults and
  splitting on taste: TypingClub for a more modern interface and young
  learners, Typing.com for straightforwardness and the bundled digital
  citizenship content.
- Standing complaints, same for both: ads in the free tiers (parents
  use ad blockers or pay the small upgrade), repetitive drills for
  fast learners, and games that entertain more than they teach.
- Neither has award-circuit presence; their mindshare is pure default
  status, like Khan for math practice: free, good enough, and already
  known.
- Trajectory: stable infrastructure. Typing itself keeps sliding
  earlier (grade 3-4 starts) as more curricula and testing move
  on-screen.
