# SQUILT Music Appreciation

## Snapshot

SQUILT (Super Quiet UnInterrupted Listening Time) is the homeschool
music appreciation brand: a method and product line by Mary Prather, a
music education graduate (Southern Methodist University) who taught
public school music for a decade before homeschooling. The method is
disarmingly simple: children listen to one piece in genuine silence,
then talk and notebook about its elements (rhythm, tempo, dynamics,
instrumentation, mood), often following a printable "listening map."
Products come in two shapes: self-paced PDF guides (music eras,
composers, instruments of the orchestra, holidays, movie music) and
SQUILT LIVE!, a membership with a clickable monthly listening calendar
(one piece per day) and two live 45-60 minute Zoom lessons a month
taught by Prather herself, with recordings available for 30 days and a
co-op license capped at five families watching together. It slots
naturally into morning time and Charlotte Mason-style routines, PK
through middle school, with families of older kids reporting it
stretches up fine.

## Reputation and rankings

- Reviewer verdicts are consistently positive: an easy, genuinely
  substantive way to make music appreciation happen with a non-musical
  parent, with the listening maps and Prather's on-screen warmth
  singled out ([Smarter Learning Guide](https://smarterlearningguide.com/squilt-music-review/),
  [Humility and Doxology](https://www.humilityanddoxology.com/squilt-music-review/)).
- The CM and morning-time blog ecosystem is its distribution channel,
  the same social path as You ARE an Artist
  ([you-are-an-artist](you-are-an-artist.md)), with which it is
  constantly co-recommended as the music-and-art pair for busy homes.
- Cost caution appears in reviews: $20+/mo for the live membership and
  a $200+ complete DIY bundle is real money for a non-core subject,
  the classic elective-budget squeeze.
- Trajectory: stable niche leader; music appreciation has no
  comparably branded competitor at this price point (Hoffman owns
  performance, SQUILT owns listening; see
  [hoffman-academy](hoffman-academy.md)).
