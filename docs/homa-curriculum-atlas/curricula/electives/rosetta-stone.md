# Rosetta Stone Homeschool

## Snapshot

Rosetta Stone is the legacy brand of language software (founded 1992)
and the immersion pole of the homeschool language market: its Dynamic
Immersion method pairs native-speaker audio with photographs and never
shows English, while TruAccent speech recognition scores pronunciation,
across 25 languages. IXL Learning acquired it in March 2021, putting it
in the same portfolio as IXL and (since 2023) Teachers Pay Teachers
(see `../../marketplaces/teachers-pay-teachers.md`), part of IXL's
consolidation of homeschool-adjacent brands. A Homeschool Edition
(lifetime license around $179-219 per student) is distributed through
the homeschool retail channel: Sonlight, BookShark, Rainbow Resource,
Christianbook, and Homeschool Buyers Club. Its homeschool niche is
transcript-grade but parent-light: unlike Duolingo (see
[duolingo](duolingo.md)), families do commonly treat Rosetta Stone as
primary high school language content, usually paired with a grammar
supplement to justify the credit.

## Reputation and rankings

- Sentiment is polarized and stable across decades: praised for
  pronunciation training, listening comprehension, and total parent
  independence; criticized for teaching essentially no explicit
  grammar, which frustrates analytical learners and forces
  credit-seeking families to bolt on a grammar resource.
- Recurring practical complaints: finicky speech recognition,
  per-student licensing that multiplies cost for big families, and
  auto-renewal and customer service frustrations in reviews.
- Reviewers position it between poles: more school-credible than
  Duolingo, less rigorous than textbook programs like Breaking the
  Barrier; a common homeschool pattern is Rosetta Stone for immersion
  plus a workbook for grammar, logged together as one credit per year.
- Trajectory: stable legacy brand under IXL; the perpetual "lifetime
  unlimited" discounting (list $399, promoted at $149-179) has become
  its de facto price and its main marketing message.
