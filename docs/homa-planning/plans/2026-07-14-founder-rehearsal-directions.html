Rehearsal mode: walk in her shoes without touching her data

You want to feel the product exactly as a family feels it, click for click (the deeper language check, the rescore, the rewritten report), before she does, without risking her rows or anyone else's. Four ways to build that, one shared errand, pick and steal below.

Every family, kid, number, and message below is fake (the Alvarez famy does not exist). No real family data is loaded, and no product code or route was changed by this document.
Finding 1 · becoming a family is cheap
The resume cookie IS identity

requireFamily() checks the resume cookie before the login session. Whoever holds a family's link token walks its onboarding, deep dives, and report as that family. The admin already mints these tokens for reminder emails.

Finding 2 · the clone kit half-exists
Family fold = a table map

lib/family-fold.ts already enumerates every family-scoped table to MOVE a family. A clone is the same walk with copy instead of move, plus fresh ids and tokens.

Finding 3 · the real danger is outbound
Email, SMS, a mailed card, crons

Report and reminder emails, task texts, the physical thank-you card sweep, retest crons, and the funnels all key on families. A rehearsal family must be fenced at these chokepoints or it emails a real person and pollutes your metrics.

Finding 4 · precedent exists
A sim family already ran the product

The season sim (scripts/_tmp_season_sim) drove a synthetic persona through signup, chat, and reports with Playwright. And scripts/prod-write-guard.mts already gates hosted-DB writes behind --confirm-prod.

The shared errand in every direction: from the admin file of the (fake) Alvarez famy, become them safely, run Nico's deeper language check (his chart reads -12), answer three questions, watch the number and report react, note the reassurance moments, then throw the rehearsal away. Real rows untouched, nothing sent to anyone.
