Meeting Homa · four directions for teaching the channels

The problem: Homa is one assistant reachable by web chat, text, email, and phone call, and she remembers across all of them. No family is ever told this. Most people have only ever chatted with an AI inside a web app, so nothing in their head expects a text back, a replyable email, or a callable number. This lab holds one family and one goal constant, and varies WHERE and HOW the product teaches it: by Friday of week one, Maria knows she can text Homa, has saved Homa's contact so texts say a name instead of a number, and knows what arrives on which surface.

Everything below uses fake fixture data: Maria Rivera and her kids Emma (4) and Mateo (7) are invented, the numbers shown are placeholders, and no real family, lead, or production record was loaded. No product code or route was changed by this file.

Live production regression, found while grounding this lab: the founder ask of 2026-07-10 sent Homa's vCard contact card at TWO moments, a stranger's first inbound text and the link text when a number connects to a family. The second was silently dropped on 2026-07-11: commit 997bda9 (multi-parent v0) rewrote apps/web/lib/family-phone.ts from a stale base, and sendLinkNotificationSms lost the card. Today no account holder ever receives the card, the header comment in apps/web/lib/contact-card.ts claims otherwise, and sms.contact_card_sent with trigger linked can never fire. Every direction below assumes this is restored; question 1 asks how.

What exists today
The pipes are built. The introduction is not.

SMS, email, and voice all route into the one agent with family context (agent/channels/telnyx.ts, agent/channels/email.ts, api/voice/context). The vCard is served at /homa.vcf. Strangers who text first get the card and a concierge. Account holders get one bare link text and silence about everything else.

Where the product teaches channels today
Three quiet corners, zero moments.

A form label ("Cell number, so you can text Homa"), a pill buried in Settings ("Text Homa · number"), and the task editor's delivery choices. The landing page, FAQ, report, report email, and chat home never mention that text, email, or calls exist. One email footer even claims "a real person answers", which is false: Homa answers.

The asymmetry to fix
Reports go by email. Research goes by text. Nobody is told why.

Kid reports send email-only (lib/map/email.ts sendReportEmail). Morning research briefs send text-first with email fallback (lib/tasks delivery, on by default). The same assistant shows up on two surfaces in her first 24 hours with no thread connecting them, from an unsaved number and an unfamiliar address.

Four directions

Same family, same week, same goal in every frame; only the structure changes. Each direction is a different bet on WHO introduces the channels (the interface or Homa herself), WHEN (one moment, the wow, a drip, or always), and WHERE the contact gets saved. Pick a winner, steal details from the others.

UI-led · one explicit moment · during onboarding

Direction 1 · The introduction step

Bet: attention is highest inside onboarding, so spend thirty deliberate seconds there introducing Homa as a person with four doors, and get the contact saved before the report ever renders. A new step right after she types her cell number. It names all four channels once, texts her the contact card while she watches, and asks her to save it now. Education happens exactly once, explicitly, and everything later builds on it.

Where does the step sit?
What it fixesThe mid-onboarding link text stops being an orphan: the screen she is looking at explains the text the moment it lands, and the save action happens while both are in hand.
What it costsOne more step between her and the report, at the exact stretch of onboarding where every added tap costs finishes. Skippers learn nothing and there is no second chance built in.

UI-led · wow-moment attach · report page + report email

Direction 2 · The report hands you her card

Bet: teach nothing until you have proven something. No new onboarding step. The report, the first real value Homa delivers, ends by introducing her: the letter from Homa closes with her contact block (face, number, save button, one line per channel), and the report EMAIL carries the same block, so both carriers of the wow teach the same lesson. The call offer that already lives in the letter grows into the full set of doors.

Agent-led · first-use drip · zero new web UI

Direction 3 · She introduces herself, channel by channel

Bet: channel education belongs IN the channel, in Homa's own voice, on first use, and nowhere else. No new screens. Each surface's first touch carries a one-time introduction beat, tracked per channel so it never repeats: the link text becomes a real introduction with the card, the first morning brief opens with one line saying who is texting, the first email reply teaches that replying works, and the first inbound text from a linked number gets a one-time welcome before the answer. Web UI stays exactly as it is.

UI-led · persistent surface · the app shell

Direction 4 · One place that never moves

Bet: moments get missed; a place can be found twice. A permanent "How to reach Homa" surface in the app: one row per channel with its live status for THIS family (texting linked or not, which email she watches, call), the save-contact button and a QR for the desktop-to-phone hop, and the what-goes-where table (reports by email, research by text) stated plainly. Onboarding and the report just LINK here instead of teaching anything themselves.

Five build-changing questions

1 · The regression: what does the link text carry, starting now?

The 2026-07-10 founder ask sent the vCard on the link text; multi-parent v0 dropped it silently. Restoring is a small change to lib/family-phone.ts. The question is whether to restore verbatim today or hold for this lab's winning copy. This decides whether a fix ships this session.

2 · First inbound text from a linked number: silent context, or a one-time welcome?

Today a linked parent's first-ever text drops straight into the family-context conversation with no acknowledgment that this is new. A welcome beat ("It's really me, you're all set to text with me") needs per-channel intro state in the session or a flag on the phone row, and it must not fire for SMS-first families who already spent a whole signup here.

3 · Where do kids' reports travel?

Reports are email-only today (sendReportEmail). Research briefs are text-first. If texting is Homa's front door, the report link not being textable is the loudest silence. Adding a text send touches the delivery ledger and the report-email dedupe (reportAlreadyEmailed).

4 · Does "how to reach Homa" get a permanent home in the app?

Direction 4 as a question: even if a moment-based direction wins, a findable reference may still be worth a route or card. This changes IA (a nav entry or settings section) and gives every other surface a place to link.

5 · Who writes the introduction copy: code or the agent?

Every proactive email today wraps agent text in a code-controlled shell, and the link text is fully fixed copy. Intro beats could stay fixed (predictable, copy-gated once, testable) or be composed by Homa in-session (warmer, personal, but every send is a fresh copy risk on a surface with no reviewer).
