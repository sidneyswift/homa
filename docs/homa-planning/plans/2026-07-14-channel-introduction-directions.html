Meeting Homa · five directions for teaching the channels

The problem: Homa is one assistant reachable by web chat, text, email, and phone call, and she remembers across all of them. The product teaches this weakly, in three quiet corners, and one surface used to teach it falsely. Most people have only ever chatted with an AI inside a web app, so nothing in their head expects a text back, a replyable email, or a callable number. This lab holds one family constant and varies WHERE and HOW the product teaches the channels. The goal, restated in events the product can actually observe (a vCard save is invisible to us): by Friday of week one, the family has sent Homa at least one text or reply on a proactive channel, and knows what arrives on which surface.

Everything below uses fake fixture data: Maria Rivera and her kids Emma (4) and Mateo (7) are invented, the numbers shown are placeholders, and no real family, lead, or production record was loaded. No product code or route was changed by this file. Fixes named as shipped were separate commits, not edits to this lab.

Fixed in this session (both live and probed): the linked-number contact card regression, commit 91337b9 (the 2026-07-10 founder ask sent the vCard on the link text; multi-parent v0 997bda9 silently dropped it; restored verbatim, sms.contact_card_sent trigger linked fires again). And the false email footer, commit ed768d7: every Homa email's fine print claimed "Reply and a real person answers", which has been false since replies started routing to the agent; it now reads "Reply to this email and Homa answers", the pattern already shipped in the research-brief footer.

Live production failure, found by the council's SMS-first persona walk, verified in code, ESCALATED here because the fix needs a founder ruling: on the tokenized report page, the two action buttons dead-end forever for a text-born family. "Have me call you now" posts to a signed-in-only route (api/voice/curriculum/start returns 401 without a Supabase user, and a text-signup family is memberless with no password), and "Open the month" needs a resume cookie or session the tokenized page never sets (api/map/plan/consent → requireFamily). Both then show "Open your email link on this device once, then try again", which is circular: the report email links straight back to this same sessionless page. User-facing symptom: the SMS-first mom taps the two most action-shaped things on her wow page, gets blamed twice in device-speak, and gives up feeling stupid. The fix is not a silent patch because the report token is shareable by design: honoring it as a credential would let any forwarded-link holder ring the family's phone or build months. Options for the ruling: (a) channel-aware fallback copy ("text me 'call me' and I'll call you") for sessionless viewers, (b) suppress the two buttons when no session exists, (c) accept the token as credential for the month but never for calls, or (d) a one-tap sign-in hint for text-born families ("use the same email you gave me by text"). Until ruled, every text-born family's report page stays partially broken.

What exists today
The pipes are built. The introduction is not.

SMS, email, and voice all route into the one agent with family context (agent/channels/telnyx.ts, agent/channels/email.ts, api/voice/context). The vCard is served at /homa.vcf. Strangers who text first get the card and a concierge. Since 91337b9 account holders get the card on the link text too; what nobody gets is a reason to reply or a map of the doors.

Where the product teaches channels today
Three quiet corners, no deliberate moment.

A form label ("Cell number, so you can text Homa"), a pill buried in Settings ("Text Homa · number"), and the task editor's delivery choices. The landing page, FAQ, and chat home never mention that text, email, or calls exist. The report page mentions one door (the call offer) and the report email mentions replying only to teach deletion; neither TEACHES the channel map. The footer that falsely promised "a real person answers" is fixed as of ed768d7.

The asymmetry to fix
Reports go by email. Research goes by text. Nobody is told why.

Kid reports send email-only (lib/map/email.ts sendReportEmail; the one exception is the agent texting the link during SMS signup). Morning research briefs send text-first with email fallback (lib/conversation-continuity/task-delivery.ts, on by default behind MORNING_BRIEF_ENABLED). The same assistant shows up on two surfaces in her first 24 hours with no thread connecting them.

Bindings and budgets (true whichever direction leads)

From the compliance and deliverability review. MUST items are carrier or TCPA-exposure bindings; the rest are the cheapest capacity and trust moves in the plan.

MUST · consentweb The onboarding form needs the disclosure block The phone field enrolls a required number into a default-ON weekday texting program with zero frequency, rates, or opt-out language at the point of collection (no "msg & data rates" string exists anywhere in the product). Under the label: one block naming what Homa texts, that weekday research texts recur, msg & data rates, STOP/HELP, and links to SMS terms and privacy. This also cures the scope gap ("so you can text Homa" describes her texting Homa, not a recurring program) and doubles as the opt-in evidence campaign registration wants. MUST · confirmationtext The link text becomes a complete program confirmation Whatever copy question 1 picks, the enrollment confirmation must carry, once: recurrence ("I'll also text one research idea on weekday mornings"), msg & data rates, HELP, and STOP. Today it has STOP only.
MUST · registrationtext Audit the 10DLC campaign before adding sends Confirm the Telnyx brand and campaign exist, are vetted, and declare what actually sends (recurring briefs, vCard welcome, MMS worksheets, codes, conversational replies, report links if question 5 ships). MUST · wrong numberstext Gate recurring sends on one engagement signal Signup skips code verification by ruling, so a typo enrolls a stranger into daily 9am texts, the highest-frequency TCPA suit pattern. Cheap fix that keeps the no-OTP posture: conversational replies stay instant, but the scheduled program (briefs, future report texts) waits for any one proof of a real expecting human (a reply, a delivered-unSTOPped welcome plus a few days), and new numbers get checked against the FCC reassigned-numbers database before the first scheduled send.

Also start consuming per-carrier delivery receipts; today the product only knows Telnyx accepted a message, so silent filtering is invisible.
Budget · trusttext One proactive text per day in week one, and clamp the clock Question-first beats, briefs, and report texts stack: three proactive texts in 24 hours to an unsaved number reads as a list blast and collides with "offers never commands". Week-one budget: one per day. And the evening check-in's 3-hour delivery window lets an 8pm send land at 11pm local, past the 9pm quiet-hours line; clamp it before the check-in ever defaults on.

Five build-changing questions (re-cut by the council)

1 · The first text: what speech act is it, and who sends it?

The card is restored (91337b9) with the founder-approved 2026-07-10 wording, but both persona walks filed that announcement shape under "confirmation robot", and both walks learned texting was real only when a text asked a question worth answering. This decides the link text's copy AND whether her-initiated texting (direction 5) becomes the primary path with the auto-send as fallback.

2 · First inbound text from a linked number: silent context, or a one-time welcome?

Today a linked parent's first-ever text drops straight into the family-context conversation with no acknowledgment that this is new. The engineer's audit hardened the cost side: the only state shape that survives is a new channel_intros table (phone-row flags die on unlink/relink, family columns break multi-adult, session state is memory-only), the suppression set spans SMS-first families, invited co-parents, claim-movers, and six link call sites, and a pre-answer beat collides with the one-reply-per-text law unless question 3 settles the transport.

3 · Intro beats and extra sends: inside the one message, or a second ledgered send?

The engineer's missing question, binding for questions 1 and 2 and direction 3. Every proactive path today delivers exactly ONE ledgered message per recipient per moment (one conversation_deliveries attempt, one composed text persisted verbatim, one reply loop), and the inbound side has a one-reply law. The mocks quietly break this three ways: the preamble drawn as a second bubble, the welcome beat before the answer, and a report text that lives in neither existing ledger. Merging into one message instead runs into the 670-UTF-16-unit ceiling and the "delivered EXACTLY as written" contract.

4 · Does "how to reach Homa" get a permanent home in the app?

Even with moment-based teaching, a findable reference gives every other surface a link target, and compliance wants a linkable SMS program terms artifact (program description, frequency, msg & data rates, STOP/HELP). Correction from the audit: Settings lives in the account menu, so "top of Settings" means inside a menu, then the top of that page; a sidebar entry ships to the marketing nav too.

5 · Where do kids' reports travel?

Reports are email-only today; the one exception is the agent texting the link during SMS signup. The council split honestly here. The phone-only persona: email-only reports means unread reports for text-born families, whose inbox is a junk drawer. The PM: premature to add a second proactive text before the first STOP-rate baseline exists, and "email plus sometimes a text" muddies the clean what-arrives-where story. The engineer: a report text lives in neither existing ledger and needs a once-only key per child per channel, plus a decision for the third sender path (the founder console resend).

Settled by the council, not asked

Three former questions closed with clear defaults. Intro copy ownership: fixed code-controlled templates with typed kid-specific slots (name, one interest), gated once; a slot-holding template is the middle path that makes question-first beats possible without per-send copy risk. Link-text wording: whatever question 1 picks goes through the reader gate then the editor gate as normal copy chores, not a founder fork. Branded sender (RCS business messaging, Apple business messages): a later bet. It would dissolve the save-a-contact ceremony on supporting devices, which is exactly why heavy ceremony UI should stay light now, but coverage is partial, Telnyx RBM lead time is unverified, and verified-business chrome may cut against Homa-as-someone. Revisit when the baseline says contact identity is still the blocker.
