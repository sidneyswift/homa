Read this first

Three facts that decide most of it

  • The phone line is not ours. Telnyx runs the whole conversation on Qwen/Qwen3-235B-A22B, and the code comment next to it already names the unlock: "GPT-4o/5.x become available with an OpenAI key."
  • Phone Homa has 4 tools. Text Homa has 62. That gap is why she invented a worksheet on a call in July and why chat has no idea what a caller is talking about (issue #171).
  • OpenAI can hold the phone audio itself over SIP, so we never have to run an audio server. That removes the one piece of this that had no precedent anywhere in the codebase.
Blocks everything

D1. May an OpenAI key exist in this project?

On 2026-07-27 you ruled that Homa authenticates every model call with Vercel OIDC and never a gateway key. The in-app voice path can honor that exactly. The phone path cannot: answering a SIP call is an OpenAI endpoint the Vercel gateway does not proxy, and the phone number has to be addressed to an OpenAI project id.

If this is a no, stop reading at D5. Steps 1 to 3 of the plan still ship and still close #171.
Architecture

D2. Which brain answers the phone?

The realtime model is fast enough to hold a conversation but it is not the same model as text Homa, so there is a real risk of the two drifting apart again. The alternative removes that risk and replaces it with silence on the line.

Architecture

D3. How do tools actually run during a call?

Two mechanisms exist. One needs us to hold a live connection open for the whole call, which Vercel caps at about 13 minutes. The other lets OpenAI call our server over ordinary HTTPS, so nothing long-lived exists on our side.

This is the weakest recommendation in the spec, and it is flagged as such there too. MCP on a phone call with per-call scoped auth is documented but unverified.
Scope and cost

D4. Which model tier?

Rough order of magnitude for a ten minute call, to be replaced with a real metered number from the spike. Today's Telnyx line is cheaper than both.

Product

D5. In the app, may Homa show things while she talks?

On the phone she can only speak. In the app the planner panel, a worksheet card, or a choice card could appear while she is talking. This is the one surface where voice and visual can happen at once.

Taste

D6. What does Homa sound like now?

Today she is Telnyx's "Callie - Encourager." Real families already know that voice. OpenAI recommends marin and cedar; alloy, echo, sage, shimmer, verse, ash, ballad, and coral are also available.

Carried over

D7. When Homa makes something after a call, what happens to it?

These three were already open as D4, D5, and D6 on the voice-materials bridge (#187). They survive this proposal unchanged. Pick any combination.

Privacy, already open

D8. Who in a family can read a call transcript?

This has been open in the ruling register since July. It becomes urgent here because moving calls into the main conversation table forces an answer: that table's rules already decide it for chat. Today, one adult's phone call is readable by another adult in the family, with no ruling behind it.
