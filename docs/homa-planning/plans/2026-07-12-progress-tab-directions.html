What should the Progress tab feel like?

Four genuinely different structures for the signed-in Progress page, all showing the same fake family, so you can judge the shape of the product instead of the paint. Use the toggles to feel each one on a laptop and a phone, and with one kid or the whole family.

Every person, record, and number here is fake. No real family data is loaded. No product code or route was changed.

The core decision: when a mom taps Progress in a stolen five-minute window, what is the FIRST thing the page does for her: read like a letter (report), tell her what changed (front page), show her the whole map (grid), or hand her the next step (desk)?
Core task held constant in every direction: see where Maya stands, notice what changed today, settle the scissors disagreement, and keep her check-in going.
Locked hierarchySubject, then domain, then topic (founder ruling, subject-domain-topic-taxonomy). Every direction keeps it.
Anti-guilt lawsNo red, no "behind", drops never render as down-arrows, siblings never compared on one axis. All four directions obey them.
Agent-led rulingThe agent is the preferred surface and Progress is one live view (agent-led-live-progress). Directions differ in HOW MUCH acting happens here versus in chat.
Data already existsMonths-ahead scores, check-in history, movement, conflicts, and the trajectory chart all exist server-side today. Nothing shown here needs new measurement, only new layout.
