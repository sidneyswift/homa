## The placement decision

From the 2026-07-11 monetization review (2x-edtech-founder pass over
landing, onboarding, report, crons, worksheets):

- **The report is free, forever.** It is the lead magnet, the trust
  artifact, the shareable link, and it costs near zero to serve (the
  scorer is deterministic, no LLM). Sibling reports, retests, deep
  dives, evidence packs, printing, and sharing all stay free.
- **The card comes out at the first tap on a door.** A door is a report
  action that starts the paid product: building a plan, opening the
  month, a curriculum call, the first chat message. The wall gates
  ACTION, never what a family already has: an existing plan or month
  keeps rendering and checking off forever.
- **Card-upfront uncapped trial (ruling T1).** The wall sells "start
  your free month"; Stripe Payment Links own the 30-day trial and the
  first charge. The week-4 retest email lands right before that first
  charge, which is the proof-then-invoice sequencing the retention doc
  already calls the renewal argument.
- This placement overrules PRD FR-2's card-at-onboarding-entry wording,
  which was written for the invite-only cohort before signup went open
  (2026-07-05). Card-before-report on an open funnel would kill the
  magnet and break the site's published "the report is free" promises.

## The four doors

| Door | Surface | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| plan | "Build the 4-week plan" (report subject drawer) | Server: plan page renders the offer instead of building a NEW plan |
| month | "Open {name}'s month" (note from Homa) | Server: /api/map/plan/consent returns 403 walled for a NEW month; client opens the offer |
| call | "have me call you now" (note from Homa) | Server: /api/voice/curriculum/start returns 403 walled, no existing-content exemption |
| chat | First message in /app | Client-side in v0 (see honesty section); the offer opens, her draft stays |

## How it unlocks

Payment Link checkout carries `client_reference_id={familyId}` and
`prefilled_email`. Stripe's after-completion redirect lands on
`/api/billing/confirm?session_id={CHECKOUT_SESSION_ID}`, which resolves
the family from auth context, retrieves the session with the secret
key, verifies `complete` + the client_reference_id matches the
signed-in family, stamps `families.trial_started_at` once, saves
`stripe_customer_id`, emits `billing.trial_started`, and lands on /app.
`families.billing_exempt` is the founder's comp switch (set by SQL).

## Instrumentation (live NOW, flag off)

`report.action_tapped {door, walled, child_id?}` fires on every door
tap starting with this deploy, so the paywall decision gets baseline
tap-through data from the free era. `wall.viewed`, `wall.dismissed`,

computed server-side); registry parity green in check:events.

## What v0 deliberately does not do (Phase D hardening list)

- **Chat gate is client-side.** The eve channel still accepts turns
  from a walled family sent outside the UI. Acceptable at cohort-one
  scale (founder watches usage; SMS/email lanes are exempt anyway);
  channel-level enforcement is the follow-up.
- **No webhooks.** A canceled or failed subscription does not re-lock
  doors; trial_started_at is one-way. Founder ops in the Stripe
  dashboard cover n≤50 (pause/refund by hand, clear the column by SQL
  to re-wall a family). checkout.session.completed +
  customer.subscription.deleted webhooks are the follow-up.
- **SMS stays unwalled** by standing ruling ("never bring up payment
  or pricing over text"). A walled family can still text Homa; the
  founder watches for abuse of that side door at this scale.
- **No pricing page.** Required before the flag flips (FAQ: "pricing
  gets published before anyone pays a cent"). Same deploy as the flip.
- **No plan inference on trial_started** (monthly vs annual needs a
  line-item expand or webhooks; wall.checkout_started carries the
  chosen plan, which covers the funnel question).
- Planner check-offs and existing-material reuse stay open (viewing
  what exists is free by design, not an oversight).
