The founder deep view: four ways to see everything

You asked to trace what families type, what Homa calls and saves, what she injects into every conversation, what materials she makes and from which prompts, and to see the app the way the family sees it. This lab holds one fake family and one morning-after task constant, and varies only the structure of the surface. Pick the direction that fits how your brain wants to work.

Every person, record, metric, and message on this page is fake. No real family data is loaded. No product code or route was changed.
The core task, same in every direction: it is Monday morning. Using the fake Rivera family, find what Dana typed yesterday, what Homa called and saved in response, why the evening check-in failed, and what Dana's app looks like right now. Then decide one improvement you would ship.

Grounding

Four facts from the repo that shape every direction

Chat reading is already ruled Night-manager ruling D-nm-2 grants the founder full raw conversation_messages access and already plans /admin/chats. This lab designs the reader; the permission question is settled.
Homa's inner voice is not written down No table stores tool calls, injected context, or material prompts. Eve's own traces live about 7 days in Vercel and are not queryable from the app. Panels showing these need the capture migration (question 1). The toggle above the directions shows life with and without it.
Memories and materials are orphans family_memories and materials rows carry no session link, so "which chat taught her this" is guesswork today. child_progress_evidence.message_id already models the fix.
No view-as exists anywhere There is no impersonation or preview machinery in the codebase and no ruling about it. "Her screen" would be new ground: read-only, founder-gated, no actions. Question 2 decides how it renders.
