Full description:

Homeschooling asks a lot of you. Homa is an assistant that carries the busy work, so more of your time goes to your kids.

Tell Homa about your family once. After that, Homa plans your week, makes your materials, and remembers what each child is working on.

Homa is $19 a month, with a 7-day free trial for new families. Subscribing and managing your plan happens on our website at thankshoma.com, the same place you already know Homa from. You can cancel there anytime.

PLAN THE WEEK IN A CHAT
Say what you want to cover and Homa lays out the week. Move things around, swap a subject, or ask for a lighter day. The plan changes to fit your day.

PRINTABLES MADE FOR YOUR CHILD
Worksheets, copywork, and practice pages made to fit each child's level and interests. Ask for dinosaurs in the math practice and you will get dinosaurs in the math practice. Print and go.

RECORDS WITHOUT THE SPREADSHEET
Homa logs hours, subjects, and progress notes as you go. When you need a clean record of what you did, it is already there.

WINS GET NOTICED
Homa pays attention to what your kids finish and celebrates it, from first steps to big milestones.

ONE ASSISTANT, NOT TEN APPS
Ask for anything in plain words, planning, printables, or records. If Homa cannot do something, it says so.
