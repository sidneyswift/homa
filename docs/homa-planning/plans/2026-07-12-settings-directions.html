Five calls that change the build

1 · Route shape: does settings stay one page?

One page keeps zero new routes and everything findable by scrolling. Rooms mean new sub-routes (or tab state), a settings rail, and one extra tap before every errand, but clean shelves for billing and notifications later.

2 · Where does a parent's phone number live?

On the parent's row: one people model, add/remove happens inside their editor, and the texting card dissolves. Its own texting section: matches the "Text with Homa" mental model and gives Homa's own number a bigger home, but keeps a whole extra section alive.

3 · Save model for typed fields (name, address)?

Explicit Save is predictable and matches what shipped. Auto-save on blur removes a tap but typed fields saving mid-edit can surprise, and errors need somewhere to land. Pickers (time zone, state) auto-save cleanly either way.

4 · Does "What Homa remembers" stay on settings?

It's the card most likely to grow (it already fetches up to 30). Staying keeps one page; graduating to its own page or a Memories room gives it space for search and grouping later, but adds navigation.

5 · When billing ships, where does it land?

Inside settings argues for building rooms (direction 3) sooner. Its own /app page keeps settings small forever. This is the call that decides whether direction 3 is inevitable or never.
