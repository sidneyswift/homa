Three promises, three pieces of evidence

CaptureSaved on this device

The received words are in local storage. A device failure or eviction can still matter.

AcceptanceReceived by Homa

The server committed the message and any accepted work request. A retry retrieves the same receipt.

ResultThe work is complete

A verified worksheet, plan or action result exists. A friendly acknowledgement does not count as completion.

Founder requirement: the server continuously records incoming and outgoing conversation text. No routine Save, Saving or Saved UI. Only a real recording or delivery problem produces a notice; recovery is automatic where possible. Stopping voice never triggers or waits for routine saving.

What the council changed

Engineering challenges

Separate saving captions from accepting work. Never let a save receipt skip unseen messages. Keep recipients pinned to the content they were actually sent. Preserve old history order during migration.

Parent situations

Keep Stop visible beside Send. Keep unfinished work findable. Automatically deliver queued sends without duplicating accepted work. Preserve reading focus and shared-tablet privacy.

The six parent perspectives are simulations, not interviews. Historical customer feedback informed the review; new customer validation is still required.

Resolve these before building the foundation

What can voice actually recover after the browser dies?

Prove continuous server recording of incoming and outgoing voice text, including partials and corrections, independently of the page. Check observer/relay, replay and final-event behavior on real devices. Browser-only save timers do not meet the requirement. Do not fabricate unavailable audio or create a raw-audio archive.

Can accepted work survive the handoff into Eve?

Kill the process around acceptance and execution. Prove one accepted request and one effect before relying on background completion. A pending promise in one browser is insufficient.

Can old and new clients safely coexist?

Inventory every writer, recipient rule and flag. Prove committed catch-up order, legacy backfill position, privacy and rollback while old clients still have pending requests.

Will parents understand Stop and Not sent?

Observe real parents through interruption and return. Accepted work recovers automatically; queued sends deliver automatically when connected. Unsent drafts remain drafts. Test comprehension, not synthetic satisfaction scores.
