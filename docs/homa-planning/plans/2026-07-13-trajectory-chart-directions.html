Small multiples, no spaghetti ever. Five calls that change the build

1 · What history does the chart plot?

This lab re-scores every moment under today's ruler, which is why the lines are calm and truthful. Shipping that means rescoring past answer sets from the evidence ledger at render time (works from Jul 8 onward). Keeping stored era numbers is cheaper but the chart plots ruler changes as child movement again, and it needs "measuring updated" markers to stay honest.

2 · What counts as a point on the line?

This lab plots every evidence moment: checklists, the music deep dive, a counting chat, two check-ins (six points this week). That makes conversations visibly move the chart, which is the product's magic, but it means the chart changes between reports. Report eras only (three points) keeps the chart a report artifact.

3 · What does the resting view show?

Directions 1, 2 and 4 draw all fourteen lines with the non-movers muted. The alternative collapses steady subjects into one quiet band with a count ("11 steady"), which is calmer still but hides the cluster shape and makes "where's writing?" a two-tap question.

4 · How does a capped number display?

Literacy and music both sit at the age-scaled cap. The scorer already flags it (beyondScale) and the narrative says "the checklist stops here, not the child", but the chart prints a flat "+15" as if that were the measurement. "15+" is one character of honesty. This lab uses 15+ everywhere so you can judge it.

5 · Does the typical zone band ship?

The soft band from at-age to +6 gives the numbers a home: +4 visibly means "right in the typical range", which is reassurance the empty grid never gives. It bends the old "no reference lines" instinct without adding a target. Try the toggle in direction 1 and the expanded cards in direction 3.
