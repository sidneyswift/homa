Recommended content boundary

The Binder is curated, not exhaustive.

A parent should never need to understand Homa's internal file system. The product decides what is safe to hide and gives the parent one clear action when something deserves a permanent place.

Always appears

  • Final worksheets, readers, games, and printables
  • Current curriculum and plans as live views
  • Parent-approved final versions only

Appears when saved

  • Books, links, and outside resources
  • Photos or scans of child work
  • Images Homa made that the parent keeps

Linked, not duplicated

  • Progress report
  • Hours and attendance record
  • Week planner and current curriculum

Never shown

  • Agent scratch files and temporary renders
  • Superseded drafts and failed generations
  • Raw chat history, task logs, and system files

Five calls that change what gets built

Tap your choices.

The recommendations are preselected. Change anything that feels wrong, then copy the assembled brief back into chat.

1 · What should families call this place?

“Binder” feels familiar and useful. “Library” is clearer for digital browsing but less personal.

2 · One Binder or a Binder per child?

One Binder with Homa's existing family and child context avoids duplicate shared files while still giving each child a focused view.

3 · What should the cover show first?

“This week” answers the daily question. “Recent” is easier to build but less helpful.

4 · Where should Binder live?

A sidebar tab makes it a family-wide destination. Hiding it inside each child's page makes shared resources harder to reach.

5 · When should a photo or scan become a Binder item?

Homa currently does not keep inbound child media as files. Explicit saving prevents clutter and avoids silently expanding retention.
