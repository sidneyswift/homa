What makes Quiet authority unmistakably Homa

Typography and color create the level of polish. These four proprietary rules create recognition. They must travel together, or the system falls back into generic premium software.

1One master Homa

Logo, character, app icon, monochrome mark, gaze, and motion states share one approved anatomy. No channel redraws the little home from local primitives.

2One measured composition

A real family object leads, Homa orients toward it, one connector carries attention, and one specific next move ends the scene.

3One coral thread

The line begins as Homa’s roof, becomes an underline or connector, and stops at the useful action. Gold remains a rare highlight, not a second signature.

4One verbal cadence

Show the family detail. Demonstrate that Homa kept the thread. Offer one useful next move. Screen, email, report, and store copy use the same cadence.

Chosen system contract

This approved foundation enters `DESIGN.md`, `docs/homa-design/`, and the typed design contract during the doctrine-first phase. The implementation must preserve the complete system, including its fallbacks and accessibility behavior.

System areaQuiet authority contract
DirectionWarm, capable, and playfully educational. Editorial humanity leads. Functional clarity carries the work. Delight helps the family move.
TypographyEB Garamond for emotionally meaningful headings at 20px and above. DM Sans for UI, body, controls, and data. Fredoka stays inside the real Logo. Exact line breaks, tracking, maximum lines, Dynamic Type, localization, and fallback behavior are specified by role.
ColorTypical screen proportion: 76% mineral neutrals, 16% midnight and ink, 6% coral signature, 2% semantic state. `#A33F22` is an accessible text proxy, never an alternate Logo coral. Semantic colors never decorate.
Signature compositionReal learning object first, Homa second, coral thread third, one useful next move last. Specify scale, crop, placement, density, and CTA position for product, marketing, email, reports, print, store, and social.
Character and markOne master anatomy defines silhouette, roof pitch, corners, eyes, gaze, minimum sizes, monochrome, dark, print, app-icon, and motion states. Success adds an external green cue without recoloring Homa.
Homa threadA fixed coral stroke begins with the house roof and connects context to action. One path per scene, with at most two purposeful turns. It never crosses faces, text, or controls, floats decoratively, or competes with content.
ImageryActual family artifacts and in-progress work lead. Documentary photography uses natural light, visible imperfection, privacy-safe crops, quiet grading, and intentional negative space. Avoid staged school stock tropes.
Shape and elevation12px functional controls, 18px grouped objects, and 28px feature moments. Pills only for compact actions, filters, tags, and status. Rotation only represents a physical artifact. Prefer whitespace and hairlines, with no more than two elevation levels in one viewport.
IconsOne 16, 20, and 24px functional set with fixed stroke, optical alignment, state treatments, and semantic mappings. Homa’s ownability lives around the glyph, not in decorative icon mutations.
Motion and stateThe recognizable sequence is peek, orient, draw the thread, land the next move, become still. Individual transitions run 120 to 320ms and the full one-time sequence stays under 900ms. Idle, listening, working, needs-you, ready, interrupted, offline, and error map to pose, gaze, color, copy, icon, haptic, cancellation, expiry, and Reduced Motion still.
Layout and densityStage, context, and supporting-shelf recipes map to desktop, phone, iPad, email, and print. One dominant object per view. Dense product screens keep the serif rare and controls rigorously functional.
Degradation tiersFull expressive for product and native, static rich for marketing, reports, and social, constrained for email, hosted providers, metadata, monochrome print, and tiny icons. Every tier retains a minimum recognizable Homa bundle.
Accessibility and appearanceContrast pairs, 44px targets, focus, zoom and reflow, Dynamic Type, forced colors, Increased Contrast, Reduce Transparency, Reduced Motion, dark behavior, and non-color meaning are design-system law.
Delivery and governance`@homa/design` becomes canonical with web and native mappings, bundled fonts, email-safe fallbacks, print embedding, asset manifests, visual parity tests, and versioned breaking changes.
