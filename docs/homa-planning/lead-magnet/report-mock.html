How this map is made (and what it is not)

Your answers describe what Maya does day to day. Each one is checked against published national benchmarks for her age, the same sources schools and pediatric milestones draw from. When you said "easily" to something typical of an older child, that showed up here as months ahead. Where you said "not sure", the map says so instead of guessing.

This is a map made from a parent's observations, not a test, a screening, or a diagnosis. Every child grows in their own order, and a first map is an estimate: it sharpens with more observations over time.
