# Socials starter pack design

## Goal

Create an upload-ready Homa social identity pack from the canonical brand assets
already in the repository. No new artwork, photography, or marketing claims will
be generated.

## Visual treatment

- Use the clean, logo-only direction selected by the founder.
- Use the Homa character mark by itself for profile pictures.
- Use the full Homa lockup, centered, for platform banners.
- Provide every profile picture and banner in warm cream and brand navy.
- Add no tagline, decorative pattern, border, shadow, or platform-specific copy.
- Keep every lockup inside each platform's crop-safe center.

## Deliverables

The folder `marketing/assets/socials-starter-pack/` will contain:

- Profile pictures: 1024×1024 PNG, cream and navy.
- YouTube channel banners: 2560×1440 PNG, cream and navy. The lockup stays
  inside a conservative centered 1235×338 all-device safe area.
- X headers: 1500×500 PNG, cream and navy. The lockup stays inside the
  centered area after reserving 60 pixels at the top and bottom for cropping.
- LinkedIn Company Page covers: 4200×700 PNG, cream and navy.
- Full transparent lockups: standard and reversed SVG plus 2400-pixel-wide PNG.
- A short README listing dimensions, upload notes, colors, and file purposes.

## Brand sources

- Character mark: `marketing/assets/brand/homa-mark.svg`
- Standard lockup: `marketing/assets/brand/homa-lockup.svg`
- Reversed lockup: `marketing/assets/brand/homa-lockup-dark.svg`
- Cream: `#FBF7F0`
- Navy: `#23304F`

## Verification

- Confirm every raster file's pixel dimensions.
- Confirm profile pictures and banners are opaque.
- Confirm transparent lockup PNGs retain alpha transparency.
- Confirm YouTube output is no larger than 6 MB.
- Confirm LinkedIn output is no larger than 3 MB.
- Visually inspect both color variants for centering, contrast, and crop safety.
