# Modular play catalog

This is the normalized navigation layer for all 33 plays. Each entry preserves
the source's core definition and explicit "Pair with" links. Open the linked
five-page extraction for the complete guidance, prompts, and visual examples.

## 1. Micro Interactions

Use small, functional animations to confirm actions, reduce uncertainty, and
make the interface feel responsive and alive.

Pair with: Permission Serve, Success Moments, Loading Feedback, Gamified
Progress, and Small Quirk.

Source: [PDF pages 42-46](plays/01-micro-interactions.md).

## 2. Empty States

Turn blank or no-result screens into opportunities to guide, teach, inspire,
and prompt a useful next action.

Pair with: JTBD Copywriting, Setup Defaults, Time to Value, Progressive
Disclosure, and Discovery.

Source: [PDF pages 47-51](plays/02-empty-states.md).

## 3. Success Moments

Reward meaningful effort or progress immediately with a brief, emotionally
appropriate moment that reinforces momentum.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, Loading Feedback, Gamified Progress, Permission
Serve, Referral, Shareability, and Value Replay.

Source: [PDF pages 52-56](plays/03-success-moments.md).

## 4. Loading Feedback

Replace dead waiting states with task-relevant feedback, context, and brand
character so users understand that the product is working.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, Success Moments, Perceived Effort Delay, JTBD
Copywriting, and Small Quirk.

Source: [PDF pages 57-61](plays/04-loading-feedback.md).

## 5. Personalisation

Adapt the interface, content, or interactions to a user's stated identity,
goals, preferences, and observed behavior while preserving control and
privacy.

Pair with: Setup Defaults, Commitment, Discovery, Pattern Alignment, and
Intent Mirroring.

Source: [PDF pages 62-66](plays/05-personalisation.md).

## 6. JTBD Copywriting

Frame interface words around the outcome the user wants, rather than the
feature the product provides.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, Empty States, Loading Feedback, Permission
Serve, and Fail Safe.

Source: [PDF pages 67-71](plays/06-jtbd-copywriting.md).

## 7. Permission Serve

Request system access, such as notifications, camera, microphone, location, or
contacts, at a moment when the user understands the specific value it unlocks.

Pair with: Success Moments, Empty States, Contact Bridge, Setup Defaults, and
JTBD Copywriting.

Source: [PDF pages 72-76](plays/07-permission-serve.md).

## 8. Intentional Friction

Add a purposeful pause or extra effort where it improves clarity, decision
quality, safety, or commitment.

Pair with: Time to Value, Setup Defaults, Progressive Disclosure, Variable
Reward, Fail Safe, and Perceived Effort Delay.

Source: [PDF pages 77-81](plays/08-intentional-friction.md).

## 9. Time to Value

Reduce the gap between a user's first touchpoint and the moment they experience
the core benefit that brought them to the product.

Pair with: Gamified Progress, Intentional Friction, Progressive Disclosure,
Empty States, Setup Defaults, Commitment, and Discovery.

Source: [PDF pages 82-86](plays/09-time-to-value.md).

## 10. Setup Defaults

Give users an editable head start through pre-filled content, smart defaults,
templates, example data, or guided starter flows.

Pair with: Gamified Progress, Intentional Friction, Empty States, Permission
Serve, Progressive Disclosure, and Personalisation.

Source: [PDF pages 87-91](plays/10-setup-defaults.md).

## 11. Progressive Disclosure

Reveal information and capability in stages so each moment contains what the
user needs to act without hiding accessible depth from experienced users.

Pair with: Time to Value, Intentional Friction, Loading Feedback, Investment,
Personalisation, Intent Mirroring, and Momentum Bias.

Source: [PDF pages 92-96](plays/11-progressive-disclosure.md).

## 12. Commitment

Let users declare a goal, schedule, pace, or other intention, then shape future
support around that choice without coercion or shame.

Pair with: Personalisation, Gamified Progress, Time to Value, Deep-link,
System Widget, and Intent Mirroring.

Source: [PDF pages 97-101](plays/12-commitment.md).

## 13. Deep-link

Route users directly into the exact screen, state, or flow promised by an
email, message, share, reminder, or returning action.

Pair with: Time to Value, Commitment, Gamified Progress, Contact Bridge,
Discovery, Limited Offer, and Referral.

Source: [PDF pages 102-106](plays/13-deep-link.md).

## 14. Contact Bridge

Connect a user's existing network to the product, with clear purpose and
permission, so familiar relationships can create immediate social value.

Pair with: Permission Serve, Success Moments, Time to Value, Deep-link, and
Discovery.

Source: [PDF pages 107-111](plays/14-contact-bridge.md).

## 15. System Widget

Surface timely, personal, glanceable product value outside the app through a
home-screen component that supports a recurring user rhythm.

Pair with: Gamified Progress, Commitment, and Deep-link. The source repeats
Gamified Progress in this companion list.

Source: [PDF pages 112-116](plays/15-system-widget.md).

## 16. Value Replay

Resurface a user's progress, actions, or milestones across channels so return
feels like continuation rather than restart.

Pair with: Success Moments, Pattern Alignment, Effort Moat, Investment, and
Deep-link.

Source: [PDF pages 117-121](plays/16-value-replay.md).

## 17. Effort Moat

Reflect time, energy, content, and personal setup back to users so their
investment remains visible, valuable, and difficult to abandon.

Pair with: Success Moments, Gamified Progress, Value Replay, Investment, and
Time to Value.

Source: [PDF pages 122-126](plays/17-effort-moat.md).

## 18. Perceived Effort Delay

Use a brief, clearly framed pause when the result should feel considered,
personal, or significant, without creating unnecessary waiting.

Pair with: Loading Feedback, Intentional Friction, Gamified Progress, and Time
to Value.

Source: [PDF pages 127-131](plays/18-perceived-effort-delay.md).

## 19. Momentum Bias

Show honest progress from the start through pre-filled content, completed
starter steps, or early achievable wins so users feel already in motion.

Pair with: Gamified Progress, Setup Defaults, Progressive Disclosure, and Time
to Value.

Source: [PDF pages 132-136](plays/19-momentum-bias.md).

## 20. Intent Mirroring

Respond to meaningful user behavior, such as hesitation, repetition, or
backtracking, with timely guidance that matches the likely current intent.

Pair with: Personalisation, Pattern Alignment, Progressive Disclosure, and
Time to Value.

Source: [PDF pages 137-141](plays/20-intent-mirroring.md).

## 21. Spark Curiosity

Reveal enough meaningful information to create a solvable knowledge gap, then
provide a clear and truthful path to the payoff.

Pair with: Intentional Friction, The Paywall, Variable Reward, Limited Offer,
and Success Moments.

Source: [PDF pages 142-146](plays/21-spark-curiosity.md).

## 22. Sandbox Experience

Let users experience a guided, limited version of core value before requiring
account creation, setup, or payment.

Pair with: Time to Value, Setup Defaults, Success Moments, Variable Reward,
and Intentional Friction.

Source: [PDF pages 147-151](plays/22-sandbox-experience.md).

## 23. Shareability

Package a real achievement or output so users can share something that
expresses their pride, progress, taste, identity, or skill.

Pair with: Success Moments, Gamified Progress, Referral, Time to Value, and
Effort Moat.

Source: [PDF pages 152-156](plays/23-shareability.md).

## 24. Referral

Build a low-friction invite loop into a moment after value, often with a
relevant benefit for both the existing user and the invited person.

Pair with: Intentional Friction, Success Moments, Variable Reward, Deep-link,
Limited Offer, Discovery, Shareability, and Contact Bridge.

Source: [PDF pages 157-161](plays/24-referral.md).

## 25. Variable Reward

Add a useful amount of unpredictability to a familiar, low-stakes flow so
novelty supports exploration and return without hiding essential functions or
creating purposeless compulsion.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, Intentional Friction, Referral, Spark Curiosity,
and Discovery. The source repeats Intentional Friction and shortens Spark
Curiosity to "Curiosity."

Source: [PDF pages 162-166](plays/25-variable-reward.md).

## 26. Small Quirk

Give a repeated, low-stakes product moment a light and recognizable signature
without sacrificing clarity or platform conventions.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, JTBD Copywriting, Pattern Alignment, and
Intentional Friction.

Source: [PDF pages 167-171](plays/26-small-quirk.md).

## 27. Fail Safe

Protect users from irreversible or high-stakes mistakes through clear risk
language, intentional confirmation, undo, grace periods, drafts, and safe
defaults.

Pair with: Micro Interactions, JTBD Copywriting, and Intentional Friction.

Source: [PDF pages 172-176](plays/27-fail-safe.md).

## 28. Limited Offer

Use a real, time-sensitive incentive at a meaningful hesitation or churn
moment without fake scarcity or repeated pressure.

Pair with: The Paywall, Referral, Deep-link, Spark Curiosity, and Time to
Value.

Source: [PDF pages 177-181](plays/28-limited-offer.md).

## 29. Discovery

Surface relevant possibilities, features, or next actions before users know
what to search for, while keeping the choice set calm and useful.

Pair with: Empty States, Contact Bridge, Spark Curiosity, Time to Value,
Referral, and The Paywall.

Source: [PDF pages 182-186](plays/29-discovery.md).

## 30. The Paywall

Present an upgrade after users understand value or have meaningful momentum,
framing payment as a clear continuation rather than a ransom.

Pair with: Investment, Success Moments, Spark Curiosity, Time to Value,
Limited Offer, and Discovery.

Source: [PDF pages 187-191](plays/30-the-paywall.md).

## 31. Investment

Ask for useful input, effort, or decisions that improve future product value,
then keep that contribution visible and connected to later reward.

Pair with: Value Replay, The Paywall, Progressive Disclosure, Time to Value,
and Personalisation.

Source: [PDF pages 192-196](plays/31-investment.md).

## 32. Gamified Progress

Use missions, milestones, and meaningful feedback to make forward movement
visible and support a purposeful repeat loop.

Pair with: Success Moments, Micro Interactions, System Widget, Time to Value,
Commitment, Shareability, and Momentum Bias.

Source: [PDF pages 197-201](plays/32-gamified-progress.md).

## 33. Pattern Alignment

Use familiar interactions, language, and mental models so users can understand
the product quickly and reserve invention for moments where it creates real
value.

Pair with: Loading Feedback, Personalisation, JTBD Copywriting, Progressive
Disclosure, Small Quirk, and Value Replay.

Source: [PDF pages 202-206](plays/33-pattern-alignment.md).
