For the parent
Fractions practice · answer key

Answers, and what to watch for

A wrong answer here is useful, it shows exactly which idea is still growing.

1
The ½ circle has the bigger shaded part.
Watch for: “¼ is bigger because 4 is bigger.” The classic trap. Fold a paper into halves, then quarters, and compare piece sizes.
2
⅝ of the slices are left.
Watch for: answering ⅜, the part she ate. If that happens, reread the question together and underline “left.”
3
¼ < ⅓ < ½
Watch for: sorting by the bottom number alone. The rule that helps: with the same top number, a bigger bottom means smaller pieces.
✦
Not fair, ¾ + ¼ make one whole pizza, but the shares aren’t equal.
Watch for: “fair means everyone gets some.” That’s a conversation starter, not a wrong answer. Ask: would you trade shares?
