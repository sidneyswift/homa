# Homa design skill

Read `readme.md` and the typed contract under `packages/design/src/contract`
before changing a visual surface. Use the generated surface manifest to find
the row, owner issue, target profile, states, and evidence requirements.

## Non-negotiable direction

- Quiet authority: calm, capable, specific, and warm.
- Real family work leads. Homa keeps the thread. One useful next move ends the
  composition.
- EB Garamond is display at 20px or larger. DM Sans is UI, body, labels, and
  data. Fredoka is the real Logo component only.
- Mineral canvas, paper, midnight action, controlled coral signature, and
  semantic state colors use the approved pairs in the contract.
- Controls are 12px, cards 18px, frames 28px. Avoid nested bordered cards and
  default pills.
- Motion is `peek → orient → draw → land → still`, truth-triggered,
  interruptible, under 900ms, and immediately still under Reduced Motion.
- Homa suggests and the parent decides. Never compare siblings or frame a
  child as behind. Never use an em dash.

## Workflow

1. Read the surface row and its upstream issue. Confirm the target profile and
   delivery state.
2. Start from semantic atoms and a generated projection. Do not invent a local
   color, font, radius, icon, Logo geometry, or motion curve.
3. Build the representative object-guide-next-move composition with synthetic
   data. Keep domain behavior in the route owner.
4. Prove every applicable state: rest, loading, empty, error, disabled,
   selected, focus, pressed, interrupted, offline, forced colors, increased
   contrast, large text, zoom, reflow, appearance, and Reduced Motion.
5. Capture the evidence named by the manifest. A source review is not rendered
   proof, and a simulator screenshot is not installed-device proof.
6. Have an independent UX, accessibility, and residue critic review the
   result. Record the largest gap and remediation before rollout.
7. Delete the migrated legacy dependency in the same surface change, or record
   a path-and-hash exception with owner, reason, and expiry.

## Channel reductions

- Full expressive: product and native can use artifact, Logo, thread, Homa
  presence, truthful motion, and one useful next move.
- Static rich: landing, reports, social, and store keep artifact, Logo, thread,
  and next move without live motion.
- Constrained: email, SMS, print, metadata, and providers keep Logo,
  accessible colors, plain language, and next move. They do not invent a
  second system.

## Safety and source law

Use the real `Logo` component or its generated adapter. Do not type a wordmark
or redraw the house and eyes. Do not treat `legacy-baseline.json` as an active
asset source. Do not edit generated files by hand. Run `pnpm check:brand-surfaces`
and the surface-specific gate before describing a row as accepted.
