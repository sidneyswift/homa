# Homa Feedback Log

Bugs, critiques, and feature requests from #homa-feedback, with resolution status.

## Format

Each entry:
- **Date** | **Reported by** | **Type**: bug | design | content | feature-request
- **What they said** (verbatim or close)
- **My assessment**: what's actually wrong
- **Action**: fixed (branch/commit) | iterated (thread) | needs-sid (product decision)
- **Status**: open | fixed | merged | won't-fix
