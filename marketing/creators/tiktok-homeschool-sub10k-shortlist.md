Outreach tracker for the ambassador/UGC program. Source: founder research
dump 2026-07-08 (part 3 of the paste whose parts 1-2 are in
`marketing/raw/2026-07-08-*.md`). Selection criteria used: under 10k
followers, recent homeschool posts, woman/mom-led on-camera content, with
a specific recent video verified per account. The model this feeds: the
ambassador networks in `marketing/swipe/2026-07-08-ai-ugc-formats.md`
(tiny branded accounts, high engagement, organic-discovery feel).

Program guardrails: creators speak in their own words, present honestly
(introduce, never fake-testify: claims rules journeys/07 §6), their kids
never appear on Homa's behalf, and briefs come from the campaign playbook
(`marketing/campaigns/2026-07-tiktok-launch/playbook.md`).

Status values: `not-contacted` → `contacted` → `talking` → `agreed` →
`active` (or `passed`).
