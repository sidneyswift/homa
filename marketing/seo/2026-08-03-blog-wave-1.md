## Shared publication requirements

- The first 100 words answer the query.
- Each article remains useful without Homa.
- Every Homa statement matches the current live feature page and names its
  limitation where the distinction matters.
- Each article links to its feature owner, one live or same-wave sibling, and
  `/onboarding` once at the close.
- The reader gate, rewrite, editor gate, final cold read, and deterministic
  score all pass before approval.
- Publication happens as one approved wave, followed by live checks for every
  canonical, Article and Breadcrumb structured data, sitemap membership,
  rendered tables or lists, and reciprocal links.

## Measurement intent

Record a prepublication baseline for the eight exact queries. After indexation,
review impressions, clicks, onboarding starts, citations, recommendations, and
feature-description accuracy by canonical URL. Improve the canonical owner
instead of creating a word-order duplicate.
