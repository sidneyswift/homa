# ugcmaxxing: the full operational guide

Source: X article by @lucaspatiri_ (article ID 2078211411725975668)
Date ingested: 2026-07-19
Context: Sid shared in #homa-dev for skill creation

2 billion organic views across 62 campaigns, managed 590 creators, and produced
22,504 videos for mobile apps. Zero ad spend. The full operational guide: not
theory, the actual systems, rates, briefs, daily routines, and decisions that
make campaigns work or fail.

## 1. Prerequisites

When to start UGC: need bandwidth and budget. If bootstrapped, allocate $5-10K
for month one creator payouts. Run it yourself. You'll always care more than an
agency.

If you don't have $5K or the time, start with organic (founder-led videos) and
paid ads.

Types: regular (volume-first, 10-50 creators, micro $15-30/video) vs focused
(quality-first, <10 creators, retainers $300-1,500/month).

## 2. The Creative Brief

The single most important document. Bad brief = bad content at scale.

5 required components:
1. Campaign objective: "after watching, viewer should believe ___"
2. Key product messaging (2-4 points, benefits not features)
3. Target audience insights (age, interests, behaviors)
4. Content requirements (platform, length, aspect ratio)
5. Creative inspiration (3-5 reference videos)

Golden rule: guide, don't script. Early briefs were 3-4 pages, polished content
that performed terribly. Now: one page, 2 sentences about app, 1 sentence
audience, 2-4 features, 3-5 reference videos, general script.

## 3. Recruiting Creators

4 vetting checks (fail one = skip):
1. Portfolio fluency (if it looks like a commercial, red flag)
2. Engagement consistency (consistent = skill, spikes = luck)
3. Communication quality (slow now = 10x worse during campaign)
4. Product understanding (can they explain it in one sentence?)

Follower count is irrelevant. Top video (15.6M views, 1.4M likes, 9.17%
engagement) came from a creator with modest following.

## 4. The 20-Minute Call

Every single creator before posting. No exceptions. Even at 590.

6 steps: Introduction (3-5 min) → App overview (3-5 min, ask them to explain
back) → Campaign terms (5 min) → Accounts/warm-up (3 min) → Compensation
(5-7 min, not negotiated) → Next steps (2 min).

## 5. Onboarding

First 15 min after call: register in master sheet, create private iMessage
group, add to general creator chat.

5-item onboarding pack. Account warm-up: 3-5 niche videos before app mention.

## 6. Compensation

Micro: $15-30/video. Established: $300-1,500/month retainer.
Bonus system turns compensation into a game.
Pay-on-time rule: pay on the date promised, not a day later.

## 7. Content Formats

8 proven types. The 15-second formula: hook (0-2s), problem (2-4s), introduce
product (3-7s), demonstrate payoff (7-12s), CTA (12-15s).

0.5-second rule: first 0.5s = 90% of performance. On-screen text, movement,
direct question, surprising stat = works. "Hey guys", slow pan, logo intro = kills.

## 8. Daily Operations

First hour every morning: watch every video from last 24h, specific feedback,
review drafts within 1-2h.

Standardized feedback language: "speed up to 1.1x", "needs a stronger visual
hook", "clean background", "first frame must be compelling".

## 9. Quality Control

Draft scoring (1-5): hook strength, clarity, brand alignment, engagement style,
conversion potential. Score ≤ 2 = send back.

One-round rule: one round, one note, fix and post. At 590 creators, 3 rounds = bottleneck.

## 10. Scaling

Monthly tiering: S (elite, autonomous) → A (top, consistent) → B (reliable) → C (underperform, phase out).

2026 benchmarks: TikTok hook rate >60%, view-through >40%, engagement >5%,
CPV $0.005-0.02. Instagram Reels: hook >50%, view-through >35%, engagement >3%.

Friday loop: top 5 + bottom 5, patterns, update format slots, share findings.

## 11. Campaign Breakdowns

- Cal AI: 250 creators, 250M views/month, $3M+ revenue, 4X ROAS
- Wayk: 50 creators, 90M+ views, 6 countries, Brazil +299% downloads
- Swyped: 595 videos, 18.4M views, $0.25 CPM in 45 days
- Knowt: 24 creators, 7.8M views, CPM dropped 68%
- Spoil.me: 30 creators, 9.3M views, breakout 8.2M single video
- Invoice Fly: 0 to $1.5M MRR, full engine build and handoff
