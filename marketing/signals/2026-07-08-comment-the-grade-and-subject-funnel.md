Verbatim: "Hook: Comment your kid's grade + hardest subject and I'll tell
you what I'd check first." Comment prompts turn TikTok into lead
generation without sounding like lead gen; tutoring and parent-help videos
already use "comment the struggle" CTAs. Each comment seeds a reply video
("for 3rd grade multiplication struggles, check these 3 prerequisite
skills first"), a self-feeding content loop.
