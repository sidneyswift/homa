The hours log is the only homeschool job that can always wait, so it always
does. Then it becomes archaeology: three weeks rebuilt from a wall calendar
and memory.

The calmer way is one habit: one messy sentence, said as it happens. "Forty
minutes on letter sounds, an hour at the museum." Captured live, it beats a
perfect log rebuilt at 9:30pm.
