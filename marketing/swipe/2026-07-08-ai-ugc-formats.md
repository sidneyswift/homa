## The one law over all of it

The AI is a character, not a tool. Every winning category makes the AI
feel like a person with opinions and flaws; the moment the video becomes a
tutorial or demo, engagement drops. Homa's translation is NOT sass (wrong
brand): Homa is the calm consultant whose personality shows in what it
notices and how gently it says it.

## Format 1 · Unhinged AI reaction (language learning)

Anatomy: ask the AI something simple → it roasts, yells, or says something
absurd → creator reacts with shock or tears. Close-up emotional face as
thumbnail, AI with genuine personality, universal pain point (language is
hard, money is tight, school is stressful).

- 6M views, the template: https://www.tiktok.com/@learnvietwithtu/video/7632381724013464852
- 2.8M, the positive flip (AI compliments during GRWM): https://www.tiktok.com/@tooned.ai/video/7630140089263328525
- 3.3M, the "crashout" variant: https://www.tiktok.com/@howtosaynihao/video/7610500791085829397
- 161K at 15% engagement, the "AI cough" (proves transfer beyond language): https://www.tiktok.com/@ellesstudycorner/video/7580017224757415186

Hit vs flop: the 6M hit was native language + relatable pain (broke) +
genuinely unhinged AI; the 877-view flop was a generic vocab mistake with
low energy. The AI must hit a real emotional nerve.

Homa borrow: the emotional-nerve rule and the reaction-face thumbnail.
Homa refuse: meanness, yelling, roast energy. The Homa equivalent of
"unexpected AI" is the AI noticing something true and kind ("that
frustration might be the level, not the kid").

## Format 2 · Competitor frustration (Swerve playbook)

Anatomy: branded micro-creators post 1-2x daily; each video references a
specific competitor frustration, then casually mentions the product. Often
no app interface shown at all. Bios read like fandom, not promotion.

- 564K at 22%, POV frustration: https://www.tiktok.com/@catalina.swerve/video/7623615734702148895
- 384K at 10%, community prompt ("tell me one thing that makes you turn off a character"): https://www.tiktok.com/@sam.swerve/video/7622148969040219413
- 334K at 25%, nostalgia play: https://www.tiktok.com/@zaria.swerve/video/7632441513405943070

Homa borrow: sell the frustration, not the product. Homa's "competitor" is
not a brand, it is the guessing: workbook piles, wrong-level lessons,
grade-level labels. Never punch at a named company or at school moms.

## Format 3 · Accused-of-AI / quiet-advantage skit (productivity)

Anatomy: workplace power dynamic story where an AI tool is the quiet
advantage; flips AI anxiety into a power move.

- 14.6M at 6.5%, the biggest AI UGC video found: https://www.tiktok.com/@wfhgabriela/video/7557863837987245343
- 774K, micromanager story: https://www.tiktok.com/@makela_rowe/video/7619478656439667981
- 698K, exam affirmation + reveal: https://www.tiktok.com/@blairstudycorner/video/7635307687470763272

Noted as the most underexploited format in productivity. Homa fit is weak
today (no workplace dynamic), but the deeper move, turning AI judgment
anxiety into agency, is exactly Homa's "you're the teacher, Homa carries
the bag" posture. Park it.

## Format 4 · Before-after transformation (photo/video tools)

Anatomy: dramatic visual transformation, practical utility or fandom.

- 2.1M, solving a real problem: https://www.tiktok.com/@richi.stuff/video/7624595025774562580
- 649K, fandom photos: https://www.tiktok.com/@isabbunny.aimirror/photo/7629821204169346312
- 282K at 12%, meme enhancement: https://www.tiktok.com/@magicshot.ai/video/7627236433614490893

Homa borrow: the before/after skeleton IS Homa's pillar 3 (old workbook vs
right-level materials, split screen). The transformation is the lesson
fitting, not an image.

## Format 5 · Absurd challenge (companion apps)

Anatomy: challenge the AI with something absurd, watch it struggle; feels
like hanging out with a friend. The AI's personality is the product.

- 2M, count to a million: https://www.tiktok.com/@nadia.alien/video/7541192744773946654

Homa borrow: chat-moment content where Homa's answer feels surprisingly
human and kind. Refuse: absurdist stunt energy (off brand for trust).

## Format 6 · Child proof (reading apps)

Anatomy: the child proves the result in one continuous unfaked shot; the
product is never explained first. Slow enough to watch the learning
happen.

- Child reads in one shot: https://www.tiktok.com/@reading.with.joey/video/7655328868991126815
- "Watch my 5-year-old read this sentence with no help": https://www.tiktok.com/@reading.with.joey/video/7647969355539434766
- Visible teaching builds trust: https://www.tiktok.com/@tionnateaches/video/7650358684773649695

Homa translation (binding): member children never appear in marketing
(claims law journeys/07 §6.7), so Homa's proof is artifacts: the worksheet
with details blurred, the level check on screen, the next-step materials.
"Watch what happened after we changed the level", shown in paper and
screen, never in a child's face or voice.

## Format 7 · Consultative intake (curriculum sellers)

Anatomy: frame recommendations as a diagnostic intake, not a product push;
ask about the family, then narrow.

- https://www.tiktok.com/@thehomeschoolsupplyco/video/7659879502880787743

Homa borrow: this is the closest to product truth. Homa should feel like a
calm curriculum consultant, never a tech tool.

## Format 8 · Lifestyle story with soft product entry (parent tech)

Anatomy: open with the family moment or the fatigue, let the app enter
naturally partway through. Never open on features.

- Pok Pok sponsored mom content: https://www.tiktok.com/@shawnathemom/video/7658406684024425758

Homa borrow: some posts start with mom overwhelm (workbook piles, "what
level do I even teach?") and only reach Homa in the second half.

## Format 9 · Trend response (literacy crisis)

Anatomy: cultural entry point, then pivot to the issue.

- https://www.tiktok.com/@nowthisimpact/video/7657984280215227661
- https://www.tiktok.com/@thealyxgreen/video/7659456127934991630

Homa rule: careful. No shame, no "your kid is behind". Entry line: everyone
is talking about literacy; here is the calm first question at home.

## The distribution model behind the winners

Pingo AI and Swerve AI both run ambassador networks of branded
micro-creators (12+ each, handles include the brand, posting daily), which
creates the feel of organic independent discovery. Tiny accounts with
extraordinary engagement (13-25%) beat big polished accounts. This is the
model behind the outreach list in `marketing/creators/`.
