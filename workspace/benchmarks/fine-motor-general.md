# Fine Motor (General)

> Last researched: 2026-05-12

## Frameworks used

- **CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early." (2022 revision)** — evidence-informed milestones at 75th percentile (most children achieve by age X). Primary reference for stringing, self-dressing, fork use.
- **OT Toolbox Fine Motor Milestones (Colleen Beck, OTR/L)** — comprehensive age-by-age breakdown, frequently cited in pediatric OT. Good granularity for bead sizes, block counts, scissors progression.
- **Growing Hands-On Kids (Heather Greutman, COTA)** — age 3/4/5 checklist based on Beery VMI, Erhardt, and Case-Smith frameworks.
- **Lurie Children's Hospital OT Milestones** — hospital-grade checklist sourced from Beery VMI, Erhardt Developmental Prehension Assessment, Exner (2001), Mulligan (2003).
- **Skill Point Therapy** — clinical summary of ages 3-5 milestones, aligns with above.
- **Montessori Practical Life scope-and-sequence** — for pouring/transferring benchmarks (not covered by CDC/OT checklists). Standard Montessori primary (3-6y) progression.

## Age-band milestones

### Stringing/Lacing

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Strings large beads (1–1.5″); laces a lacing card | Strings small beads; can follow a color pattern while stringing | CDC 3y ("strings items together, like large beads or macaroni"); OT Toolbox 2-3y; Lurie 3y |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Strings small beads consistently; makes necklaces with pattern assistance | Strings small beads in complex patterns independently; threads needle-like tools | OT Toolbox 3-4y ("strings small beads"); Growing Hands-On Kids 3y ("strings ½ inch beads") |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Strings small beads in alternating patterns; laces sewing card independently | Can sew simple running stitch on burlap/card with supervision | OT Toolbox 4-5y ("laces sewing card") |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Sewing cards independently; beginning real lacing (e.g., shoe lacing motions) | Cross-lacing shoes with verbal guidance | OT Toolbox 4-5y |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Laces shoes; can thread small beads rapidly | Ties laces together | OT Toolbox 5-6y ("ties laces together") |

### Buttons/Zippers/Fasteners

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Unbuttons large buttons; unzips zippers; pulls up pants with elastic waist | Buttons large buttons independently; zips + connects zipper | OT Toolbox 3-4y; Lurie 3y ("unzips zippers and unbuttons large buttons"); CDC 3y ("puts on some clothes by himself") |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Begins to manipulate large buttons (button + unbutton); zips with assistance for starting | Buttons small buttons; manages snaps independently | OT Toolbox 3-4y ("snaps clothing, zips with assistance, begins to manipulate large buttons") |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Buttons clothes independently; opens all fasteners | Manages all fasteners including hooks/buckles | Growing Hands-On Kids 4y ("gets dressed and undressed without help"); OT Toolbox 4-5y ("buttons clothes," "opens all fasteners") |
| 4y6m–5y0m | All fasteners independently including snaps, hooks | Beginning to manage belt buckles | OT Toolbox 4-5y; Lurie 4-5y |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Independent with all dressing fasteners; learning shoe tying | Ties shoes independently | Lurie 5-6y ("independent with all dressing, including shoe tying"); OT Toolbox 5-6y |

### Pouring/Transferring

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Pours from small pitcher to cup with some spilling; transfers dry goods (rice/beans) between containers with scoops | Pours water pitcher-to-pitcher with minimal spilling; uses tongs/tweezers to transfer objects | Montessori primary sequence (dry pouring → wet pouring at 2.5-3y); OT Toolbox 2-3y ("self feeds with utensils and open cup, some spillage"); CDC 3y ("uses a fork") |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Pours water pitcher-to-cup with minimal spilling; uses funnels and ladles | Pours to a marked line; uses eyedropper/turkey baster for transferring | Montessori primary (wet pouring precision by 3.5-4y); OT Toolbox 3-4y ("self feeds with utensils and open cup, little spillage") |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Pours to a line; pours for others at table; scoops and transfers with precision | Measures and pours for cooking recipes (½ cup, 1 cup) with verbal guidance | Montessori primary (pouring to line, spooning, sieving 4-5y); Skill Point 4y ("self-feeding with minimal spillage") |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Measures and pours independently; uses kitchen tools (whisk, spatula) with control | Can carry a tray with a glass of water without spilling | Montessori primary (tray carrying, kitchen prep 4.5-5y) |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Independent in pouring, measuring, and kitchen prep tasks | Can pour thin liquids (oil, vinegar) with precision | Montessori primary (advanced practical life 5-6y) |

### Tower Building/Block Construction

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Stacks 6-8 blocks; begins to follow simple block designs (train, bridge) | Stacks 9-10 blocks; copies block designs (bridge, wall, enclosure) | OT Toolbox 2-3y ("stacks 6-8 block tower, begins to follow block designs"); Lurie 2-3y ("stacks 2-6 block tower" at 12-24m, much higher by 3y) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Stacks 9-10 blocks; copies block designs (train, bridge, wall) | Imitates 4-6 part block designs; creates own designs | OT Toolbox 3-4y ("stack 9-10 block tower, begins to copy block designs") |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Imitates 4-6 part block designs from a model | Creates complex multi-step designs independently | OT Toolbox 4-5y ("imitates 4-6 part block designs") |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Copies complex block designs; builds with intention/planning | Builds complex structures with Legos, Tinker Toys | OT Toolbox 5-6y (emerging) |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Builds complex designs with Legos, Tinker Toys, etc. | Creates representational structures (houses, vehicles) | OT Toolbox 5-6y ("builds complex designs with Legos, Tinker toys, etc.") |

### Puzzles

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Completes simple form puzzles (circle, square, triangle); 4-6 piece interlocking puzzles | 8-12 piece puzzles; matches more complex shapes | OT Toolbox 2-3y; Growing Hands-On Kids 3y; CHR 4y ("able to do simple puzzles 6-10 pieces") |
| 3y6m–4y0m | 6-10 piece interlocking puzzles | 12-20 piece puzzles; beginning jigsaw strategies (edge pieces first) | CHR 4y; Growing Hands-On Kids 4y ("completes puzzles of 4-5 pieces" seems low — cross-referencing with OT Toolbox and standard OT expectations: 6-10 pieces by 4y is more consensus) |
| 4y0m–4y6m | 12-20 piece puzzles | 24-48 piece puzzles; uses strategy | Standard OT expectations (Case-Smith) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | 24-piece puzzles independently | 48+ piece puzzles | Standard OT progression |
| 5y0m–5y6m | 24-48 piece puzzles; jigsaw strategies well established | 50-100 piece puzzles | Standard OT progression |

## Recent research

- [OT Toolbox Fine Motor Milestones](https://www.theottoolbox.com/fine-motor-milestones/) — Comprehensive birth-6y checklist by Colleen Beck, OTR/L. Key reference for block/bead/scissors/drawing progressions.
- [CHR Fine Motor Skills 2-5y](https://www.chrichmond.org/services/therapy-services/developmental-milestones/fine-motor-skills-2-to-5-years/) — Children's Hospital of Richmond OT-authored checklist. Concise age breakdown.
- [Growing Hands-On Kids Preschool Checklist](https://www.growinghandsonkids.com/fine-motor-skills-checklist-preschoolers.html) — COTA-authored 3/4/5y checklist based on Beery VMI, Erhardt, Case-Smith frameworks.
- [Lurie Children's Fine Motor Milestones](https://www.luriechildrens.org/en/specialties-conditions/pediatric-occupational-therapy/developmental-milestones/fine-motor-skills/) — Hospital-grade checklist citing Beery VMI, Erhardt, Exner (2001), Mulligan (2003).
- [Skill Point Therapy Ages 3-5](https://www.skillpointtherapy.com/early-fine-motor-milestones/) — Clinical summary with clear age-3/4/5 breakdown, 1-in-10 delay stat.
- [CDC Milestones by 3 Years](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/3-years.html) — 2022 revision, evidence-informed at 75th percentile. Fine motor: strings items, uses fork, puts on some clothes.
- [Montessori Practical Life Pouring](https://theglobalmontessorinetwork.org/resource/primary/pouring-wet-english/) — Global Montessori Network. Dry→wet pouring sequence for 3-6y.
