## Frameworks used

1. **Head Start ELOF (Early Learning Outcomes Framework)** — gold standard for US preschool math milestones. Covers 36–60 months with detailed progression indicators across counting & cardinality, operations, patterns, measurement, and geometry. Chosen because it's federally backed, evidence-based, and maps cleanly to our subdomain structure.
2. **CDC "Learn the Signs. Act Early"** — developmental milestone checklists (75th percentile). Used as a floor reference: what *most* children can do by a given age. Good for flagging true delays.
3. **Common Core K (CC-K)** — Kindergarten entry expectations. Used as the "12mo ahead" target for a 3.5–4.5-year-old. If your child meets CC-K standards, he's solidly 12mo+ ahead.
4. **Understood.org / Funexpected synthesis** — practitioner-friendly summaries that cross-reference multiple research traditions. Used for sanity-checking age bands and filling gaps between clinical/academic sources.

**Not used (and why):**
- Singapore Math PKA scope & sequence: your child's *curriculum*, not a *benchmark*. We score against external standards, not the textbook he's following — otherwise we'd be grading the curriculum, not the child.
- Montessori scope: useful for pedagogy (how to teach), less useful for age-normed milestones (what's typical when). Referenced in practice/ files instead.

## Age-band milestones

### Rote Counting

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Says some number words in sequence up to 10; may skip numbers | Counts to 10 reliably without skips | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 1 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Counts to 10 reliably; understands counting words are separate | Counts to 15–20 in sequence | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 1; Funexpected |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Counts verbally to at least 20 by ones | Counts to 30+ | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 1 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Counts to 20+ reliably | Counts to 50; begins skip-counting by 10s | Head Start ELOF; CC-K.CC.1 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Counts to 100 by ones and by tens (CC-K entry) | Counts to 100+ by 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s | CC-K.CC.1 |

### Cardinality (counting objects / "how many?")

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Begins 1-to-1 correspondence with small groups in a line; may not yet understand last-number = total | Reliably counts 5 objects with 1:1 correspondence | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 3 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Points to objects while counting small groups (up to 5–6); beginning to understand cardinality | Counts up to 10 objects accurately; answers "how many?" | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 3 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Counts and answers "how many?" for ~10 objects; 1:1 up to 10+ | Counts 15+ objects accurately; understands each successive number is one more | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 3 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Accurately counts ~10 objects including scattered arrangements (up to 5 scattered) | Counts 20 objects in any arrangement | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 3 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Counts objects to at least 20; answers "how many?" reliably for 10+ objects | Counts 30+ and understands conservation of number | CC-K.CC.4–5 |

### Number Recognition (written numerals)

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Begins to understand numerals represent quantity; may recognize 1–2 numerals | Recognizes numerals 1–5 | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 5 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Recognizes some numerals (1–5 common) | Recognizes and names numerals 1–10 | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 5; Understood.org |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Associates quantity with written numerals 0–5; may write some | Recognizes all numerals 0–10; beginning to write them | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 5 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Recognizes and with support writes numerals up to 10 | Recognizes numerals to 20; writes 0–10 independently | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 5; CDC 5y |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Names numerals 1–5+ when pointed to (CDC 5y); writes some | Recognizes and writes numerals 0–20 | CDC 5y milestones; CC-K.CC.3 |

### Subitizing (instant quantity recognition without counting)

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Developing — begins to recognize 1–2 objects instantly | Subitizes up to 3 reliably | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 2 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Subitizes 1–3 objects | Subitizes up to 4 | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 2 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Quickly recognizes up to 4 objects without counting | Subitizes up to 5; beginning conceptual subitizing (3+2) | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 2 (48–60mo); Funexpected |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Instantly recognizes up to 5 objects (perceptual subitizing) | Subitizes 5+ via conceptual grouping (seeing 6 as 3+3) | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 2 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Subitizes up to 5 reliably; beginning to see groups within groups | Conceptual subitizing to 8–10 via structured arrangements | Research (Clements & Sarama learning trajectories) |

### Patterns

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Recognizes simple repeating patterns with help (AB) | Duplicates AB patterns independently | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 7 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Recognizes and fills in missing element of AB pattern; duplicates ABABAB | Extends AB patterns; begins ABB or ABC patterns | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 7 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Creates, extends, and duplicates simple repeating patterns (objects, sounds, movements) | Creates AAB, ABB, ABC patterns; identifies core unit | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 7 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Identifies core unit of repeating patterns; duplicates in new medium | Creates growing patterns (1, 2, 3…); translates patterns across media | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 7 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Extends and creates patterns in multiple forms | Recognizes number patterns (skip counting, odds/evens) | CC-K.OA precursors |

### Shapes & Geometry

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Recognizes and names circle, square, triangle (typical orientations) | Names rectangle, oval; recognizes shapes in real world | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 9 (36–48mo); Understood.org |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Recognizes basic shapes in environment; beginning to describe attributes (sides, corners) | Distinguishes similar shapes (square vs rectangle); puzzles involving rotation | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 9 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Names and describes shapes by attributes (# sides, # corners); composes shapes (two triangles → square) | Names 3D shapes (cube, sphere, cone, cylinder); composes complex shapes | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 9 (48–60mo); Funexpected |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Identifies shapes in 3D world; uses positional language (in, on, under, beside) | Creates and describes 3D constructions; understands symmetry | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 9–10 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Names 2D and basic 3D shapes; describes and compares | Identifies shapes from different perspectives; spatial reasoning with maps | CC-K.G.1–3 |

### Measurement

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Begins to understand attributes can be compared (tall/short, big/small) | Uses comparative language (bigger, smaller); lines things up to compare | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 8 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Compares two objects by size or length with support | Orders 3 objects by size; uses non-standard measurement (hand spans, blocks) | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 8 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Measures using same unit (snap cubes to measure height); compares up to 5 objects | Uses tools (ruler with help); seriation of 5+ objects | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 8 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Uses comparative language (shortest, heavier, biggest); orders by measurable attribute | Understands need for standard units; beginning weight/capacity concepts | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 8 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Compares and orders objects by length, height, weight | Measures with ruler; compares using numbers ("this is 5 cubes, that is 3") | CC-K.MD.1–2 |

### Addition Concepts (pre-operations)

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Understands "more" concept; beginning to add/subtract very small collections (1+1, 2+1) with adult support and physical objects | Solves "1 more" and "1 less" problems to 5 with objects | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 6 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Adds and subtracts with very small groups (up to 3) using objects/fingers | Adds within 5 using objects; beginning subtraction within 5 | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 6 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Solves addition by joining objects, subtraction by separating; uses fingers and manipulatives | Adds/subtracts within 5 fluently; beginning to "count on" from larger number | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 6 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Represents addition/subtraction with fingers, objects, drawings; solves word problems within 5 | Adds/subtracts within 10; uses counting-on strategy | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 6 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Fluently adds and subtracts within 5; represents with equations | Adds/subtracts within 10 fluently; beginning to decompose numbers | CC-K.OA.1–5 |

### Comparison (number comparison)

| Age band | Typical | Advanced (12mo ahead) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3y0m–3y6m | Compares two small groups (same/different number) with help; identifies "more" | Compares groups of 3–5 and identifies which has more | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 4 (36–48mo) |
| 3y6m–4y0m | Identifies first/second in sequence; compares groups of same-size objects | Compares groups of up to 5 even when object sizes differ | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 4 |
| 4y0m–4y6m | Compares groups up to 5 (more/less/same) regardless of object size; uses ordinal position (first–fifth) | Compares groups to 10; uses ordinal to 10th | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 4 (48–60mo) |
| 4y6m–5y0m | Uses ordinal first–tenth; identifies more/less/same for groups up to 10 | Compares written numerals ("which number is bigger: 7 or 4?") | Head Start ELOF P-MATH 4 |
| 5y0m–5y6m | Compares two numbers 1–10 using "greater than / less than" | Compares numbers to 20; uses >, < symbols with support | CC-K.CC.6–7 |

## Recent research

- [Head Start ELOF — Math Preschool](https://headstart.gov/school-readiness/article/math-preschool) — Most detailed US federal standard for 36–60mo math. Eight goals across counting, cardinality, operations, patterns, measurement, geometry. Gold reference.
- [CDC Milestones by 5 Years](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/5-years.html) — Counts to 10, names numbers 1–5 by age 5 (75th percentile floor). your child likely already exceeds this.
- [Understood.org — Math Skills at Different Ages](https://www.understood.org/en/articles/math-skills-what-to-expect-at-different-ages) — Practitioner-friendly overview. Ages 3–4: count to 20, understand numerals stand for numbers, sort by color/shape/size, spatial puzzles.
- [Funexpected — Math Milestones Ages 3–7](https://funexpectedapps.com/en/blog-posts/math-learning-milestones-ages-3-to-7-explained) — Good synthesis. Highlights subitizing as critical at 3–4, 1:1 correspondence, ordinal number concepts. References research showing early math predicts academic success better than reading.
- [IES Practice Guide — Teaching Math to Young Children](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/practiceguide/early_math_pg_111313.pdf) — Federal evidence-based recommendations for ages 3–6. Five key recommendations including dedicated math time and progress monitoring. (PDF — full text not extractable but cited for authority.)
