# HWT Capital Letter Stroke Reference

How to draw each capital letter for the trace guides. Each letter lists its strokes in order, with coordinates relative to a unit box where (0,0) is bottom-left and (1,1) is top-right.

The smiley starting point goes at the START of stroke 1.

## Letters

### A
1. Diagonal from top-center (0.5, 1.0) down-left to (0.0, 0.0)
2. Diagonal from top-center (0.5, 1.0) down-right to (1.0, 0.0)
3. Horizontal at mid (0.2, 0.5) to (0.8, 0.5)
- Smiley: top-center

### B
1. Vertical down: (0.15, 1.0) to (0.15, 0.0)
2. Top bump: (0.15, 1.0) curve right to (0.85, 0.75) back to (0.15, 0.5)
3. Bottom bump: (0.15, 0.5) curve right to (0.9, 0.25) back to (0.15, 0.0)
- Smiley: top-left

### C
1. Curve from top-right (0.85, 0.85) around left to bottom-right (0.85, 0.15)
- Smiley: top-right (start of curve)

### D
1. Vertical down: (0.15, 1.0) to (0.15, 0.0)
2. Curve from (0.15, 1.0) right and around to (0.15, 0.0)
- Smiley: top-left

### E
