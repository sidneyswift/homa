# Decodable Reader Phonics Levels

## Level 1: CVC Words
- Pattern: consonant-vowel-consonant (cat, dog, run, big, hat, mud)
- All short vowels: a, e, i, o, u
- the child can sound these out by blending: c-a-t → cat
- Example sentence: "The cat sat on a mat."

## Level 2: CVC + Initial Blends
- Adds: bl, br, cl, cr, dr, fl, fr, gl, gr, pl, pr, sc, sk, sl, sm, sn, sp, st, sw, tr
- Example words: stop, trip, glad, spot, swim, frog
- Example sentence: "The frog can swim to the spot."

## Level 3: CVC + Blends + Final Blends
- Adds: -nd, -nk, -nt, -mp, -ft, -lt, -lk, -sk, -st
- Example words: jump, fast, land, milk, tent
- Example sentence: "He can jump and land in the sand."

## Level 4: Digraphs
- Adds: sh, ch, th, wh, ck
- Example words: ship, chat, this, when, duck
- Example sentence: "The ship went past the big dock."

## Level 5: Long Vowels (CVCe)
- Adds: a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e (magic e)
- Example words: cake, bike, home, cute
- Example sentence: "He rode his bike to the lake."

## Level 6: Vowel Teams
- Adds: ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, ow, oo, ou
- Example words: rain, play, tree, boat, moon
- Example sentence: "The boat went out on the sea."

## Progression Notes
- the child is currently at Level 1 (CVC confirmed, blending confirmed)
- Move to next level only when the parent confirms mastery
- Each level includes ALL previous levels' patterns
- Keep sentences short (5-8 words per line)
- 1-3 lines per page, 8-10 story pages per book
