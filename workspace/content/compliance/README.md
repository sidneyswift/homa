# content/compliance/

STATE-REPORTING capture for the child (many jurisdictions require a Notice of Intent + portfolio +
attendance once the child hits compulsory age). **The reporting *portfolio* is dormant** until then — but
the **daily-log capture starts NOW**, here, so there's no impossible back-fill later.

- `{YYYY-MM-DD}.md` — the daily "what we did" log, appended by `homa-capture-journal`. This is the
  state-report substrate. **It lives here, not in the parent's Obsidian binder** — she explicitly doesn't
  want daily logs cluttering her view — but they're needed for the submission and the keepsake she said
  she'd love to look through with the child.
- `<school-year>/` — `homa-system-reflect` materializes the actual reporting portfolio when
  `children/<child>.json` age crosses the compulsory threshold, assembled by pulling these daily logs +
  `curriculum/` progress + work samples + `fun/` projects that count. Assembled, not re-collected.
