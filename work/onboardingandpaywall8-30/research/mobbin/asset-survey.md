# Mobbin visual-asset survey: onboarding & paywall screens

Method: two `search_flows` queries against the Mobbin MCP (iOS), each returning
evenly-spaced preview images per flow. This is a view-and-describe pass, 
Mobbin gives inline preview images and a `mobbin_url` per flow, never a
downloadable file, so nothing here is a saved asset.

- Query A: "onboarding with illustrated hero screens and character mascot" (8 flows, limit 8)
- Query B: "paywall with testimonials and social proof imagery" (8 flows, limit 8)

Total surveyed: **15 flows, 57 screen previews** (some screens recur across
flows returned by both queries, e.g. Mindvalley's story screen).

For each flow below: screen position in the flow → asset type → style → one-line description.

## Recurring asset types across everything surveyed

1. **Brand character/mascot illustration (flat vector)**: the dominant asset in onboarding specifically: Finch (bird), BitePal (raccoon), Life Reset (pixel character), Gentler Streak (Yorhart), Ahead (ghost), Alma (leaf icon). Almost always reused across multiple screens of the same flow rather than a one-off hero image, and almost always simple, rounded, 2-3-color flat vector work.
2. **Photo-real imagery (editorial or UGC)**: the dominant asset in paywalls/social-proof screens: Citizen, Mindvalley, Instagram, Apple News, adidas, Any Distance, plus Glovo's and Pi's onboarding heroes. Splits into two flavors: styled editorial/lifestyle photography (Pi, Glovo, Any Distance) vs. authentic-looking UGC/portrait photography presented as proof (Citizen, Mindvalley, adidas, Instagram).
3. **Testimonial/review card paired with a photo-real portrait**: Citizen and Mindvalley build the entire social-proof moment around one large photo plus a pull-quote; BitePal and Alma instead compress this into a small rating badge (stars + review count) sitting beside a mascot.
4. **Icon sets / checklists**: nearly universal on paywalls regardless of illustration richness: simple line-icon or checkmark lists of included features (Alma, Any Distance, bless., Instagram, Apple News+), usually the one constant even when the hero art style changes completely.
5. **Charts / stat visualizations**: a smaller but notable bucket: Life Reset's colored stat bars, Gentler Streak's hand-drawn-style line chart, Alma's circular score ring. Used specifically to dramatize a "starting state" or progress metric rather than as pure decoration.

Other patterns worth flagging: plain **empty/text-only** screens (phone/OTP entry, settings lists) persist even inside otherwise illustration-heavy flows, form-filling steps stay unstyled almost everywhere. **Badges** (subscriber crown, Subscriber Edition stamp, glowing app-icon logomark) appear specifically at the moment of confirming/celebrating a subscription. Life Reset's retro pixel-art game render was the one clear outlier style, no other flow surveyed used a game-render or 3D-render aesthetic this deliberately (Any Distance's small 3D avatar was the only other 3D touch, and it was minor).
