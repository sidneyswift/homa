# Calm: visual asset survey (welcome + onboarding + paywall)

Source: Appllama MCP, app_id `571800810`. Calm is the highest-revenue result for "meditation mindfulness sleep" ($3M/mo, 100K downloads, 4.77★ across 1.97M ratings, launched 2013). This is an asset survey, not a full journey study: all 10 screens across Calm's Welcome Screen (3), Onboarding (3), and Paywall (4) flows were downloaded and viewed, cataloging what visual asset each screen leans on and its art style, in preparation for judging what asset types (illustration, photo, icon, chart, etc.) actually carry a top-grossing wellness app's first-run experience.

## Recurring asset types

- **Photo-real lifestyle/nature photography** is the dominant hero asset, used on the splash screen and 3 of 4 paywalls (family-in-bed, celebrity-portrait-in-nebula, and the tilted photo collage). Calm sells the outcome (calm people, calm places) with real photography, not illustration.
- **Pure gradient/typographic screens with zero imagery**: two full welcome-flow screens ("take a deep breath," "Welcome!") and one paywall (Plan Selection) carry no visual asset at all beyond Calm's signature blue gradient and clean sans/serif type.
- **Flat white line-icon set** appears exactly once (the Goals screen): thin-stroke, single-color, no fills or scenes, functional rather than decorative.
- **UI chrome components repeat across every paywall**: rounded white pill CTAs, yellow/green savings tags, checkmark bullet lists, and a renewal-reminder toggle row recur on all 4 paywall screens regardless of which hero asset sits above them.
- Notably absent across the whole set: no brand mascot/character illustration, no chart or data visualization, no testimonial or rating card, and no badge beyond the small pricing "Save %" and "7-Day Free Trial" tags.
