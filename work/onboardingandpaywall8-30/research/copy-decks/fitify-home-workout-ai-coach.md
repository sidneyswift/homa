# Fitify: Home Workout, AI Coach: onboarding + paywall copy deck

Source: Appllama (app_id 1463386686), journey-ordered screen walk. Captured 2026-08-31. Verbatim third-party capture; third-party punctuation preserved exactly as rendered, including dashes.

## Flow overview

- 61 journey screens total: 3 welcome-section screens (positions 1-2 are splash video/wordmark, position 3 is the first interactive screen), 55 onboarding-section screens (position 1 is an unnamed flow montage video), 3 paywall screens. Another 90 screens are post-paywall app tabs, not covered here.
- Account gate: absent. No account creation anywhere in onboarding. The only account affordance is an optional "Already have an account?" + "Log In" on the very first interactive screen (S3). The quiz, plan build, and paywall all run anonymous.
- Paywall position: end of flow, immediately after the final onboarding question (Referral Source, onboarding position 55). Three stacked paywall screens: base paywall, then a 30% countdown offer, then a 50% countdown offer (each reachable after dismissing the prior).
- No dedicated loading/plan-generation screen was captured as a static screen in this walk (the onboarding montage video shows progress loading bars, but no standalone "building your plan" image exists in the screen list).

## Reading

- Second-person coach voice, one job per screen. Question screens are a single 4-6 word sentence ("How old are you?", "What's your goal?") with zero body copy; the app spends words only on interstitials (facts, projections, social proof) and lets an occasional reactive interjection ("Wow, you're hard on yourself! Let's go!") make the quiz feel listened-to across its 50+ steps.
- Price is framed per week everywhere ($1.34, $0.94, $0.67 per week in huge type) with the real billed total in smaller secondary text, and the discount ladder is a three-act sequence: 61% "Save" framing on the base paywall, then countdown-timed "You've been selected for a special deal!" screens at 30% and 50% off the same $69.99 anchor. Renewal terms appear exactly once, on the final 50% screen.
- Persuasion is almost entirely borrowed authority: Harvard Medical School logo plus journal citations on "Did you know?" fact screens, laurel-wreathed "Editor's Choice" and "4.9 ★ 150K+ Ratings" badges, "Over 2 million 5-star ratings", named member testimonials with concrete numbers ("dropped 43 kgs in 1.5 years"), and personalized projections stated as user-cohort fact ("Based on the results of our users, you should be 158 lbs in 20-30 weeks").
- What is omitted: no free trial anywhere, no feature checklist on the paywall (one 4-word benefit headline does all the selling), no account creation gate (login is optional, on screen one only), and the decline affordance is a bare X with the reassurance line "No commitment, cancel anytime" doing the anxiety work instead.
