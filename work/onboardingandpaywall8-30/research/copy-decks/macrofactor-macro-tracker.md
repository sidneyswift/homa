## Reading

- Two registers, cleanly split: SHOUTY condensed all-caps display lines for moments ("DRIVEN BY SCIENCE, FEELS LIKE MAGIC", "PROTECTING YOUR PRIVACY", "SEE FOR YOURSELF", "CHOOSE A PLAN") and calm, complete, first-person-plural sentences for everything explanatory ("We will adjust your calorie targets as needed..."). Questions are short and clinical ("What is your sex?"); explanations are long and mechanistic. The app never hides how a number was computed; the Goal Summary and Macro Summary screens are essentially annotated math.
- Persuasion is credibility-shaped, not urgency-shaped. The banner copy sells the algorithm ("MacroFactor's algorithms take this into account"), the social-proof claim is modest ("helped thousands"), and the paywall omits every classic pressure device: no timer, no discount countdown, no loss-aversion copy, no pre-paywall "93% of users..." stat. The only playful conversion lever is a hidden reward for scrolling ("You found the bottom (and a secret reward)" / "An extra week on us"), which frames the trial extension as a gift the user unlocked.
- Price framing is bare: sticker prices with a per-month equivalent on every card, one struck anchor ($143.88 » $71.99) on the annual plan, and exactly two reassurance lines ("Billed annually after trial", "Cancel in the App Store anytime"). The trial lives in the CTA verb ("Start Your 7-Day Free Trial") rather than in a headline. Roughly 30 words on the entire paywall.
- Notable omissions: no account creation anywhere in the flow (log-in exists only for returning users), no name/email ask, no notification-permission screen in the captured journey, no testimonial on the paywall itself, and the loading moment is 6 words with zero fake-progress theater.
