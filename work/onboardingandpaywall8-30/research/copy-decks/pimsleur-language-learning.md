## Flow overview

- 40 screens total in Appllama's journey map: 4 welcome, 12 onboarding, 2 paywall, 22 other-tabs.
- Account gate: START of onboarding. Create Account is onboarding position 3 of 12, before language selection and before the personalization quiz. The gate is framed as required "for your free lesson."

- Quiz is short: 3 questions (motivation, level, routine) plus a language pick, then a plan summary that replays the quiz answers.

## Reading

- Second person, plain declarative, almost no adjectives. Headlines run 4-7 words ("Continue Your Learning", "Make it a daily habit, your way."), quiz answers 1-5 words. The one flourish is the trademark tagline "Outstanding Understanding™"; everything after the splash is utilitarian. Exclamation marks are reserved for milestone moments ("Your language program is ready!", "Start Speaking!", "7 Days Free!").
- The free lesson is the spine of the funnel: it is the welcome screen's primary CTA, the stated reason to create an account ("Create an account for your free lesson"), and the paywall decline path ("No thanks, I want to try Lesson One for free"). Account creation comes third-screen early, but it is sold as claiming the free lesson rather than joining the product.
- Price is hidden until the final card, then shown with unusual completeness: monthly and annual tiles side by side, the annual tile restated as an effective monthly rate ("$13.75 per mo"), a lifetime single-course alternative one swipe away, an explicit pre-CTA consent sentence, and a 55-word plain-language renewal/cancel paragraph directly under the button. Trial length appears in the CTA itself ("Start 7-day Free Trial").
- What is omitted: no testimonials, ratings, user counts, streak bribes, discount timers, or "science-backed" claims anywhere in the flow; no plan-generation theater (the loading screen is a bare logo); no guilt copy on the declines ("Don't Allow" and "No thanks..." are neutral). Personalization is shallow but honest: the plan summary just replays the user's own three quiz answers, labeled as such.
