Source: Appllama (mcp.appllama.io), app_id 657189652, category health. Captured 2026-08-31. Verbatim third-party capture; third-party punctuation preserved exactly as rendered.

## Flow overview

- 42 screens total in the library walk: 2 welcome (1 video + 1 image), 16 onboarding, 2 paywall, 22 other-tabs.
- Pre-paywall journey is 18 screens (welcome + onboarding), then a 2-step paywall (plan selection, then a 50%-off discount save screen).
- Account gate sits at the START of onboarding: terms consent (position 4) then sign-up (5) then create-account form (6-7), BEFORE the personalization questions (goals, regularity, last period, period length, health conditions). The welcome screen's primary CTA is already "Create account".
- No loading/"building your plan" moment appears anywhere in the walk. Both paywall screens have a top-left X close.

## Reading

- Every ask is paid for in one sentence. Each question screen carries exactly one plain-language justification ("Since cycles can change over time, this helps us customize the app for you", "We can then predict your next period"), and decline affordances are first-class: a full-size "Not sure" button beside Next, a "Prefer not" link on health conditions, X-closable paywalls. The copy treats consent as a feature, not friction.
- The account gate leads with a promise, not a benefit. "You and Clue" + "We promise to keep your data safe, secure and private" fronts three granular, separately-checked consents (terms, privacy, health-data processing) before any sign-up button; the only account value claim is the modest "where all your preferences are stored". Trust language does the conversion work that feature language does in most apps.
- Price is framed as the monthly equivalent of the annual plan: "$3.33 / mo" is the headline number and "12 months • $39.99" is demoted to small text; the discount screen pushes the same move further ("$1.67/month"). Trial mechanics and charge terms sit in full sentences directly under the CTA ("Free trial is for 7 days and then you'll be charged... Renews at $39.99. Cancel anytime.").
- What's omitted: no user counts, no "most popular" badge, no feature-checklist table, no loading/"analyzing" theater, and no name-calling of the free tier. Social proof is one attributed user quote; the entire feature pitch on both paywalls is a single bolded 13-word sentence.
