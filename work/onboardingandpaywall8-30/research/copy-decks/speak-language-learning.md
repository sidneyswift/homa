## Reading

- The welcome carousel argues a thesis, not features: three full-screen declarative sentences that name the user's fear ("Adults can become fluent — even faster than kids."), reveal a mechanism ("Speaking early and often, not memorizing flashcards."), then position the product as the mechanism's delivery ("science into real speaking practice from Day 1"). No screenshots, no bullets, one idea per screen.
- Voice is a confident second-person teacher that turns first-person at the commitment moments: system copy says "you" throughout, but the CTAs flip ownership to the user exactly twice, "Start my free week" and (post-quiz) "Here's what we learned about you." The quiz itself is framed as a conversation with a mascot rather than a form, so even attribution research ("How did you hear about Speak?") reads as small talk.
- Price is buried by intent: the default paywall shows only annual billing with a small per-month equivalent ($6.99, $13.74), leads with social proof ("Join over 5 million users", "4.8 / 5 STARS", "More than 140,000 reviews") and risk removal ("No payment due now. Cancel anytime."), and the true monthly stickers ($17.99, $39.99) exist only inside the "View more plans" sheet. The trial is renamed from "free trial" to "my free week" at the final tap.
- What is omitted: no feature checklist or comparison table on the primary paywall, no countdown or discount theatrics, no skip on the account gate, and no per-plan feature list anywhere in the flow; plan differentiation is carried by one sentence per plan.
