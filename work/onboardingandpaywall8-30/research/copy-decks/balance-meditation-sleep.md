## Reading

- Second person throughout, sentences of 4-11 words, one idea per screen; the app switches to "we" exactly at trust moments ("We'll remind you 2 days before your trial ends", "Try a free year, on us"), so commitments read as promises from a person rather than terms.
- Price is always framed trial-first and per-month: "7 days for free" is the headline, "$3.33 / month" the subhead, and "$39.99 billed yearly after trial" a parenthetical; the monthly plan is actively demarketed with "No free trial included", and every paywall carries an honesty line near the button ("You won’t be charged today.", "Then $69.99. Cancel anytime.").
- The decline path is a four-rung price ladder, not a wall: dismissing or hesitating surfaces $29.99, then $23.99, then a donation-framed free year whose copy discloses unit economics ("It costs us $70 per trial to compensate our team."), a reciprocity play that inverts normal paywall pressure.
- Notable omissions: no testimonials or press logos anywhere (social proof is only aggregate: "10M+ happy users", "4.9 STAR RATING", "Balance has helped 1,051,603 people your age"), no feature tour before questions, no name/email asked until step 16 of 31, and every stress question offers a "Not sure" out.
