## Reading

- Numbers do all the persuading, and every number carries a receipt. The pre-paywall run is a stat parade ("NO.1 INSTALLS", "100M+ plant lovers worldwide", "400,000+ plant species covered", "200+ countries") and the boldest claim footnotes its source and date range ("Source : Sensor Tower." "Statistical time: 2020.01.01-2025.01.01"). Reviews ship unedited, typos and mid-sentence clips included, which reads as authenticity by roughness.
- The paywall's core move is defusing trial anxiety instead of hiding the subscription: the headline hands control to the user ("Design Your Trial"), the first two checklist items are "it's free" and how to cancel, a "Remind me 2 days before trial ends" toggle is offered unprompted, the selected plan card says "No Payment Now", and the full price appears exactly once, in one line directly above a one-word CTA ("7 days free, then just $39.99/yr(~$3.33/mo)").
- Voice is plain second person in short declaratives; content screens run 11-12 words total (a stat headline plus one proof sentence), and CTAs are one to four words ("Continue", "Let Me Try", "Get Pro"). The comparison paywall barely uses sentences at all: 2-3 word feature names with lock vs check icons carrying the argument.
- What is omitted is as deliberate as what is said: no account creation, no email ask, no personalization quiz, no urgency timers, and no fake scarcity. Declines stay quiet but real (plain-text "Cancel" top-right, X closes, and a secondary "Identify My Plants" that routes around the sample-plant funnel). The post-paywall screen immediately converts the pitch into a guaranteed first success: "We've prepared a plant for you!"
