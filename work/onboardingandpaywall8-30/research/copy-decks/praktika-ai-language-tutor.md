## Reading

- Second person throughout, then a deliberate flip to first person at the commitment moment: "I, Morris, commit to practice speaking every day." The user's own name is injected into headlines twice (greeting, pledge), and quiz answers are spliced into social proof ("3 million A0 Japanese language learners") and the paywall headline ("your personal AI Japanese tutor"), so the paywall reads as the output of the quiz rather than a generic offer.
- Sentences are short and question screens are nearly wordless (a one-line question plus tappable rows, often with no button at all); the flow buys its 43-step length by making each step 3-20 words. Copy-heavy moments are reserved for social proof, which recurs three separate times before the paywall (reviews, laurel stats, 30M full-bleed).
- Price is framed trial-first and per-month: headline terms "1 week free, then $119.99 annually" are stated plainly above the fold, but both plan cards show only per-month figures ($9.99 / $16.66), and the anchor is an external comparison ("50x cheaper than a human tutor") rather than a crossed-out price. The CTA never mentions money ("Start for Free"), and risk is managed with "Cancel anytime" plus an opt-in "Notify me before the trial ends" toggle.
- Notable omissions: no account creation anywhere in the flow (name only), no discount/urgency timer, no "most popular" badge, no lifetime plan, and no monthly plan on the default paywall; the shortest commitment shown is 3 months.
