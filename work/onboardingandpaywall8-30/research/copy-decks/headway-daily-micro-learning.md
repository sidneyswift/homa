## Flow overview

- 35 screens in the onboarding-to-paywall journey (3 welcome-section screens, 29 onboarding screens, 3 paywall screens); 29 more screens cover post-paywall tabs (home discovery etc.), out of scope here.
- Account gate: absent. There is no forced account creation anywhere in onboarding. The only account affordance is an optional secondary button, "I already have an account", on the store-claim screen near the start (onboarding position 5). ## Reading

- Second person, near-zero body copy. Most screens are one bolded headline (5-14 words) with a single blue-highlighted phrase doing the emphasis work, plus a Continue button. Full sentences appear only where they lower anxiety ("You can always change goals when they become irrelevant", "2 taps to start, super easy to cancel"). The two-screen setup/punchline pair ("The most successful people have one thing in common" → "They read. A lot.") is the most copy-crafted moment in the flow.
- Price is framed trial-first and per-month even for annual plans: the primary paywall's only sentence is "Try 7 days for free, then $4.99 per month (billed yearly at $59.99)", with the free phrase highlighted and the yearly total in parentheses. The plan sheet then punishes leaving the default: switching off Annually is shown as losing the "Free trial" row entirely.
- Cancellation anxiety is answered inside the buy button itself ("2 taps to start, super easy to cancel") rather than in a terms paragraph, and trust is delegated to Apple ("Secured with App Store"). Legal copy is compressed to three footer links.
- What is omitted: account creation (no signup at all, just an optional "I already have an account" link), any feature tour of the actual product UI, and any decline button on the paywall besides a quiet back chevron / X. Social proof is external and specific ("#1 most downloaded book summary app" with "SensorTower, Jun 2026, worldwide"; "Enjoyed by 55M+ users") rather than testimonial-heavy on the viewed screens.
