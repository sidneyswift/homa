## Reading

- Problem-question, payoff-exclamation. Content screens run a strict two-beat formula: a 2-word question naming a photo failure ("Awkward face?", "Eyes closed?") with a 3-4 word empathetic quip ("We've all been there.", "Let's fix that timing."), then a payoff screen whose headline exclaims ("Retake got you!", "Your Retake is Ready!"). Almost no screen except the privacy modal exceeds 12 words.
- Second person hands the mic to first person at the money moment. Copy addresses "you" throughout, then the demo-result CTA flips to the user's own voice ("Now My Turn") and the paywall headline answers it ("Now it's your turn!"), making the paywall feel like the continuation of a promise rather than an interruption.
- Price is framed as a toggle the user already flipped. The trial is "enabled" (confetti screen + "Free Trial Enabled" row) before the ask; the primary paywall then shows a dated 0.00 USD → 9.99 USD timeline and a "Try Free" CTA that never says subscribe. The plan screen anchors yearly at "1.35 USD / Week" against weekly 9.99 so the "87% OFF" badge does the selling.
- What is omitted: any account creation, testimonials, ratings, feature checklists, and questions about the user. The only personalization input is picking photos. The one verbose screen is the privacy consent, which names its AI vendors on screen (and ships a duplicated sentence, evidence nobody edits what nobody reads).
