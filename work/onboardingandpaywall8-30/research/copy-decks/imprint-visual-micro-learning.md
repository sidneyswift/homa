## Reading

- The whole quiz is one conversation. Every onboarding question renders as a chat bubble spoken by the logo mark, in second person, one short sentence at a time ("What do you want to focus on?", "Set a daily learning goal."), and the loading moment flips to first person ("Let me create your learning plan…"). The app has a voice before it has a paywall.
- The primary paywall sells the trial mechanics, not the product. "Design Your Trial" spends its words on a Today/Day 5/Day 7 timeline whose middle step is a promise to remind you before charging, and the CTA prices the click at zero ("Redeem 7 Days for $0.00"). Feature bullets are exiled to an optional "Other plan details" sheet.
- Price framing is layered and honest: the full charge terms sit directly above the button in one line ("7 days free, then $74.99/year ($6.25/mo). Billed annually. Cancel anytime."), annual is always re-expressed as a monthly figure, and the save screen leads with the discount percent, then the struck price, then "Only $4.17 per month."
- Restraint is the signature: most screens carry 5-30 visible words, options do the talking, and the few persuasion moments (5M community claim, two bolded review quotes, "That's 75 bite-sized lessons in a month!") are single lines dropped into an otherwise silent flow. Notably absent: urgency timers, "Most popular" tags, and any body paragraph anywhere.
