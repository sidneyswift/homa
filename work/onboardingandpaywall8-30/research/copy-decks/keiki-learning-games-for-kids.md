## Reading

- The whole flow is a first-person conversation with the mascot ("Hi, I'm Didi", "I know what works best for Sun"): questions arrive as typed speech bubbles, not form labels, and the child's name is injected into nearly every screen from the name step onward, including the paywall headline and the account-gate subline.
- Copy is radically short everywhere except the two proof screens: question screens run 10-30 words with one-word or two-word option cards, while Science Proof carries full academic citations (~85 words) — trust is front-loaded into two dense screens so every other screen can stay near-silent.
- Price framing: the highlighted plan leads with "SAVE 50%" and the CTA claims the discount ("Get my 50% OFF!") rather than naming a plan or trial; the anchor "$15.99" appears only struck through under the button, the 6-month plan is normalized to "per week" ($1.49) to make the weekly plan look expensive, and the free trial is labeled "$0.00 pay now".
- What is omitted: no testimonials or star ratings on the paywall, no renewal-terms sentence near the button (only "Cancel anytime. Secured with App Store." plus Terms/Privacy/Restore links), no explicit decline link, and the account gate never says "create an account" — it says "Sign up to track Sun's progress" with CTA "Start tracking".
