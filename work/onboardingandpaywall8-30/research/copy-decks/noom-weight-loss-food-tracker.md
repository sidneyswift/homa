## Flow overview

- 69 screens captured for the app total; the onboarding journey is 38 screens: 3 welcome-section screens, 34 onboarding screens, 1 paywall screen. The rest are in-app tabs (food logging etc.).
- Account gate: at the START. Social/email sign-up is onboarding position 2-4 (before a single quiz question), followed by an account-setup loading moment at position 5.

## Reading

- Voice is second-person editorial serif with one italicized emotional word per headline ("*insights*", "*a habit*", "*every day*", "*Biological Age*"); headlines are 4-8 words, body copy is one or two sentences, and every sensitive question (GLP-1, face scan) carries a one-line "why we ask" plus a Skip. Clinical credibility is done typographically: ® marks on drug names, asterisked claims with dated footnotes.
- The account gate sits at the very start (screen 2), before any quiz value is delivered; the reward for signing up is immediate personalization (your name in the next headline). Most quiz-paywall competitors gate at the end; Noom fronts the commitment and then keeps the 30+ question quiz feeling light with interstitials ("Answer a few questions to get started") and a mascot.
- Price framing is a checkout, not a pitch: "PRICE TODAY: Free 7-day trial" versus "PRICE AFTER TRIAL*: $209/$299", weekly equivalents ($4.06/week, $5.81/week) under the annual stickers, an absolute billing date ("Billed on June 11 unless canceled beforehand"), and a web-vs-app price split that openly discounts the web path 30%. Urgency comes from a "Personalized plan saved: 14:46" countdown rather than discount copy.
- What is omitted is as deliberate as what is present: the paywall has no testimonials, no feature checklist, no "most popular" badge, and no decline link; social proof (3,627,436 people, footnoted) is spent one screen earlier so the paywall itself can stay ~59 words.
