## Flow overview

- 69 screens total: 3 welcome, 36 onboarding, 4 paywall-flow, 26 post-onboarding app tabs.
- 39 screens stand between first open and the primary paywall (welcome 3 + onboarding 36). The onboarding is quiz-dominated: roughly 24 of the 36 onboarding screens are single-question screens.
- Account gate: split. An Apple auth sits on the very first interactive screen ("Continue with Apple" / "Existing Users", with a Skip pill available), then name capture at onboarding 30 and a required email capture at onboarding 34 of 36, framed as saving progress. No password screen appears in the journey.
- Paywall position: end of onboarding. A "growth plan" offer interstitial appears mid-flow at onboarding 17, and the paywall flow proper is a 4-step descent: primary paywall with trial toggle, then 57% discount offer, then exit-intent "Are you sure?", then a personalized gift offer.

## Reading

- The user's own name is the persuasion engine. The name typed at screen 30 is immediately spent twice: a first-person vow the user "signs" by holding a button ("I, Morris, will make the most of tomorrow. I will always remember that I will not live forever."), and a personalized final offer ("We appreciate you, Morris!"). The app writes copy in the user's voice, not just at the user.
- Authority replaces testimonials. The front door is a 4-word credo ("Not magic. Just science."), press logos, Duke provenance, and two stats (96%, 22M). Nowhere in the viewed flow does a named customer speak; the proof is institutional, and diagnosis stays gentle: every weak area is "Could be better", never a failing grade.
- Price is always reframed one unit smaller and the sticker is buried below the fold. "39.99 USD per quarter." is instantly translated to "Meaning only 3.33 USD per week.", and each descent step re-anchors downward (57% off, $1.41 per week as the CTA text itself, $19.99 per year as a "gift"). The free trial is an opt-in toggle defaulted OFF, which lets the primary CTA stay a one-word "Continue".
- Declines are icons, never sentences. There is no "No thanks" text button anywhere in the paywall flow: exits are a small X or "Tap Anywhere to Continue", while the one explicit exit moment is spent on loss aversion ("If you exit now, you will not have access to this offer for the next 12 months."). Sentences are short, second person, and relentlessly warm; the only long text on any screen is Apple-mandated renewal legal.
