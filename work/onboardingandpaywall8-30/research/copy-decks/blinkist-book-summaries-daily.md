## Reading

- Second person, benefit-verb headlines, almost no body copy: "Understand powerful ideas in 15 mins", "Invest in your personal growth while running". One accent word per headline is colored blue (the time claim or the personalization variable), and question screens carry zero or one line of reassurance ("You can always update your answers.") rather than explanations.
- The trial paywall sells the mechanics of the trial, not the product: its entire body is a dated 3-step timeline (start today, email reminder May 25, charge May 27) plus "Cancel anytime." The feature checklist is saved for the fallback discount paywall. Price is framed twice, annual first in microcopy ("7 days free, then $139.99 per year") then re-anchored per week in bold ("Only $2.68 / week"); the discount paywall repeats the per-week reframe on both plan cards with strikethrough anchors.
- What is omitted: any account creation before the paywall (only an optional "Log in"), any custom notification pre-prompt (the raw OS alert fires mid-questionnaire), and any plan choice on the first paywall (single SKU; the two-tier picker appears only on the 25%-off save screen).
- Social proof is a single stat + single long testimonial ("87% of people... made positive changes"; Paul C.'s quote attacks social media as the alternative), and the interest questionnaire is kept honest by a visible step counter ("STEP 1 OF 4") with Skip available on the swipe step, so the flow reads shorter than its 19 captured screens.
