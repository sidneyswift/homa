## Reading

- Two-audience voice, cleanly split: every onboarding question addresses the parent about the child in second person ("How old is your kid?", "What should your kid focus on?"), with a one-sentence "We'll..." benefit-reason under each question; the child never reads a word. The kid-facing surfaces (home, loading, parent gate) run on near-zero text.
- Trust is delegated to badges and licensed IP, not adjectives: kidSAFE COPPA CERTIFIED, kidscreen awards, Oxford University Press, NASA, Blippi, Disney locks. The only superlatives in the whole flow are "#1 interactive app for kids", "irresistible", and "unlimited FUN".
- Price framing is full-disclosure and trial-first: the primary paywall states the entire deal in one sentence ("First week is FREE, then $71.99/year. Cancel anytime.") before a "Try for $0.00" CTA, and the plan screen restates renewal terms ("Recurring billing after your FREE trial") next to a struck-through $215.88 anchor and a $5.99/month reframe of the annual price.
- What is omitted: no testimonials, no ratings/counts, no urgency timers, no "most popular" badge, no loading-screen theater, and the account gate asks for an email only (no password, no card), justified purely by progress updates for the parent.
