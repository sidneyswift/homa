## Reading

- Second person everywhere, first person in the answers: every question is "your" ("What is your native language?", "Set a daily study goal") but level options are written as learner self-talk ("I can talk about a broad range of topics with confidence."), which turns a placement test into a confidence statement. Sentences rarely exceed 8 words; almost every question carries exactly one line of "why we ask" rationale tied to personalization ("This helps AI recognize your voice...", "Lessons will be created to fit your goal").
- Price is deferred, then softened three ways: nothing about money until after the "learning path ready" receipt; the primary paywall leads with "Get 7 Days Full Access for Free", shows the $127.99 sticker only inside the plan card, and pairs it with "No payment today" plus "You will be notified before trial ends, Cancel anytime". The comparison paywall prints per-month math ($10.67 per month) under every annual sticker.
- Urgency is manufactured twice with countdowns: a 5-minute "surprise gift" timer with an explicit loss-aversion footer ("If you close this screen, you'll lose the gift!") and a ~10-minute "Claim this exclusive offer in" flip-clock on the paywall itself.
- Social proof is quantified and stacked, not testimonial: "50 Million learners", "4.9 Based on 50 million reviews", "1.6M+ Ratings from Satisfied Learners", "#1 Rated app in USA and Japan", a "6% of learners are at this level" norm tooltip, and a B2B logo wall ("Used by teams at" Grab/Nestlé/Coca-Cola...) dropped into a loading spinner. Omitted entirely: named testimonials, money-back guarantees, and any feature prose on the primary paywall (benefits are icons; the feature table lives one tap deeper).
