## Flow overview

- 61 screens in the library total; the onboarding + paywall journey is 29 screens: 3 welcome, 24 onboarding, 2 paywall (the remaining 32 are other-tabs product screens).
- Account gate: mid-flow, onboarding positions 18-19 of 24. It lands AFTER the full quiz and the "Calculating your nutritional needs" moment but BEFORE the plan reveal; the gate screen's own copy is the plan-ready announcement ("Your personalized plan is ready / Create an account to begin your progress"). ## Reading

- Second person, therapist-adjacent warmth. The app congratulates before it asks ("You have already taken a big step — deciding to change.", "Great—we've got your starting point."), frames the quiz as personalization rather than data collection (the same helper line "This helps us personalize your daily recommendations" recurs under questions), and euphemizes pace: the fastest slider setting is labeled "Focused commitment", not "aggressive".
- Radical word economy on question screens, density only where it sells. Questions run 25-35 words with one idea per screen and often no CTA (tap advances); the two screens that carry the persuasion load, social proof (~55 words) and the second paywall (~75 words), are 2-3x denser than everything around them.
- Price is framed per-month even for the annual plan ("$4.16 / month" leads, "Only $49.99 first year, billed yearly" is the fine print), always against a struck-through anchor and a "50% off" banner, and the first paywall shows prices before features; the feature-compare table only appears on the second paywall, personalized with the user's first name and a "5x more likely" outcome claim.
- Notably omitted: no free-trial language anywhere, no "science-backed" jargon on question screens (evidence is tucked behind a "Sources of recommendations" link), no guilt or urgency copy, and the account gate carries zero marketing text; it borrows all its motivation from the just-finished plan ("Your personalized plan is ready").
