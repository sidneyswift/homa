Source: Appllama (app_id 1149994032), journey-ordered screen walk. Captured 2026-08-31. Verbatim third-party capture; third-party punctuation (em dashes, curly quotes) preserved exactly as rendered.

## Flow overview

- 20 screens in the library: 3 welcome, 9 onboarding, 2 paywall, 6 other-tabs. Pre-app funnel is 14 screens (welcome → consent → 3-question quiz → loading → account gate → paywall).
- Account gate: END of onboarding, positions 7–9 of the onboarding flow (sign-up landing → email entry → 6-digit code), placed AFTER the quiz and the loading moment, immediately BEFORE the paywall. Gate is closeable (X in the corner) but styled as the mainline path.
- Paywall: final step before the app, a two-screen sequence: a trial-timeline explainer ("How your free trial works") followed by a plan-picker offer ("Ready to go VIP?"). Both carry a top "Already purchased?" restore link and a close X.

## Reading

- Second-person conversational questions, sentence-cased, mostly under 8 words ("What's your email?", "How do you identify?", "What year were you born?"); the one sentence-completion prompt ("I want to use Facetune to...") turns the goals screen into the user speaking, not the app asking. Every quiz screen has a visible Skip, so personalization is framed as optional.
- Price is introduced trial-first and disarmed twice: the explainer screen makes the charge schedule the content (Today / In 5 days / In 7 days, "Cancel anytime before"), prices the CTA at literally "$0.00", and puts full charge terms ($77.99/year, $6.49/month equivalence) immediately above the button; the offer screen then re-anchors yearly against an expensive weekly plan and re-states yearly as "$1.49/Week".
- What is omitted: no testimonials, no star ratings, no "Most popular" badge, no feature-comparison table, no urgency countdown; social proof appears exactly once, as a user count in the account-gate headline ("Join 200 million people already using Facetune!"). The marketing-email opt-in checkbox arrives pre-checked.
- The word budget is deliberately inverted from most flows: the consent screen (biometric Face Data disclosure) is the longest screen in the funnel by far, while the emotional screens (welcome, loading) run 7–8 words. Legal candor is loaded onto one screen so every other screen can stay near-silent.
