## Flow overview

## Reading

- The paywall sells trust, not features. Its headline is the reminder promise ("We'll remind you 2 days before your trial ends"), the biggest text block is cancellation instructions under a "Your Peace of Mind" kicker, and "Cancel anytime in 15 seconds." sits inside the trial timeline itself. Price appears exactly once, in the footnote under the CTA ("7-day free trial, then $59.99/year. Cancel anytime."), with one plan and no picker.
- Scale is the persuasion engine everywhere else: a live counter on the first screen ("582,995 here today"), a market-share donut naming competitors (Headspace, Calm), "Join 36 Million People" as the consent CTA, and "289k Meditations / 25k Meditation Teachers / 1M Playlists" as chips. The app sells the crowd before it asks a single question.
- Voice alternates registers deliberately: prompt cards and answers are warm first/second person with a signature ("Insight Timer Team", "I'm a total beginner"), while benefit screens go clinical-serif with one huge word ("Mood", "Consistency") plus a hedged evidence card ("People report...", "More consistent practices led to..."). Sentences stay under ~12 words; the sole long sentence in the flow is the how-to-cancel paragraph.
- The free promise is structural, not fine print: a whole pre-paywall screen stacks "Free / No ads / Forever" with a "Got it" CTA, so the paywall that follows reads as optional upsell rather than a gate, reinforced by the plain "Skip" link. There is no account gate, no testimonial, no star rating, no "most popular" badge, and no urgency timer anywhere in the flow.
