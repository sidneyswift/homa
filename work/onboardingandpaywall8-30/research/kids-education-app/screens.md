# Lingokids: Games & Shows, onboarding + paywall

Source: Appllama MCP, app_id `1002043426`. Revenue $3M/mo, ~500K downloads, 4.28★ (634K ratings). Category: Education / Entertainment, rank #29 in US Education. Publisher: Monkimun Inc. Chosen as the kid-facing comparison app (subtitle "Everything kids love!", KidSAFE COPPA-certified, ages 2-8 target) because no dedicated kids app surfaced in the first pages of the general Onboarding flow-apps ranking (Toca Boca World appeared but has no Paywall flow); Lingokids is the first Education/Entertainment app in that ranking with a full Welcome → Onboarding → Paywall set and a clear child-facing identity.

This file's mandate is narrower than the sibling folders: catalog the VISUAL ASSET each screen displays and its art STYLE, not adjudicate a pain-point-sequencing thesis. A cross-app synthesis on what reads as distinctly kid-facing follows the tables.

## Cross-app synthesis: what reads as distinctly kid-facing

Four asset patterns recur across all 13 screens, and each contrasts sharply with the adult-facing sibling apps (Flo, MyFitnessPal, Duolingo, PictureThis, Facetune) already cataloged in this research set:

1. **The mascot is the logo, the loader, and the narrator, not a supporting graphic.** The panda character is literally built into the wordmark (welcome splash, home header), reappears as a full illustrated character in three separate onboarding scenes, and even survives into the loading state as a rotated glyph. Adult apps in this set put a plain wordmark on the splash and reserve illustration for data/mockup moments; none give their brand mark a face that persists through loading.
2. **Trust is proven by licensed-brand association and a safety badge, never by testimonials or ratings.** Every adult sibling app leans on star ratings, a doctor/expert photo-testimonial, or a user quote directly on the paywall (Flo puts a 5-star row + quote on paywall screen 1). Lingokids never shows a single star, quote, or reviewer photo anywhere in onboarding or paywall. Its entire proof mechanism is (a) naming recognizable content partners (Blippi, Disney, NASA, Oxford University Press) and (b) displaying a KidSAFE COPPA-Certified compliance badge twice in onboarding. Safety-and-brand-recognition substitutes for efficacy-and-social-proof.
3. **Full-bleed storybook illustration carries every narrative/emotional beat; adult apps use device mockups or photography for the same beats instead.** The "why this app matters" moments (brushing teeth, walking a path, a fridge covered in a kid's drawings) are rendered as flat-vector illustrated scenes, not screenshots of the product or photos of real people, a register no adult app in this set uses even once.
4. **Illustration is switched off, not toned down, for purely transactional screens.** Interest Selection and Plan Selection are completely flat UI with zero illustration: the app doesn't blend styles on functional screens the way some adult apps do; it hard-cuts between "storybook moment" and "plain form/table," which reads as a deliberate two-mode system rather than one continuously-illustrated experience.
