# PictureThis - Plant Identifier: onboarding + paywall

Source: Appllama MCP, app_id `1252497129`. Revenue $12M/mo, 1M downloads, 4.81★ (1.1M ratings), #7 in Education (US). Category: Education / Lifestyle. Publisher: Glority Global Group Ltd.

Read against `article-94-percent-paywall-view-rate.md`, this app's real sequence is closer to the article's **old, discarded flow** than to its winning one. There is no name/gender/usage-frequency capture, no goals or preference multi-select, no four-angle pain-point block, no "personalizing…" fake-progress moment, and no stats-tied-to-a-felt-pain-point pairing. Instead all 12 onboarding screens after the splash are a straight feature tour plus a wall of third-party authority signals (Michigan State University study, Sensor Tower "No.1 installs," Apple/Google editorial badges, a star-rating card stack) that dead-ends directly into the paywall, screen 11 of "onboarding" and screen 1 of "paywall" are pixel-identical, meaning there is effectively no seam between the tour and the offer. This is not a compressed version of the article's identity → pain → proof loop; it is the "pitch deck" pattern the article explicitly rebuilt away from, executed with unusually high production value and zero user-input friction (nothing to type, choose, or answer). It converts anyway at $12M/mo, which is the useful counter-data-point for the spec: a confident, no-questions authority tour is a second viable path, not proof the article's pain-first order is the only one that works. Also notable: the "aggressive quiz-then-paywall" reputation this app carries in the industry is not visible anywhere in this captured funnel, there is no quiz screen at all in the 18 screens pulled (welcome, onboarding, paywall); whatever quiz pattern PictureThis is known for likely lives in a different cohort/experiment or a post-scan upsell path outside what Appllama's onboarding/paywall sections captured here.

## Verdict for our spec

- Usable: the authority-stat screen (screen 9, direct bar-chart comparison vs. "other" competitors) and the reviews-wall screen (screen 10, laurels + real quotes) are strong, low-friction trust patterns Homa could adapt without needing any user-input quiz machinery: relevant if Homa wants a faster, lower-friction onboarding variant to test against a pain-first one.
- Usable: zero-seam onboarding-to-paywall transition (screen 11 = paywall screen 1, no separate "here comes the offer" beat) is worth testing as a variant: it removes a whole transition screen the article's flow spends on the personalizing-loader moment.
- Not directly usable: the two paywall screens contradict each other on tier count and framing (trial/monthly vs. Plus/Pro annual), and neither matches the full IAP price list: this is a rough edge to avoid copying, not a pattern to emulate; Homa's paywall should keep one coherent, complete pricing table per surface.
- Not usable as "the industry's aggressive quiz pattern": this capture shows no quiz screens at all, so it cannot serve as our reference for quiz-then-paywall mechanics; if that pattern matters for the spec, it needs a different app or a different Appllama flow for PictureThis (e.g., a scan-triggered upsell) rather than this welcome/onboarding/paywall capture.
- Caution: this app defers account creation entirely (no email/signup screen anywhere in the 18 screens), consistent with the article's "account last" rule: but it achieves that by never really asking for an account inside this funnel at all, rather than asking after purchase; confirm whether PictureThis creates an account silently (Apple ID) versus truly skipping it, since Homa's model still needs a family/child identity before or shortly after purchase.
