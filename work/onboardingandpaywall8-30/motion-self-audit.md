# Motion audit: onboarding walk + paywall (quick effort)

Scope: `making-show.tsx`, `walk-scene.tsx`, `walk-character.tsx`, `making.tsx`, `kids.tsx`, `next.tsx`, `intent.tsx`, `hello.tsx`, `billing-gate.tsx`. Audited against the 8 categories in `.agents/skills/improve-animations/AUDIT.md`, adapted for React Native Reanimated instead of CSS. Recon (grep for `withSpring`/`withTiming`/`withRepeat`/`withSequence`/`useSharedValue`/`useAnimatedStyle`/`useReducedMotion`/`Animated.`/`FadeIn`/`Easing`) found real motion in exactly two of the nine files, `making-show.tsx` and `making.tsx`; the other seven (`walk-scene.tsx`, `walk-character.tsx`, `kids.tsx`, `next.tsx`, `intent.tsx`, `hello.tsx`, `billing-gate.tsx`) have zero Reanimated primitives, so every screen swap and selection change in them is an instant hard cut. Every finding below was re-read at its cited file:line before being listed; nothing here is by-design and undocumented as a settled tradeoff, except where noted.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Category | Location | Finding | Fix summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | Purpose & frequency | `features/onboarding/making-show.tsx` (whole file, esp. lines 142-270) | `MakingShow` is fully built (easel breathing loop, spring-dropped fact chips, spark bursts, a 450ms exit turn) but is never imported or rendered anywhere in the app (`grep -rn "MakingShow" apps/mobile/src` returns only its own definition). The commit that introduced it (4beb2202, #998) says in its own message "MakingShow (the gauntlet-winning easel show) stays for the no-questions path via making-show.tsx", but the actual `making.tsx` shipped in that same commit never branches to it; the no-questions path (`checklistAvailable === "no"`) falls through to the plain `InkDots` wait instead. The stated intent and the shipped code disagree, and no family has ever seen this animation. | Either wire it in exactly where the commit says it belongs (render `<MakingShow>` in `making.tsx`'s `checklistAvailable === "no"` / no-questionnaire branch, in place of or alongside the current ink-dots wait) or delete the file if the design was superseded. While reviewing it for revival: line 226-227 marks the whole subtree `accessibilityElementsHidden` / `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, which also hides the `FactChip` text (the parent's own verbatim answers) from screen readers, not just the decorative easel and sparks; narrow that to the decorative graphic only. |
| 2 | MEDIUM | Easing & duration | `features/onboarding/making-show.tsx:184-190` | The sheet's exit uses `Easing.in(Easing.cubic)` over 450ms. AUDIT.md's decision order puts both entering and exiting under `ease-out`, and states "ease-in on UI is always a finding" because it delays the exact moment being watched; here the element visibly hangs before the turn-and-fade accelerates. | Swap to an ease-out curve, e.g. `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, or adopt the repo's strong-ease-out token if one exists on native (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` equivalent) so the turn starts moving immediately instead of stalling. |
| 3 | MEDIUM | Missed transition / Easing & duration | `app/(onboarding)/kids.tsx:556-578` (`Chip`), `app/(onboarding)/intent.tsx:66-85` (option `Pressable`), `components/billing-gate.tsx:642-681` (`PlanPicker`) | Every selection pill in the walk toggles `backgroundColor`/`borderColor` by directly branching the style object on `selected`: an instant color swap with no transition, repeated near-identically in three separate files. These are tapped repeatedly per session (every age/love/working-on chip, every intent option, every plan choice), so the flatness reads as unresponsive on the walk's most-interacted controls, and per AUDIT.md a color/state change belongs on `ease` with a short duration. | Wrap each in a Reanimated `useAnimatedStyle` driven by a `withTiming(selected ? 1 : 0, { duration: 160-180, easing: Easing.inOut(Easing.ease) })` progress value, interpolating `backgroundColor`/`borderColor` between the unselected and selected tones. Do this once as a shared pattern (a small hook or wrapper) rather than a third hand-rolled copy, so the three screens don't drift into three different durations later. |
| 4 | MEDIUM | Physicality & origin | `app/(onboarding)/next.tsx:100-127` (door cards), `app/(onboarding)/kids.tsx:556-578`, `app/(onboarding)/intent.tsx:66-85`, `components/billing-gate.tsx:650-680` | None of these `Pressable`s have any press-down feedback: no `onPressIn`/`onPressOut` scale, no ripple config. Per AUDIT.md, pressable elements should get `transform: scale(0.97)` on press with a ~160ms ease-out release; right now tapping the walk's primary CTAs and every choice card gives zero tactile confirmation before the (sometimes async) `onPress` handler resolves. | Add a shared press-scale treatment: `withTiming` the pressed shared value to `0.97` on `onPressIn` and back to `1` on `onPressOut`, `duration: 160`, `easing: Easing.out(Easing.quad)`. Keep it subtle (0.95-0.98 per the audit's band) and apply it to the same four locations together so it lands as one consistent feel, not four separate ones. |
| 5 | LOW | Interruptibility | `app/(onboarding)/making.tsx:295-317` | Completed wait-notes enter with `FadeInDown.duration(280)` (line 299), but the array is capped at 4 (`current.slice(-3)` plus the new note, line 156), so once a 4th note lands the oldest one is silently dropped from the array and its `Animated.View` unmounts instantly with no exit, an asymmetric fade-in/pop-out pair on the same list. | Give the same `Animated.View` a matching `exiting={FadeOut.duration(200)}` (or `FadeOutUp`) so old notes leave the way the new ones arrived, instead of a fade in but a hard vanish out. |

## Missed opportunities

- **`app/(onboarding)/hello.tsx:71-80`**: the entire greeting block (the personalized "Nice to meet you, {name}." heading plus the intro paragraph) renders nothing until an async name lookup resolves, then pops in as a flat, un-animated block. This is the very first screen after signup, the highest-emotion "hello" beat in the whole walk, and the file's own comment explains real care went into making the text "arrive whole" rather than swap mid-read (line 30-31), but arriving whole still means arriving with a hard cut after an unpredictable network delay. A short opacity fade-in (150-200ms, `ease-out`, gated by `useReducedMotion`) on that block would honor the same "arrives whole" intent without the pop.
- **`features/onboarding/walk-scene.tsx:44,58-59`**: `fieldColor` snaps directly between `palette.clay50` and `palette.gold100` with a plain `backgroundColor` assignment; the component's own comment calls gold "the reveal's ceremony," i.e. the one deliberately celebratory beat in the whole walk, and it currently just cuts. Cross-fading the sky color (interpolating the background over ~300-400ms when `sky` changes) would make the one ceremonial moment actually feel like one.
- **`app/(onboarding)/making.tsx:241-261`**: the "page is ready" banner (gold-100 row plus a "See it" button) is the payoff of the entire multi-minute wait, and it currently just appears in the conditional tree with no entrance at all, indistinguishable from any other re-render. A brief scale-and-fade in (`opacity 0→1`, `scale 0.95→1`, ~220ms `ease-out`) would give this specific state change the delight budget AUDIT.md reserves for rare, high-emotion moments.
- **`components/billing-gate.tsx:563-572` → `573-618`**: when the App Store product finishes loading, "Loading App Store plans" (a `LoadingStatus` row) is swapped out for the full pricing card (plan name, price, weekly-equivalent line, subscribe button) with no transition; the paywall's most important piece of information appears as a layout jump. Since this is the single highest-stakes screen in the funnel, a short cross-fade (with `filter`-style blur unavailable natively, a plain opacity fade suffices) between the loading and loaded states would prevent that state change from reading as unstable content.
