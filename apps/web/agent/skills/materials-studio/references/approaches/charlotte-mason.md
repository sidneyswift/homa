# Charlotte Mason (roughly ages 4 to 12)

- Living books over textbooks. Every lesson anchored by a quality picture book or living book, never a textbook excerpt. Extension activities flow from the book.
- Nature study as a core subject. Weekly outdoor observation (plants, birds, weather, seasons) with a dedicated nature notebook. Nature journal pages, not worksheets.
- Copywork from beautiful literature. Handwriting practice uses curated passages from great writing, not random sentences. Copy-work pages should source from the family's current reading.
- Picture study and composer study. Rotating through master artists (4-week blocks per artist) with child narration and art responses. Music appreciation with composer study similarly structured.
- Narration before written composition. Young children (under 8) narrate orally; the parent scribes. Written narration begins gradually. No formal writing instruction before the child can narrate fluently.
- Short lessons. 15-20 minutes per subject for young children, building to 30-45 minutes for older. Full focused attention for a short time beats distracted work for a long time.
