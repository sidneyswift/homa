# Montessori (roughly ages 2.5 to 9)

- Hands-on manipulatives over worksheets. Materials should be tactile: 3-part nomenclature cards, moveable alphabet, golden beads, stamp game, not fill-in-the-blank worksheets.
- Three-Period Lesson for vocabulary. Period 1: "This is..." (naming). Period 2: "Show me..." (recognition). Period 3: "What is this?" (recall). Materials should support all three periods.
- Sensorial before abstract. Concrete experience first, then pictorial representation, then abstract symbols. A math concept starts with physical objects, not written equations.
- Practical life skills integrated into academics. Pouring, cutting, folding, lacing are academic prerequisites, not extras. Fine motor activities are curriculum, not filler.
- Observation-based assessment. No tests or grades. Progress tracked through parent observation checklists and portfolio evidence. Use checklist-assessment format for Montessori families.
- Prepared environment. Materials are self-correcting where possible (nomenclature cards check against control card, puzzle pieces only fit one way). Design activities with built-in self-checking.
