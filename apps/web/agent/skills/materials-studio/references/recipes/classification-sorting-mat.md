# Classification sorting mat

A T-chart or multi-column sorting activity where the child places
cut-out pieces into labeled category zones. Designed for lamination
and reuse with cut-out manipulatives.

Great for: ages 3-7, sorting by attribute (color, size, shape, type),
science classification, phonics sorting (short a vs short e words).

Always html path. Precise column layout and cut-out grid require exact
positioning.

Design patterns (all binding for classification-sorting-mat):

- **Two-section page**: top section is the sorting mat, bottom section
  is the cut-out pieces grid.
- **Sorting mat**: 2-3 labeled columns separated by bold lines
  (`border: 2px solid var(--homa-ink);`). Large category headers
  (`font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700;`). Zones large enough for
  4-6 pieces each.
- **Cut-out pieces grid**: dashed borders (`border: 2px dashed var(--homa-control-boundary);`)
  around each piece. Pieces sized to fit in the mat zones.
- **Cut line label**: "✂️ cut apart" between the mat and pieces sections.
- **Lamination-friendly**: no color fills that waste ink. Outlines and
  line art only.
- **Piece count**: 8-12 total pieces, distributed across categories
  with 1-2 tricky/ambiguous pieces for discussion.
