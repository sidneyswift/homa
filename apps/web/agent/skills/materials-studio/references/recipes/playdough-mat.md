# Playdough mat

Golden anchor: pass `anchorIds: ["playdough-mat"]` to generate_image so
the output matches the Homa playdough mat bar (two to four huge hollow
letters or shapes with one uniform quarter-inch charcoal outline, a
white rounded play panel on cream paper, a large "Playdough Mat" plus
concept header with a Name line, one child-facing instruction line, a
small finished-playdough model in one corner). Write your own prompt

Large outline shapes or letters designed as a surface for playdough.
Child places playdough on shapes to form them. Designed for
lamination and repeated use.

Great for: ages 2-5, letter formation, number formation, shape
recognition, fine motor development. Tactile and sensory-friendly.

Html path preferred for thick outlines and generous sizing.
image-direct viable for themed versions with illustrations.

Design patterns (all binding for playdough-mat):

- **Thick bold outlines**: `border: 4px solid var(--homa-ink);` on all shapes.
  Lines must be wide enough for playdough to sit on.
- **Generous sizing**: minimum 3 inches per shape. 2-4 shapes per
  page maximum.
- **No fine detail**: eliminate any lines thinner than 3px. Playdough
  cannot follow fine detail.
- **White background**: essential for lamination. No color fills.
- **Label at top**: "Playdough Mat" with the target concept
  ("Letter A", "Number 5", "Shapes") in large text.
- **Optional themed border**: simple decorative border or small
  illustration in one corner (does not interfere with play area).
- **Optional model**: small reference image of the target in a
  corner, showing what the completed playdough form should look like.
